⛵ Sailor Piece
Complete Beginner Guide (2026)

📅 Updated April 2026 ⏱️ 18 min read 🏷️ Beginner · Anime RPG · Farming ✅ Verified by BLOXBURST

Starting out in Sailor Piece can feel incredibly overwhelming. The game throws multiple progression systems at you simultaneously: stat rolling, Haki training, artifact farming, server hopping, weapon upgrading, and title management. Most beginners quit within the first few hours because they grind inefficiently or skip foundational setups. This guide removes the guesswork — follow these sections in order for the smoothest experience.

Essential Settings & Early Setup

Before you even think about grinding levels or hunting bosses, you need to configure your client properly. This is the number one reason beginners fall behind. The game's default settings are optimized for casual exploration, not efficient progression. Taking five minutes to adjust these will save you dozens of hours later.

Enable Auto Quest Immediately

Found in the settings menu, Auto Quest automatically turns in completed objectives and fetches the next one without requiring you to run back to the NPC. Without this, you will waste significant time traversing islands just to hand in tasks. Enable it before your first grind session.

💡

Enable Auto Quest first — it's the single biggest time-saver in early game. It compounds over time, especially during repetitive quest chains.

Claim Group Rewards First

When you spawn for the first time, navigate to the group rewards menu. These provide starting gold, gems, and occasionally early-game consumables. Use these resources to purchase Cortana or basic survival items. Skipping this step leaves you underfunded for your first few island clears.

Set Your Spawn Point on Every New Island

Each island contains a spawn crystal. Touch it immediately upon arrival. If you die without setting it, you will respawn three islands away and lose 5–10 minutes walking back. Make it a habit: land → touch crystal → start farming.

The Hockey Quest: Your First Major Power Spike

This quest requires 150,000 Gold and 200 Gems, but it grants a permanent +25% base damage multiplier that scales with your level. It is the single most impactful early-game purchase. Save up deliberately and complete it before pushing into higher-tier zones.

  1. Defeat 150 NPCs using standard combat style
  2. Activate your Z ability 65 times — use the Auto Skill toggle in settings to spam safely while moving
  3. Land 750 basic punches — an auto-clicker is recommended here for consistency

Stat Rolling Strategy & Merchant System

Early-game gems are extremely scarce. Spending them incorrectly will lock you out of optimal race and clan combinations for weeks. Understanding the roll economy is crucial.

The 500-Gem Roll Trick

Most players roll individual stats for 1,000 gems each. Instead, go to Sailor Island and use the Roll All Stats option for exactly 500 gems. This distributes points across all categories simultaneously, giving you immediate survivability and damage without draining your wallet.

Never Skip the Merchant

The traveling merchant sells race, clan, and trade rerolls. His stock is limited but refreshes on a hidden server timer. Even if he only has one reroll available, purchase it. Early race/clan optimization dramatically increases your drop rates, movement speed, and passive damage. Check back every 30–45 minutes.

🛒

Prioritize races with luck or damage passives, and clans that complement your intended playstyle (AFK vs active farming).

Enable Auto Haki Early

Once you unlock Armament and Observation Haki (typically on Desert Island), open settings and toggle Auto Haki. This ensures your defensive and detection buffs activate automatically on respawn, saving manual setup time and preventing surprise deaths in high-traffic zones.

Figure 2: Use the 500-gem roll trick and check the merchant frequently for race/clan rerolls.

Ascension Farming Routes & Server Hopping

Ascension materials are the primary bottleneck for mid-game progression. Boss drops are RNG-based, but you can heavily influence your efficiency through route optimization and server management.

Optimal Farming Loop

Follow this exact island rotation to minimize travel time and maximize boss encounters:

  1. Sailor Island — Set as primary spawn. Clear initial mobs for basic mats
  2. Shibuya Station — High-density NPC zones with mid-tier boss spawns. Focus on area-clear weapons
  3. Hollow Island — Rare material drop zone. Bosses have longer respawn timers but higher yield

The Server Hop Method

Joining a friend's server is the fastest way to farm. Boss spawns are instanced per server, so a private or friend-joined server means zero competition. If you play solo, public server hopping still works but requires faster rotation. Use the friend method whenever possible.

Titles & Weapon Upgrades

Titles are not cosmetic — they provide flat multipliers to luck, damage, defense, and movement speed. Equip them immediately upon unlock. At Shibuya Station, upgrade your primary weapon and accessories. Prioritize Damage → Crit Chance → Damage Reduction. Avoid upgrading non-essential gear until level 50.

Figure 3: Follow the 3-island loop and prioritize weapon stats at Shibuya Station.

Mid-Game AFK Farming & Skill Tree Priority

Once you reach the mid-game threshold, active grinding becomes inefficient. Switch to passive progression using the Quake Fruit AFK method.

Shinjuku Island AFK Setup

Requirement: 160% total damage to one-shot 10 mobs simultaneously. Position your character near mob clusters, enable auto-attack, and let the game farm for you. You will accumulate chests, gems, and artifacts passively. This is the most time-efficient progression method in the game.

Skill Tree Distribution (Level 7500+)

When allocating skill points, follow this exact priority to maximize scaling:

  1. Damage (primary scaling stat)
  2. Luck (drop rate & crit synergy)
  3. Crit Chance (multiplicative with damage)
  4. Crit Damage (late-game burst)
  5. HP (survivability threshold)
⚠️

Don't invest in HP or defense early. They provide diminishing returns compared to offensive scaling at this stage.

Boss Pity System Explained

The game uses a hidden pity counter for boss drops. Use Night Boss to auto-spawn until you reach 23/25 pity. Turn off auto-spawn, manually defeat Night Boss (24/25), then spawn your target boss. This guarantees a drop without wasting hours on RNG cycles.

Figure 4: Optimal AFK positioning at Shinjuku Island and skill tree priority order.

Artifact Management & Milestone Scaling

Artifacts dictate your late-game viability. Managing them incorrectly will stall your progression for weeks.

Beginner Artifact Strategy

Focus exclusively on damage-oriented artifacts early on. Upgrade them to +5, but do not max out non-legendary pieces. Save resources for higher-tier drops. The stat difference between a maxed rare and a +5 legendary is massive.

Milestone EXP Scaling

Every set of artifacts dropped grants bonus EXP through the milestone tracker. This creates a compounding effect: more drops → faster leveling → higher chance to roll superior artifacts. Do not skip artifact farming even if drops seem random. The milestone system rewards consistency over time.

📈

The milestone system is passive and cumulative — even bad drops contribute. Never stop farming artifacts once you've started.

Figure 5: Focus on damage stats and track milestone EXP for accelerated leveling.

Haki & Rune Leveling Optimization

Haki and runes provide passive multipliers that scale indefinitely. Optimizing their acquisition is essential for endgame readiness.

Observation Haki Fast-Track

Reset your stats to zero temporarily. Use combat style to drag a high-HP NPC into a corner. Enable Auto Haki and let the NPC attack you repeatedly. This triggers observation procs continuously, granting massive EXP in a controlled environment. It is the fastest legitimate method available.

Rune Farming Strategy

Switch to Extreme Mode to increase rune drop rates. Pair this with the King of Heroes spec, which grants additional rune gain multipliers. Runes scale with difficulty, so higher modes yield better base stats and upgrade materials.

Figure 6: Corner training method for Observation Haki and Extreme Mode for rune farming.

7

Drop Chance Stacking & Luck Mechanics

Most players do not realize you can drop 2–3 materials from a single mob. This is governed by multiplicative luck stacking, not additive. Understanding the math will double your farming efficiency.

Optimal Race, Clan & Spec Combinations

  • Race: Luckburn or Kitsune — highest base luck multipliers
  • Clan: Eminence or Monarch — drop chance & gold scaling
  • Spec Passive: Fortune Chosen — flat drop rate increase

These three stack multiplicatively. Equip them before farming rare ascension materials or boss drops. Track your drops per hour to verify efficiency improvements.

🍀

Always equip your luck build before entering a boss fight or farming session — switching mid-session wastes precious pity counter progress.

Figure 7: Stack race, clan, and spec passives for maximum drop chance efficiency.

💡 Honorable Mention: Build Saver System

As you progress, you will need different loadouts for AFK farming, pure damage pushes, and luck-based material runs. Manually swapping stats, artifacts, and gear is time-consuming and error-prone. Use the Build Saver system to store optimized configurations. Name them clearly (e.g., "AFK-Shinjuku", "Boss-Push", "Luck-Farm") and swap instantly. This small quality-of-life feature saves hours of micromanagement and prevents stat misallocation during critical farming windows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I reset my stats if I mess up early rolling?
Yes, but it costs gems. Use resets sparingly and only after reaching level 30 to minimize waste.
Is AFK farming bannable?
No, as long as you are actively present in-game and not using third-party automation software. The built-in auto-attack and skill toggles are fully supported.
How often do merchant stocks refresh?
Timers vary by server population, but typically every 30–45 minutes. Check frequently during peak hours.
Should I prioritize weapon upgrades or artifact farming first?
Weapons first until level 50. Artifacts scale better in mid-to-late game when base damage is already optimized.
