⛵ Sailor Piece
Complete Beginner Guide (2026)

📅 Updated April 2026 ⏱️ 18 min read 🏷️ Beginner · Anime RPG · Farming ✅ Verified by BLOXBURST

Starting out in Sailor Piece can feel incredibly overwhelming. The game throws multiple progression systems at you simultaneously: stat rolling, Haki training, artifact farming, server hopping, weapon upgrading, and title management. Most beginners quit within the first few hours because they grind inefficiently or skip foundational setups. This guide removes the guesswork — follow these sections in order for the smoothest experience.

Essential Settings & Early Setup

Before you even think about grinding levels or hunting bosses, you need to configure your client properly. This is the number one reason beginners fall behind. The game's default settings are optimized for casual exploration, not efficient progression.

Enable Auto Quest Immediately

Found in the settings menu, Auto Quest automatically turns in completed objectives without requiring you to run back to the NPC. Enable it before your first grind session.

💡

Enable Auto Quest first — it's the single biggest time-saver in early game.

Claim Group Rewards First

Navigate to the group rewards menu on first spawn. These provide starting gold, gems, and consumables. Use them to purchase Cortana or basic survival items.

Set Your Spawn Point on Every New Island

Touch each island's spawn crystal immediately upon arrival. Make it a habit: land → touch crystal → start farming.

The Hockey Quest: Your First Major Power Spike

Requires 150,000 Gold and 200 Gems but grants a permanent +25% base damage multiplier.

  1. Defeat 150 NPCs using standard combat style
  2. Activate your Z ability 65 times
  3. Land 750 basic punches

Figure 1: Enable Auto Quest in settings before starting your grind session.

Stat Rolling Strategy & Merchant System

Early-game gems are extremely scarce. Spending them incorrectly will lock you out of optimal race and clan combinations for weeks.

The 500-Gem Roll Trick

Use the Roll All Stats option at Sailor Island for exactly 500 gems instead of rolling individual stats for 1,000 each.

Never Skip the Merchant

The traveling merchant sells race, clan, and trade rerolls. Check back every 30–45 minutes.

🛒

Prioritize races with luck or damage passives and clans that complement your playstyle.

Enable Auto Haki Early

Toggle Auto Haki once you unlock Armament and Observation Haki on Desert Island.

Figure 2: Use the 500-gem roll trick and check the merchant frequently.

3

Ascension Farming Routes & Server Hopping

Ascension materials are the primary bottleneck for mid-game progression.

Optimal Farming Loop

  1. Sailor Island — primary spawn, clear mobs for basic mats
  2. Shibuya Station — high-density NPC zones with mid-tier boss spawns
  3. Hollow Island — rare material drop zone, higher boss yield

The Server Hop Method

Joining a friend's server means zero competition for boss spawns. Use the friend method whenever possible.

Titles & Weapon Upgrades

Prioritize Damage → Crit Chance → Damage Reduction. Avoid upgrading non-essential gear until level 50.

Figure 3: Follow the 3-island loop and prioritize weapon stats at Shibuya Station.

Mid-Game AFK Farming & Skill Tree Priority

Once you reach mid-game, switch to passive progression using the Quake Fruit AFK method.

Shinjuku Island AFK Setup

Requirement: 160% total damage to one-shot 10 mobs simultaneously. Position near mob clusters and enable auto-attack.

Skill Tree Distribution (Level 7500+)

  1. Damage
  2. Luck
  3. Crit Chance
  4. Crit Damage
  5. HP
⚠️

Don't invest in HP or defense early. Diminishing returns compared to offensive scaling.

Boss Pity System

Use Night Boss until 23/25 pity, then spawn target boss to guarantee a drop.

Figure 4: Optimal AFK positioning at Shinjuku Island and skill tree priority order.

Artifact Management & Milestone Scaling

Artifacts dictate your late-game viability.

Beginner Artifact Strategy

Focus on damage artifacts. Upgrade to +5 but don't max non-legendary pieces.

📈

The milestone system is passive and cumulative — even bad drops contribute.

Figure 5: Focus on damage stats and track milestone EXP for accelerated leveling.

Haki & Rune Leveling Optimization

Observation Haki Fast-Track

Reset stats to zero, drag a high-HP NPC to a corner, enable Auto Haki and let it train continuously.

Rune Farming Strategy

Switch to Extreme Mode and use the King of Heroes spec for bonus rune multipliers.

Figure 6: Corner training method for Observation Haki and Extreme Mode for rune farming.

7

Drop Chance Stacking & Luck Mechanics

You can drop 2–3 materials from a single mob through multiplicative luck stacking.

Optimal Race, Clan & Spec Combinations

  • Race: Luckburn or Kitsune — highest base luck multipliers
  • Clan: Eminence or Monarch — drop chance & gold scaling
  • Spec Passive: Fortune Chosen — flat drop rate increase
🍀

Always equip your luck build before entering a boss fight.

Figure 7: Stack race, clan, and spec passives for maximum drop chance efficiency.

💡 Honorable Mention: Build Saver System

Use the Build Saver system to store loadouts. Name them clearly (e.g., "AFK-Shinjuku", "Boss-Push", "Luck-Farm") and swap instantly to avoid misallocation during critical farming windows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I reset my stats if I mess up early rolling?
Yes, but it costs gems. Use resets sparingly and only after reaching level 30.
Is AFK farming bannable?
No, as long as you're present in-game and not using third-party automation software.
How often do merchant stocks refresh?
Typically every 30–45 minutes depending on server population.
Should I prioritize weapon upgrades or artifact farming first?
Weapons first until level 50. Artifacts scale better in mid-to-late game.
