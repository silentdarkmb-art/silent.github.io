Boneca Ambalabu

Boneca Ambalabu is a Rare Brainrot in the Roblox game Steal A Brainrot and the fourth Rare unit in the in-game Brainrot Index — positioned above Bandito Bobritto and below Cacto Hipopotamo. It costs $5,000 and generates $40 per second — the highest income of any standard Rare Brainrot, maintaining a 0.80% income-to-cost efficiency ratio. Originating from the Indonesian Brainrot meme wave, it was created by TikTok user @ofuscabreno on February 2nd, 2025, and depicts an anomalous frog-tire hybrid described in lore as an evil entity no scientist has been able to contain. The in-game Roblox voxel model renders the character as a bright green American bullfrog head mounted on a black upright car tire, supported by two short orange human legs. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki documents that Boneca Ambalabu shares its body model with La Grande Combinasion and is a mandatory acquisition for the 2nd Rebirth, required alongside Brr Brr Patapim and $3,000,000 in cash.

What is Boneca Ambalabu?

Boneca Ambalabu is a Rare-tier Brainrot in the Roblox game Steal A Brainrot — the 4th Rare unit in the in-game Brainrot Index, positioned above Bandito Bobritto and below Cacto Hipopotamo. It costs $5,000 and generates $40 per second, making it the highest-earning standard Rare Brainrot in the game as confirmed by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and steal-a-brainrot.wiki. The BloxInformer wiki documents its in-game lore: Boneca Ambalabu is classified as an evil entity causing widespread societal unrest, compelling the scientific community to pursue countermeasures — including researcher Prof. Rusdi, who disclosed findings on the anomaly in a video shortly before being reported missing. The Fandom Wiki confirms the creature has no known method of escape once encountered.

Critically, the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki identifies Boneca Ambalabu as a mandatory unit for the 2nd Rebirth — required alongside the Epic Brainrot Brr Brr Patapim and a minimum of $3,000,000 in in-game cash. Completing the 2nd Rebirth unlocks the second floor of the player's base, grants an additional Brainrot slot, extends the base lock timer by 10 seconds, and adds a 1x income multiplier. Boneca Ambalabu is the only Rare unit required in this Rebirth, giving it a dual role as both a top-tier income Rare and a core progression gate.

How to Obtain

Boneca Ambalabu is available exclusively from the Red Carpet — the central conveyor belt in Steal a Brainrot where Brainrots of all rarities pass through and may be purchased by any player in the session. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and u4gm.com guide both confirm that no alternative acquisition path exists: it does not appear through Fishing Events, Fuse Machine outputs, Lucky Block pools, or seasonal event spawns. Based on the large-scale community survey documented in the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki — tracking 1,515 Red Carpet Brainrot appearances — Boneca Ambalabu appeared 43 times, yielding an approximate spawn rate of 2.83%.

This rate is lower than the three Rares preceding it in the Index — Trippi Troppi (4.48%), Gangster Footera (3.96%), and Bandito Bobritto (4.29%) — making Boneca Ambalabu the rarest of the first four standard Rares by observed spawn frequency. Players purchase it for $5,000 upon appearance, or acquire a copy by stealing from another player's unprotected base — the two standard methods confirmed by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and steal-a-brainrot.wiki.

Meme Origin

Boneca Ambalabu originates from the Indonesian Brainrot branch of the AI-generated meme trend that spread across TikTok beginning in early 2025. Know Your Meme and the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki both document that the character was created by TikTok user @ofuscabreno — also the creator of Brr Brr Patapim — and first posted on February 2nd, 2025, amassing more than 7.7 million views within two months. The video presents an AI-generated image of the creature set in a daytime urban environment, accompanied by an Indonesian-language narration describing the entity's sinister nature and the impossibility of escape upon contact.

The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki provides the name etymology in detail: Boneka is the Indonesian (and Portuguese) word for "doll," while Ambalabu is a constructed term blending "Ambatukam" — a phrase associated with Dreamybull internet memes from 2022 — and "Labubu," the bunny-eared Pop Mart toy originally created by Dutch-Hong Kong author Kasing Lung. The Miraheze Wiki also notes that the audio used in the original TikTok video was not produced for the character — it was taken from an older video dating to October 2024, part of a separate series involving a doll with the face of Dreamybull.

Community Status

Boneca Ambalabu occupies a prominent position in the Steal A Brainrot community as both a progression milestone and a top-tier Rare income unit. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki, u4gm.com, BloxInformer, and Fandom Wiki all note it as the standard endpoint of the Rare acquisition journey — the unit players target before transitioning to Epic-tier farming. Its dual function as the Rebirth 2 gate and the highest-income standard Rare gives it persistent demand at every stage of the game, from players who need it for progression to veterans who value it for its Bloodrot mutation potential. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki specifically highlights its Bloodrot mutation at $80/sec as one of the strongest income upgrades achievable at Rare rarity — capable of matching or exceeding base-income Epic Brainrots without requiring Epic-tier expenditure.

Boneca Ambalabu's in-game Roblox voxel model is adapted from the Indonesian Brainrot character of the same name — an anomalous hybrid creature documented across the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki, steal-a-brainrot.wiki, u4gm.com, and the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki. The Fandom Wiki identifies its body model as appearing on the in-game unit La Grande Combinasion. The full visual composition based on verified sources:

Head

The most distinctive feature of Boneca Ambalabu is its bright green frog head, documented by the steal-a-brainrot.wiki as specifically resembling an American bullfrog — a wide, flat-crowned head with large, round, blank-looking eyes and a notably wide mouth with an orange interior. The u4gm.com guide describes the eyes as giving the character a vacant, unfocused expression, reinforcing the meme's tone of an unresponsive anomaly that exists beyond rational logic. The head is outsized relative to the creature's body, sitting atop the tire torso with a proportional imbalance that contributes to the character's surreal silhouette.

Body (Tire)

Rather than a conventional torso, Boneca Ambalabu's mid-section is formed by a black car tire standing upright — the creature's body is the tire itself, with the head mounted on top and the legs extending downward from its base. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki describes this as a "tire body" rendered in the game's voxel block format, maintaining the cylindrical profile of an automotive tire. The u4gm.com guide notes this makes the body one of the most immediately recognizable shapes in the entire Rare tier, since no other Brainrot shares this particular silhouette. The original meme set the creature against a stark black background, emphasizing the contrast of the black tire against bright surroundings — though the in-game model appears in the standard Roblox base environment.

Legs

Supporting the tire body are two short, orange, human-like legs — documented by steal-a-brainrot.wiki and the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki as the anatomical element that gives the character its ambulatory motion in-game. The legs are distinctly human in shape, attaching to the underside of the tire and extending downward in a standard bipedal stance. The orange coloration of the legs contrasts with the dark rubber of the tire and the bright green of the frog head, creating the creature's three-color visual identity. The Miraheze Wiki notes the human legs are a core part of what makes the original meme character unsettling — the combination of frog, machine, and human anatomy in a single form.

La Grande Combinasion Connection

The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki records that Boneca Ambalabu's head and body asset appears on La Grande Combinasion — another character in the game that shares part of the same visual model structure. The u4gm.com guide describes this as the "La Grande Combinasion Model" used as the base for Boneca Ambalabu's eccentric proportions. This shared model connection links the two characters visually within the game's asset library, though they are distinct Brainrots with separate Index entries, stats, and lore.

Boneca Ambalabu is the fourth Rare Brainrot in the Index and the highest-earning standard Rare in the game at $40/sec. At a cost of $5,000, its income-to-cost ratio sits at approximately 0.80% — the strongest efficiency figure among the four standard Red Carpet Rares. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki identifies it as the definitive pre-Epic income unit, describing it as the top acquisition target for any player who cannot yet afford Epic-tier Brainrots. The u4gm.com guide further notes that its dual role as the highest Rare earner and a Rebirth 2 gate unit gives it persistent value at every stage of progression — from new players acquiring it for the first time to veterans farming Bloodmoon Event mutations.

Trading Value

Boneca Ambalabu commands strong, consistent trade demand among Rare-tier Brainrots — driven by two overlapping factors: its standing as the highest-income standard Rare and its mandatory role in the 2nd Rebirth. Players who have not yet completed the 2nd Rebirth actively pursue it through trades, giving even base-mutation copies reliable demand from progression-focused buyers. The u4gm.com guide notes this dual-purpose status means trade interest does not taper off as players advance, since Rebirth-locked players at every stage of mid-game progression require it. Mutated copies command the strongest trading interest — particularly Rainbow ($400/sec), Lava ($240/sec), and Bloodrot ($80/sec) — with the steal-a-brainrot.wiki specifically highlighting the Bloodrot variant as one of the most income-efficient Rare-tier units relative to rarity, capable of matching or surpassing several base-income Epic Brainrots.

Created by @ofuscabreno on February 2nd, 2025: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki, Know Your Meme, and the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki all document that Boneca Ambalabu was created by TikTok user @ofuscabreno and first posted on February 2nd, 2025. The same creator also produced Brr Brr Patapim — another character directly tied to Boneca Ambalabu through the 2nd Rebirth requirement. Name Etymology — Boneka + Ambatukam + Labubu: The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki provides a detailed breakdown of the name: Boneka (also spelled Boneca in Portuguese) means "doll" in Indonesian. Ambalabu is a compound word constructed from Ambatukam — a phrase associated with Dreamybull internet memes that circulated around 2022 — and Labubu, a Pop Mart toy originally created by Dutch-Hong Kong author Kasing Lung as part of a series called The Monsters. The name therefore blends two unrelated internet meme references into a single portmanteau, consistent with the Indonesian Brainrot tradition of layered cultural references.

Audio Was Repurposed — Not Original to the Character: The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki documents that the audio track used in the original Boneca Ambalabu TikTok video was not created for the character — it originated from an older video dating to October 2024, which was part of a separate series featuring a doll with the face of internet personality Dreamybull. The sound was subsequently repurposed and attached to the frog-tire creature by @ofuscabreno. This means the narration describing the anomaly's threat to society was originally produced in an entirely different context, later adopted to become Boneca Ambalabu's defining audio identity.

Mandatory for Rebirth 2 — Required with Brr Brr Patapim and $3M: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki confirms that Boneca Ambalabu is one of two Brainrots required to complete the 2nd Rebirth in Steal a Brainrot, alongside the Epic-tier Brr Brr Patapim and a minimum cash balance of $3,000,000. Completing this Rebirth unlocks the second floor of the player's base, adds one extra Brainrot slot, increases the base lock timer by 10 seconds, and applies a permanent 1x income multiplier. Deltia's Gaming and the Fandom Wiki both note the 2nd Rebirth is mandatory for accessing second-floor base storage — a significant progression milestone.

Spawn Rate — 2.83% (43 / 1,515): Based on the large-scale community tracking survey documented in the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki — recording 1,515 Red Carpet Brainrot appearances — Boneca Ambalabu appeared 43 times, producing an approximate observed spawn rate of 2.83%. This is the lowest spawn rate of the first four standard Rares in the Index — below Trippi Troppi (4.48%), Gangster Footera (3.96%), and Bandito Bobritto (4.29%) — making it the most difficult standard Rare to encounter on a given carpet cycle despite being one of the most sought-after units in its tier.

Shares Body Model with La Grande Combinasion: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki explicitly records that Boneca Ambalabu's head and body asset appears on La Grande Combinasion — a separate in-game Brainrot that uses the same underlying model structure. The u4gm.com guide refers to this as the "La Grande Combinasion Model" — a body framework characterized by the eccentric tire-and-frog proportions that define Boneca Ambalabu's visual identity. This shared model origin links the two characters within the game's asset system while they remain entirely distinct units in the Index.

Halloween Variant — Mummy Ambalabu: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki documents that Boneca Ambalabu received a Halloween variant called Mummy Ambalabu — released during the Spooky Update on Saturday, October 25th. Mummy Ambalabu's design replaces the standard frog head with a pumpkin featuring yellow glowing eyes, and wraps the tire body in bandage material to create a mummy aesthetic. It is obtainable through the Spooky Lucky Block with a 32.75% chance and is classified as a Brainrot God — a significantly higher rarity than base Boneca Ambalabu. The Fandom Wiki records Mummy Ambalabu as the first Rare Brainrot to receive a variant in the game's history.

Strategy Tips

💡 Tips for Using Boneca Ambalabu

1

Acquire it as your primary Rare target after Gangster Footera: Boneca Ambalabu is the highest-earning standard Rare at $40/sec, and it is a mandatory unit for the 2nd Rebirth — making it the most functionally important Rare unit in the entire tier. Once you have secured Trippi Troppi and Gangster Footera for the 1st Rebirth, shift all carpet-watching effort toward landing a Boneca Ambalabu. Its 2.83% spawn rate is the lowest of the four standard Rares, so do not delay pursuing it — purchase it immediately whenever it appears on the carpet regardless of your current cash position, as missing a spawn extends your 2nd Rebirth timeline considerably.

2

Prioritize it during every Bloodmoon Event: The steal-a-brainrot.wiki identifies Boneca Ambalabu as the single most important Bloodmoon Event mutation target at the Rare tier. A Bloodrot-mutated copy generates $80/sec — an income figure that matches or exceeds several base Epic Brainrots at a fraction of their acquisition cost. Because the Bloodrot mutation is obtained through the event rather than purchased directly, a Bloodrot Boneca Ambalabu represents one of the most income-efficient upgrades achievable without spending toward Epic-tier units. During every Bloodmoon Event, expose your Boneca Ambalabu first before any other Rare unit in your base.

3

Hold at least one copy permanently after completing the 2nd Rebirth: Because Boneca Ambalabu is required for every subsequent 2nd Rebirth cycle — each time you reset, you will need it again — always retain at least one copy in your base after using it to meet the Rebirth 2 condition. Sportskeeda and Deltia's Gaming both advise stashing the required Brainrots on an alternate account before rebirthing so they can be retrieved afterward. Never sell your only copy of Boneca Ambalabu between Rebirths, as re-acquiring it at a 2.83% spawn rate on a cold carpet cycle can cause significant progression delays.

4

Use spare copies as high-value trade assets: Boneca Ambalabu's combined status as the highest-income standard Rare and a hard Rebirth gate unit gives extra copies strong trading leverage. Players who are Rebirth 2-locked will actively trade for it — often offering multiple Rare-tier copies or lower-rarity bundles to secure one. The u4gm.com guide confirms that base-mutation copies trade consistently above their raw stat value because of Rebirth demand, while mutated copies — particularly Bloodrot ($80/sec), Lava ($240/sec), and Rainbow ($400/sec) — command interest from a wide range of mid-to-late-game players who recognize the income value.
