What is Holy Arepa?

Holy Arepa is a Common-rarity Brainrot in Steal a Brainrot — the 12th and newest addition to the Common tier, added as part of the Divine Fuse Machine update on March 7, 2026. Confirmed by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki, the character takes the form of an arepa — a golden cornmeal flatbread native to Venezuela and Colombia — adorned with a pair of large golden wings, a floating gold halo, and purple puppy eyes that give it an angelic, endearing appearance. It costs $1,700 and generates $14 per second, making it joint-highest in both cost and income within the entire Common tier alongside Pipi Corni.

The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki documents two notable Index distinctions: Holy Arepa is the 5th Common Brainrot that cannot be obtained from the Red Carpet, and the 4th Common Brainrot to originate from a Fuse Machine — specifically the Divine Fuse, which released on March 7, 2026 as a replacement for the original Fuse Machine. The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki records that the Divine Fuse Machine was announced by creator SpyderSammy through a community poll on February 28, 2026, during Admin Abuse — and players voted in favor of the Divine Fuse over the return of the OG Craft Machine.

🎯

How to Obtain

Holy Arepa is obtainable through the Divine Fuse Machine — a limited-time fusion device released on March 7, 2026, placed at the center of the game map between the Escape Tsunami LTM NPC and the Robux Shop. The Sportskeeda guide and the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki both confirm the machine operates identically to the standard Fuse Machine: players submit four Brainrots from their base inventory, pay the required cash, and wait for the fusion timer to complete before claiming the result. The output is drawn from a pool of 15 Divine-tier Brainrots spanning Common through Secret rarity, with Holy Arepa representing the lowest-tier possible output in that pool.

The Fandom Wiki confirms that the exact drop probability for Holy Arepa has not been officially stated for the Divine Fuse, unlike the 67% probability documented for Noobini Santanini from Santa's Fuse when using four Common-tier inputs. The allthings.how guide notes that Holy Arepa is one of the few "clearest confirmed endpoints" in the Divine Fuse pool — meaning it is acknowledged as accessible but its precise individual percentage remains undisclosed. Beyond the machine, the secondary acquisition method confirmed by the Fandom Wiki is stealing a copy from another player's unlocked base. Holy Arepa does not appear on the Red Carpet under any circumstances.

Meme / Lore Origin

The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki documents Holy Arepa's in-game lore in detail: the character was once a human waiter who had an extreme fondness for arepas — eating so many that he eventually transformed into an arepa himself. Rather than accept death, he prayed to God for survival, and God responded by granting him a face and a pair of wings so that he could continue to live. The lore describes him as now flying around restaurants and inspecting whether the food being served meets the correct spice levels for guests. The Miraheze Wiki further notes that the seasonings and spices he uses in this role are both rich and intensely flavorful.

The arepa itself is a pre-Columbian food staple with deep cultural roots in Venezuela and Colombia — a round, thick cornmeal cake that has been prepared largely unchanged for over 2,800 years. Its prominence in Latin American cuisine makes it a recognizable choice for a Brainrot design within the broader Italian Brainrot and South American internet meme tradition. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki notes that the character's name follows the standard Brainrot naming convention of combining a descriptive English word ("Holy") with the name of its food basis ("Arepa"), consistent with other food-based Brainrots in the Index.

Community Status

Holy Arepa was released as the most recently added Common Brainrot in the game at the time of the March 7, 2026 Divine Fuse update — a distinction the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki records directly. Community reception documented in the Fandom Wiki's discussion forum was mixed regarding the Divine Fuse machine overall: one widely-cited player comment expressed that Holy Arepa's design was among the more visually appealing of the Divine Fuse batch, but noted frustration that a unit players considered worthy of a higher rarity was assigned to Common tier. The allthings.how guide positions Holy Arepa as the baseline entry point of the Divine Fuse pool — the lowest floor of a machine whose ceiling reaches Secret-tier Brainrots like Griffin ($400M/sec).

Holy Arepa's in-game Roblox voxel model depicts a stylized version of a traditional arepa — a round, flat cornmeal food — rendered in the game's block-art style with divine-themed accessories. The full visual description is confirmed by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki, the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki, and the uploaded in-game screenshot provided for reference. The design combines a food body with angelic iconography, consistent with the character's lore of divine transformation.

Body — The Arepa

The central form of Holy Arepa is a round, disc-shaped golden-yellow body — rendered in the game's voxel block format as a thick, circular flatbread shape with a slightly domed top surface. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki describes the overall color as golden/yellow throughout the entire body, consistent with the toasted corn appearance of a freshly cooked arepa. The body is notably wide relative to its depth, preserving the flat, patty-like profile of a real arepa in blocky voxel form. The front face of the body bears the character's facial features, centered on the forward-facing side of the disc.

Face — Purple Puppy Eyes

The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki specifically documents Holy Arepa's eyes as purple "puppy eyes" — large, expressive, rounded eye shapes rendered in a bright violet/purple color that contrasts sharply with the golden body. These eyes are described as giving the character a soft, endearing expression aligned with its angelic design theme. Visible in the uploaded in-game screenshot, the eyes are accompanied by a small, upward-curved smile below them — a subtle expression that reinforces the character's benevolent, heavenly persona without additional facial features.

Wings

Extending from both sides of the arepa body are two large golden wings — the most visually dominant feature of the model outside the body itself. Both the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and the Miraheze Wiki confirm the wings are golden/yellow in color, matching the body tone and reinforcing the divine aesthetic. As seen in the uploaded screenshot, each wing is constructed from multiple stacked voxel segments arranged in a layered, feather-like pattern that fans outward from the body's edge. The wings give the character its characteristic angelic silhouette and are the primary design element that visually distinguishes it from a standard food-themed Brainrot.

Halo

Floating above the top of the arepa body is a gold halo — a flat, circular ring suspended slightly above the head surface that is standard iconography for angelic or holy characters. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki confirms the halo is present as part of the design, and the uploaded screenshot shows it positioned centrally above the dome of the body as a glowing yellow ring. The halo, combined with the wings, completes the "holy" visual identity of the character and connects directly to its lore — the divine intervention that granted it continued existence after transforming into an arepa.

Holy Arepa is positioned at the top of the Common-tier income bracket, tied with Pipi Corni at $1,700 cost and $14 per second income — the joint-highest stats achievable within the Common rarity. Its income-to-cost efficiency of 0.82% is slightly below Noobini Santanini's 1.00% but consistent with the upper range of Common-tier efficiency ratios. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki notes directly that Holy Arepa has the same income and price as Pipi Corni, and generates $1/sec more than Tartaragno at a cost of $200 more — a marginal but documented step up within the tier. As the most recently added Common unit, it sits at the ceiling of what the Common tier offers before income jumps significantly with the first Rare, Trippi Troppi, at $15/sec.

Trading Value

Holy Arepa holds low baseline trading value as a Common-tier unit with no Rebirth gate requirement and no event-exclusivity window that has yet closed — meaning it remains actively craftable in the Divine Fuse Machine as of March 2026. Because players can generate copies through the machine at will while it remains live, default-mutation copies carry little leverage in trades and are primarily useful as income fillers or Index completion entries for players missing it. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki's discussion thread notes some community members felt the unit deserved a higher rarity than Common given its design quality, which may contribute to modest collector interest above its raw stat value. Mutated copies — particularly Divine ($140/sec), Rainbow ($140/sec), and Radioactive ($119/sec) — hold the strongest trading appeal, as the mutation itself substantially elevates a $14/sec unit into a range comparable with base-level Rares.

Newest Common Brainrot — Added March 7, 2026: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki explicitly records that Holy Arepa is the most recently added Common Brainrot in the game's history as of March 2026, introduced with the Divine Fuse Machine update on March 7, 2026. Prior to its addition, Pipi Corni held the last position in the Common Index. With Holy Arepa's release, the Common tier expanded to 12 units — the 12th entry in order of addition to the game since the launch of Steal a Brainrot.

5th Common Not on the Red Carpet — 4th from a Fuse Machine: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki documents two specific positional facts about Holy Arepa's acquisition type. It is the 5th Common Brainrot that cannot be obtained from the Red Carpet — following Noobini Santanini, Raccooni Jandelini, Tartaragno, and Pipi Corni — and it is the 4th Common Brainrot to come from a Fuse Machine, after Noobini Santanini (Santa's Fuse), Tartaragno (Witch Fuse), and Pipi Corni (standard Fuse Machine). These distinctions make Holy Arepa part of a growing category of Common units that require active engagement with limited-time or special machines rather than passive Red Carpet observation.

Same Stats as Pipi Corni — The Joint-Highest Common: The Fandom Wiki notes directly that Holy Arepa shares its income and cost with Pipi Corni — both cost $1,700 and both generate $14 per second. This makes them joint-highest earners within the entire Common tier. The same Fandom Wiki entry also records a comparative stat note: Holy Arepa earns only $1 per second more than Tartaragno, and costs $200 more — a marginal difference that the Wiki documents as a direct comparison between adjacent Common units at the top of the tier's income ceiling.

Divine Fuse Was Chosen by Community Poll — Leaked Feb 28, 2026: The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki and the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki both document that the Divine Fuse Machine was first revealed by creator SpyderSammy on February 28, 2026 during an Admin Abuse session. Sammy presented a community poll asking players to choose between the return of the OG Craft Machine or the new Divine Fuse — and the majority voted for the Divine Fuse. The machine was subsequently released on March 7, 2026, replacing the original Fuse Machine and introducing Holy Arepa as its Common-tier output alongside 14 other new Brainrots spanning Rare through Secret rarity.

Community Debate — Some Players Felt It Deserved a Higher Rarity: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki's discussion board documents a community reaction to the Divine Fuse pool: a player comment referenced in the Fandom Wiki's discussion thread stated that Holy Arepa was among the more visually appealing designs in the batch, but expressed that assigning it to Common rarity was a missed opportunity — particularly contrasted with other Divine Fuse outputs that the same commenter considered less impressive but received higher rarity placements. This sentiment reflects a broader community discussion around whether Holy Arepa's angelic food design was undervalued by its Common tier classification.

Lore — A Waiter Who Became an Arepa Through Divine Intervention: The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki provides the complete backstory: Holy Arepa was once a human restaurant waiter who developed such an intense devotion to arepas that he consumed them in extreme quantities until he physically transformed into one. Rather than accept the consequence of this transformation as death, he prayed to God for survival. God responded by granting him a face and a set of wings, allowing him to continue living as an airborne arepa. In his current form, he now flies around restaurants verifying that every dish meets the correct spice standards before being served — and personally applies his own rich, intensely spiced seasonings to food that does not pass his inspection.

The Arepa Is a Pre-Columbian Food With 2,800+ Years of History: Holy Arepa's design is rooted in one of the oldest continuously prepared foods in the Americas. The arepa is a round, thick flatbread made from ground maize dough that has been documented in Venezuelan and Colombian culinary tradition for approximately 2,800 years, with corn cultivation records in Colombia dating back approximately 6,000 years. The food has remained largely unchanged since pre-Columbian times, making it one of the few pre-contact traditions still widely consumed today. Its prominence as a cultural staple of Venezuela and Colombia gives Holy Arepa a distinct regional identity within the broader Italian Brainrot and food-themed Brainrot categories.

Strategy Tips

💡 Tips for Using Holy Arepa

1

Use the Divine Fuse early to secure the Index entry while it's live: Holy Arepa is the Common-tier output of the Divine Fuse Machine — a limited-time device. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki documents that previous fuse machines (Santa's Fuse, Witch Fuse) were eventually replaced, making their outputs steal-only afterward. The same pattern is expected to follow for the Divine Fuse. While the machine remains active, the most reliable path to acquiring Holy Arepa is direct fusion: submit four Brainrots to the Divine Fuse and work through the output pool. Securing the Index entry now avoids the more restrictive steal-only acquisition method once the machine is removed.

2

Do not hold base-mutation copies long-term — sell or fuse them: At $14/sec, Holy Arepa's default income contribution is minimal beyond the early game. The allthings.how guide positions it as the floor of the Divine Fuse pool, and once a player has secured the Index entry and any desired mutations, additional default copies should be sold for the $850 sell value or re-inserted into the Divine Fuse as fusion fodder. Retaining multiple base-mutation copies occupies base slots that produce diminishing returns compared to Rare-tier or higher units that can be acquired with the proceeds.

3

Target a Divine mutation for a notable income upgrade: The Holy Arepa Gallery documented in the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki confirms the Divine mutation is available for this character. A Divine-mutated Holy Arepa generates $140/sec — a 10x multiplier that elevates a Common-tier unit into the income range of base-level Epic Brainrots. Since the Divine mutation is tied to completing the Divine Index, players who are actively pursuing index completion will find Holy Arepa's Divine mutation a worthwhile byproduct of that progression goal.

4

Use it as low-cost Divine Fuse input if chasing higher-rarity outputs: Because Holy Arepa costs only $1,700 and can be re-fused into the Divine Fuse Machine, players farming for high-rarity Divine Fuse outputs — particularly Brainrot God and Secret-tier results like Griffin ($400M/sec) — can use accumulated Holy Arepa copies as part of a four-unit input batch. The ggwtb.com guide advises pairing lower-value inputs like Holy Arepa with higher-value fodder Brainrots to balance cost against output probability, rather than using only top-tier Brainrots which may produce wasteful over-investment for a given target rarity.
