What is Caramello Filtrello?

Caramello Filtrello is a Legendary-rarity Brainrot in the Roblox game Steal A Brainrot. With a base cost of $255,000 and a solid $1,000/sec passive income, it serves as a dependable late-game income source. Its unique caramel-filter design — featuring rich amber hues, playful filtering elements, and whimsical coffee-themed accents — makes it a distinctive addition to any collection.

Caramello Filtrello plays a valuable role in late-game progression: its $1,000/sec output provides reliable passive income that helps players save for higher-tier Brainrots. Originally introduced through the Craft Machine, it represents one of the game's most creatively themed Legendary units. As a Craft Machine-exclusive unit currently marked unobtainable, it rewards legacy players who obtained it during its original release.

How to Obtain

Caramello Filtrello is obtainable through two verified methods, though currently limited:

1. Craft Machine (Exclusive): Caramello Filtrello was originally obtainable via the Craft Machine by combining specific Brainrots in unique recipes. The Craft Machine featured a tiered success system where Legendary units like Caramello Filtrello had lower crafting success rates than Common or Rare units. However, the Craft Machine was replaced by newer mechanics, making this method currently unavailable.

2. Stealing: As a core game mechanic, players can also acquire Caramello Filtrello by raiding other users' bases and stealing it from an unlocked base. This method remains viable only if another player already owns the unit, making it a rare but possible acquisition path for legacy collectors.
