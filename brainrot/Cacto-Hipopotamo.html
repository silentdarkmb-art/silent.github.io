Cacto Hipopotamo

Cacto Hipopotamo is a Rare Brainrot in Steal a Brainrot — the 5th Rare unit in the in-game Brainrot Index, positioned above Boneca Ambalabu and below Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur. It costs $6,500 and generates $50 per second, placing it as the third-highest-earning standard Red Carpet Rare. Created by TikTok user @ofuscabreno — the same creator behind Boneca Ambalabu and Brr Brr Patapim — the character depicts a tall green cactus body topped with a blocky purple hippo face and walking on a pair of large orange sandals. It was added to the game in Update 7 on July 12, 2025, alongside Pandaccini Bananini and Las Tralaleritas. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki notes it should not be confused with Tric Trac Baraboom, a separate Rare Brainrot, and that its original meme name is Il Cacto Hippopotamo.

What is Cacto Hipopotamo?

Cacto Hipopotamo is a Rare-tier Brainrot in Steal a Brainrot — the 5th Rare unit in the in-game Brainrot Index, positioned directly above Boneca Ambalabu ($40/sec) and below Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur ($55/sec). Confirmed by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki, it costs $6,500 and generates $50 per second, placing it as the third-highest-earning standard Red Carpet Rare behind Tric Trac Baraboom and Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur. The character depicts a tall, green, vertically-striped cactus body topped with the blocky purple head of a hippopotamus — complete with short tusks — and supported by two large orange sandals. It was added to the game in Update 7 on July 12, 2025, alongside Pandaccini Bananini and Las Tralaleritas.

The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki explicitly records that Cacto Hipopotamo should not be confused with Tric Trac Baraboom — a separate Rare Brainrot at a higher Index position and income. It was created by TikTok user @ofuscabreno, the same creator behind Boneca Ambalabu and Brr Brr Patapim. The character carries no Rebirth gate requirement and holds no Ritual, making it a straightforward income unit whose acquisition is entirely through standard Red Carpet observation or theft.

🎯

How to Obtain

Cacto Hipopotamo is obtainable exclusively from the Red Carpet — the central conveyor belt in Steal a Brainrot where Brainrots of all rarities walk past and can be purchased by any player in the server for $6,500. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and u4gm.com both confirm there is no alternative acquisition route: it cannot be obtained through Fuse Machines, Fishing Events, Lucky Blocks, or seasonal event systems. Based on the large-scale community tracking survey in the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki — recording 1,515 Red Carpet Brainrot appearances — Cacto Hipopotamo appeared 64 times, yielding an observed spawn rate of approximately 4.22%.

This rate is lower than Bandito Bobritto (4.29%) and Trippi Troppi (4.48%) but slightly higher than Gangster Footera (3.96%), placing it in the mid-range of Red Carpet Rare spawn probabilities. Players purchase it the moment it appears on the carpet, or steal a copy from another player's unlocked base — the two acquisition methods documented by the Fandom Wiki. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki notes it is a permanent member of the Red Carpet pool with no limited-availability history.

Meme / Lore Origin

Cacto Hipopotamo originates from the Italian Brainrot meme created by TikTok user @ofuscabreno, who uploaded the original video under the name Il Cacto Hippopotamo. The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki documents the original TikTok clip — which garnered 502,000 likes and over 6,600 comments — as a short video pairing the AI-generated image of a cactus-hippo figure with an original Italian poem read in a dramatic voice. The poem, translated and documented by the Miraheze Wiki, narrates a giant cactus with an elegant hippopotamus head wandering a scorching desert on oversized sandals, walking endlessly through the wind in search of a destination that is never reached. The character is described as solitary and persevering — walking "never stopping, in a vast world, where the sun cannot be embraced."

The Miraheze Wiki also records a separate lore element from a VS video: Cacto Hipopotamo encounters Lirili Larila and, despite being described as having modest combat strength, manages to kick Lirili Larila — an outcome the Wiki notes is notable given the power discrepancy. A toilet also appears near the character in the desert for unknown reasons, which the Miraheze Wiki records as unexplained. The name combines the Spanish/Portuguese words for "cactus" (cacto) and "hippopotamus" (hipopótamo), with the Italian original using hippopotamo — a slight orthographic deviation consistent with Italian Brainrot's tradition of mixing Italian and Portuguese/Spanish.

Community Status

Cacto Hipopotamo is widely regarded within the Steal A Brainrot community as one of the most visually distinctive mid-game Rares. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki describes it as a "wonderfully bizarre and charming character" that embodies Italian Brainrot or Balkan Brainrot meme culture through its absurd hybrid design. The u4gm.com guide notes that Cacto Hipopotamo gained significant viral presence on TikTok and YouTube, where its image appeared in numerous Brainrot compilation videos — giving it broader recognition outside the game itself. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki Gallery page documents that the Gold mutation combined with the Galaxy Trait is regarded by the community as the best visual combination for this character, particularly under "meteor event" lighting where the gold gloss amplifies the contrast between the cactus texture and the hippo head.

Cacto Hipopotamo's in-game Roblox voxel model is based on the Italian Brainrot character of the same name — a cactus-hippo hybrid — adapted from the original TikTok image created by @ofuscabreno. The full visual composition is confirmed by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki, steal-a-brainrot.wiki, the u4gm.com guide, and the uploaded in-game screenshot provided for reference. The design blends two completely unrelated organisms — a desert plant and an African megafauna — into a single upright figure. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki describes it as "a tall cactus reminiscent of a classic Saguaro" in visual profile.

Body — The Cactus

The primary form of Cacto Hipopotamo is a tall, rectangular green cactus torso rendered in Roblox's voxel block format. The u4gm.com guide and steal-a-brainrot.wiki both describe the body as bright green with a vertical striped pattern — alternating lighter and darker green columns running from top to bottom — with scattered small yellow patches across the surface. As visible in the uploaded in-game screenshot, the body is blocky and wide, filling the majority of the character's height. There are no arms extending from the cactus body in the in-game model — the Fandom Wiki and the uploaded image both confirm the character consists only of the torso, head, and sandals, with no lateral limb protrusions despite the Saguaro-cactus aesthetic.

Head — The Hippopotamus

Protruding from the top of the cactus torso is the head of a hippopotamus — a large, blocky, roughly square-shaped head rendered in purple/grey-blue coloring, clearly visible in the uploaded screenshot. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and u4gm.com both confirm the head features two short white tusks visible at the lower jaw, consistent with standard hippo anatomy and Italian Brainrot character exaggeration. The head has two square dark eyes and small pink inner ears visible on both sides. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki notes the head is described in the original meme as an "elegant hippopotamus head" — a descriptor directly quoted in the Miraheze Wiki's poem translation and reflected in the model's proportioned, upright face placement.

Sandals / Slippers

The most immediately recognizable accessory on the character is its pair of large orange sandals — oversized footwear at the base of the cactus body, confirmed by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki, u4gm.com, and the uploaded screenshot. The Fandom Wiki describes them as "yellow slippers" in its entry, while the steal-a-brainrot.wiki and the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki refer to them as "sandals" — the uploaded in-game screenshot confirms their orange/amber coloration with a slightly raised, ridged sole structure rendered in darker orange-brown. The sandals are disproportionately large compared to the rest of the body, sitting flat on the ground and extending outward. The Miraheze Wiki poem refers to them as "due sandali giganti" — two giant sandals — consistent with the oversized in-game rendering.

Gold Mutation Visual Note

The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki Gallery page documents a community-recognized visual detail for the Gold mutation: when Cacto Hipopotamo carries a Gold mutation, the gloss effect significantly amplifies the visual contrast between the cactus texture and the purple hippo head — particularly during the in-game "meteor event" lighting, which the u4gm.com guide notes players commonly misspell as "metoer." The Fandom Wiki Gallery confirms that the Gold mutation combined with the Galaxy Trait is widely regarded by the community as the most aesthetically compelling combination for this character.

Cacto Hipopotamo occupies the 5th position in the Rare tier by Index order, generating $50 per second from a base cost of $6,500 — an income-to-cost efficiency of approximately 0.77%. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki confirms it is "competitive for a Rare unit" relative to others in its cost bracket, offering a step up from Boneca Ambalabu ($40/sec) at a moderate price increase. The ofzenandcomputing.com guide notes that Cacto Hipopotamo and above are considered the best value points in the entire Rare tier, marking the range where income gains per dollar invested are strongest before players transition to Epic-tier acquisitions.

Trading Value

Cacto Hipopotamo holds moderate but consistent trade value as a mid-range Rare unit. Unlike Boneca Ambalabu, it carries no Rebirth gate requirement — meaning its demand is driven entirely by income optimization and collector appeal. The u4gm.com guide notes that its $50/sec base income and relatively approachable $6,500 cost position it as a desirable trade target for mid-game players looking to bridge the gap between the lower Rares and the more expensive upper Rares. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki notes its combination of a distinct visual design and steady income makes it a collector favorite. Mutated copies carry the strongest trade leverage — particularly Bloodrot ($100/sec) during Bloodmoon Events, Radioactive ($425/sec), and Rainbow ($500/sec) — with the Fandom Wiki Gallery specifically noting that a Gold mutation with Galaxy Trait is regarded as the single most attractive visual combination for this character.

Original Name is Il Cacto Hippopotamo: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki explicitly records that Cacto Hipopotamo's original Italian Brainrot name is Il Cacto Hippopotamo — using the Italian article "Il" (the) and the Italian spelling "Hippopotamo" with a double "p," which differs slightly from the Spanish/Portuguese "hipopótamo." This naming follows the Italian Brainrot tradition of assigning Italian-language prefixes or descriptors to characters whose names otherwise blend Spanish, Portuguese, or invented words. The in-game name "Cacto Hipopotamo" drops the article and simplifies the spelling, following the same localization pattern used for other Italian Brainrot characters entering the game's Index.

Created by @ofuscabreno — Same Creator as Boneca Ambalabu and Brr Brr Patapim: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki both confirm that Cacto Hipopotamo was created by TikTok user @ofuscabreno — the same individual who also created Boneca Ambalabu and Brr Brr Patapim, two of the most recognized characters in the game. @ofuscabreno's TikTok post of the original "Il Cacto Hipopotamo" clip accumulated 502,000 likes and over 6,600 comments, placing it among the highest-engagement videos in the Italian Brainrot category. This makes @ofuscabreno one of the most represented creators in Steal a Brainrot's Rare tier.

Added in Update 7 on July 12, 2025: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki documents that Cacto Hipopotamo was introduced in Update 7, released on July 12, 2025 — the same update that also added Pandaccini Bananini and Las Tralaleritas. Update 7 was one of the larger content drops of Steal a Brainrot's mid-game phase, expanding the Rare tier with multiple new units during a period when the game was already breaking concurrent player records. Cacto Hipopotamo was one of the earliest @ofuscabreno characters to enter the game following Boneca Ambalabu's launch-day addition.

Do Not Confuse with Tric Trac Baraboom: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki includes a direct disambiguation note on the Cacto Hipopotamo page: the character is not to be confused with Tric Trac Baraboom — a separate Rare Brainrot that appears higher in the Index at $9,000 cost and $65/sec income. The Fandom Wiki does not specify the exact reason for the disambiguation, but the visual similarity may stem from both characters being tall, non-humanoid entities on the Rare Red Carpet. Tric Trac Baraboom occupies a significantly higher income bracket and is distinguishable by its own separate design.

A Toilet Appears Near the Character for Unknown Reasons: The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki documents an unexplained detail in the original meme content: a toilet appears near Cacto Hipopotamo in the middle of the desert — present in some versions of the visual but with no explanation provided in the lore or the original creator's content. The Miraheze Wiki records this as unknown in context, consistent with the broader Italian Brainrot tradition of including bizarre, non-sequitur objects in character imagery. This toilet has not been incorporated into the in-game Roblox voxel model.

Spawn Rate — 4.22% (64 / 1,515): The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki's community tracking data — based on 1,515 observed Red Carpet appearances — recorded Cacto Hipopotamo appearing 64 times, yielding an observed spawn rate of approximately 4.22%. This places it slightly below Bandito Bobritto (4.29%) and Trippi Troppi (4.48%) but above Gangster Footera (3.96%) and Boneca Ambalabu (2.83%). The u4gm.com guide classifies it as within the practical acquisition range for active players without requiring extended carpet-farming sessions.

Gold + Galaxy Trait is the Community's Preferred Mutation Combo: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki's dedicated Gallery page for Cacto Hipopotamo documents a community-recognized visual preference: the Gold mutation combined with the Galaxy Trait is explicitly described as "the best combo of mutations" for this character. The u4gm.com guide adds context: during the in-game "meteor event" lighting — often misspelled by players as "metoer event" — the Gold gloss effect significantly enhances the visual contrast between the cactus body's texture and the purple hippo head, giving the character a distinctly luminous desert aesthetic that no other mutation replicates in the same way.

Strategy Tips

💡 Tips for Using Cacto Hipopotamo

1

Acquire it as the natural next Red Carpet target after Boneca Ambalabu: The ofzenandcomputing.com guide identifies Cacto Hipopotamo as the point in the Rare tier where income-per-dollar value is strongest — placing it as the recommended acquisition priority for players who have already secured Boneca Ambalabu ($40/sec). Its 4.22% spawn rate is comparable to the other standard Rares, so purchase it immediately upon appearance on the carpet rather than waiting. It requires no Rebirth or Ritual conditions, meaning it functions as a pure income upgrade with no attached progression gates.

2

Target Bloodrot during the Bloodmoon Event for Epic-level output: A Bloodrot-mutated Cacto Hipopotamo generates $100/sec — a clean doubling that places it in the income bracket of several base-level Epic Brainrots. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki confirms all standard mutations apply to this character, and the Bloodmoon Event provides the Bloodrot upgrade without direct purchase. Prioritize exposing a Cacto Hipopotamo to the Bloodmoon mutation window before investing in Epic-tier acquisitions, as the resulting $100/sec output may delay the need for an Epic unit and free up cash for higher-tier farming.

3

Aim for Gold mutation with Galaxy Trait for maximum visual and income synergy: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki Gallery page specifically highlights that the Gold mutation combined with the Galaxy Trait is regarded by the community as the best combination for this character. At $62.50/sec with Gold applied and the visual enhancement of the Galaxy Trait — particularly under meteor event lighting — this combination offers both practical income improvement and a visually distinctive base unit. The u4gm.com guide notes the Gold form is particularly striking under meteor lighting, making it one of the most recognizable mutated units in the Rare pool.

4

Hold copies for trading leverage once you progress beyond the Rare tier: Because Cacto Hipopotamo sits at a significant income step-up ($50/sec) from the cluster of lower Rares and carries no mandatory Rebirth or Ritual role, extra copies are useful as mid-game trade assets. Players who have not yet acquired it will actively trade for it while building toward the upper Rares. A base-mutation copy can be leveraged for Common bundle trades or equivalent-tier Rare swaps, while mutated copies — particularly Bloodrot ($100/sec) and Radioactive ($425/sec) — carry genuine interest from a broad range of mid-to-late-game players seeking income upgrades.
