Tim Cheese

⭐ Common

Timothy Weinersnitzel Cheese, better known as Tim Cheese, is a notorious criminal mastermind and the leader of the "Breadsticks" gang. Abandoned as a youth on the streets of Chicago, he evolved into a master strategist and assassin. His life is driven by a refined palate for rare and exotic cheeses and a long-standing rivalry with other figures like John Pork.

💰 Base Cost $500
⚡ Income / Second $5
📈 Efficiency 1%
🎉 Event Regular

🎯 How to Obtain Tim Cheese

📖

Overview

Tim Cheese is a Common rarity Brainrot that serves as a cornerstone unit for new players. Due to his affordable price range of $500, players often "spam" him to build a quick economic foundation for their first Rebirth.

🎨

Appearance

He is an anthropomorphic rat dressed in a sharp black suit, white dress shirt, and a blue necktie. His signature look is defined by his square-shaped sunglasses and a confident stance, typically with his hands tucked into his pockets.

📊

Value

While he holds a low market value for veteran players, his utility value is extremely high for beginners. His worth increases significantly when found with rare mutations such as Diamond, Yin Yang, or the elusive Bloodrot variant

🧠

Trivia

  • • Origins: Unlike many characters in the game, Tim Cheese is not of Italian origin; he is derived from the John Pork Lore that went viral on TikTok in 2022.
  • • Family Ties:: He is reportedly the cousin of the famous rapper Biggie Cheese.
  • • Obtainment: He can be found roaming the Red Carpet or acquired by stealing from other players' bases.
  • • Avatar Item: There is an official Tim Cheese Head UGC item available on Roblox for players who wish to match his look.
