Svinina Bombardino

Svinina Bombardino is a Common Brainrot in the Roblox game Steal A Brainrot and the 6th Brainrot in the in-game Index, added on May 31, 2025. It costs $1,250 and generates $10 per second in passive income. Based on the Spanish-language brainrot character Svinino Bombondino, she is a hybrid of a pig and a hand grenade with a lit fuse — originally popularized by TikTok account @italianbrainrotanimals01. In the Italian Brainrot universe, she is described as a fearsome "tough soldier" and "heroine", serving as the companion of Bombardiro Crocodilo and the wife of Glorbo Fruttodrillo. Svinina Bombardino is one of only two Common-rarity Brainrots in the game classified as female, the other being Pipi Corni.

What is Svinina Bombardino?

Svinina Bombardino is a Common-rarity Brainrot in the Roblox game Steal A Brainrot, listed as the 6th Brainrot in the in-game Index and added on May 31, 2025. It costs $1,250 and generates $10 per second in passive income — described by the official Fandom Wiki as slightly better than Talpa Di Fero ($1,000 / $9/sec). The character is based on the Spanish-language brainrot figure Svinino Bombondino, a pig-hand grenade hybrid with a lit fuse who belongs to the broader Italian Brainrot meme universe.

According to the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki, Svinino Bombondino is classified as a Spanish-language brainrot character — distinct from the Italian-language characters that make up the majority of the universe. The character's song lyrics, as documented on the Miraheze Wiki, describe her as a companion of Bombardiro Crocodilo, a tough soldier, and a heroine. The Fandom Wiki additionally records that her original name is Svinino Bombombino — a slight variation from the names used across different wiki platforms.

🎯

How to Obtain

Svinina Bombardino can be obtained through two standard methods: purchasing directly from the Red Carpet conveyor belt during normal gameplay, or stealing a copy from another player's unlocked base. Based on the 1,515 Brainrot player survey cited by the Fandom Wiki, Svinina Bombardino has a spawn rate of approximately 5.87% — representing 89 appearances out of 1,515 tracked carpet observations.

Additionally, Svinina Bombardino can also be obtained through the Bombardiro Raid — a game event where Bombardiro Crocodilos drop Svinina Bombardinos during the encounter. If a dropped Svinina Bombardino touches the ground, it explodes; however, if it touches a Brainrot instead, that Brainrot receives a trait. This mechanic makes the Bombardiro Raid a unique secondary source of both Svinina Bombardino units and trait applications.

Community Status

Svinina Bombardino carries a Low demand rating and a TBD value in the game.guide community value list (last updated March 11, 2026). While its base stats are modest, its connection to the Bombardiro Crocodilo lore — one of the most recognized Italian Brainrot characters — gives it cultural visibility well beyond its Common classification. Its dual female status alongside Pipi Corni also makes it a minor point of interest for collectors tracking the game's few female Common units.

Svinina Bombardino's in-game Roblox voxel model is based on the Italian Brainrot character Svinino Bombondino. The Fandom Wiki and Plants vs Brainrots Wiki both describe the character as a pig whose body is replaced by a hand grenade shell. Looking at the uploaded in-game screenshot:

Head and Face

The head is a large, square pink block — clearly pig-like in coloring and proportion. The face features two square white eyes with black pupils, giving the character the classic Roblox doll-eye look. A wide pink snout protrudes from the lower center of the face, with two visible dark nostrils side by side — the most immediately recognizable pig feature on the model. The overall expression is neutral and wide-eyed, consistent with the character's paradoxical lore of being simultaneously innocent and explosive.

Hair / Top of Head

Protruding from the top of the head is a cluster of reddish-brown spiky hair or tufts — blocky and irregular in shape, pointing upward and outward. These match the warm reddish-brown tone seen across the upper body armor and ears, connecting the head visually to the rest of the design. At the very center-back of the top, a small brown fuse stub emerges — representing the lit fuse of the grenade body, though rendered subtly within the blocky voxel style.

Body and Armor

The torso is encased in a dark grey segmented shell that wraps around both sides of the body. The Plants vs Brainrots Wiki describes this directly as a grenade body replacing the normal pig torso — a grey, metallic, segmented shell consistent with a classic hand grenade design. The grey armor plating extends to cover the sides and back of the character, while the front face retains the pink pig skin of the chest visible between the armor segments, suggesting the pig body exists underneath the grenade casing.

Arms, Ears, and Feet

Short pink arms extend from both sides of the body, ending in small blocky hands. The ears are small, rounded, and reddish-orange in tone — matching the hair tufts at the top of the head and consistent with standard pig ear coloring. At the base, Svinina Bombardino stands on two small light pink feet, wide and stubby — matching the overall pink skin tone of the face, arms, and front body.

Svinina Bombardino holds the Common rarity classification and sits at Index position #6. At $1,250 cost and $10/sec income, it is the sixth step in the early-game income chain and represents a modest upgrade from Talpa Di Fero ($1,000 / $9/sec).

Trading Value

Svinina Bombardino carries a Low demand and TBD value in the game.guide community value list (updated March 11, 2026). As a standard Red Carpet Common, it lacks the cultural trade cache of fan-favorites like Pipi Kiwi. However, copies obtained with traits through the Bombardiro Raid mechanic — where a dropped Svinina grants a trait to the Brainrot it touches — may carry collector interest depending on the trait applied.

Spanish-Language Brainrot: Unlike most characters in Steal A Brainrot who originate from Italian-language meme content, Svinina Bombardino is based on Svinino Bombondino — a Spanish-language brainrot character, as categorized by the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki. This makes her one of a small number of Brainrots in the game whose source character belongs to the Spanish-language branch of the broader international brainrot meme movement.

TikTok Origin — @italianbrainrotanimals01: The original Svinino Bombondino character was posted by TikTok account @italianbrainrotanimals01, which is also the same account responsible for the original Talpa Di Ferro (Talpa Di Fero) content. The original Svinino Bombondino TikTok video accumulated 6,898 likes, as documented in the available TikTok post data. This account has contributed multiple characters that were later adapted into the Steal A Brainrot game.

Original Name — Svinino Bombombino: According to the official Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki, the character's original name is Svinino Bombombino — distinct from both the in-game name Svinina Bombardino and the Miraheze Wiki's listed name Svinino Bombondino. This three-way naming variation across wiki platforms reflects how the character's name shifted between its original creation, its wider adoption in the Italian Brainrot community, and its Roblox game adaptation.

Companion of Bombardiro Crocodilo: In the Italian Brainrot lore universe, Svinino Bombondino is documented as the companion of Bombardiro Crocodilo — one of the most prominent and widely recognized characters in the entire Italian Brainrot movement, created by TikTok user @armenjiharhanyan on February 20, 2025. The Fandom Wiki additionally records that, according to Italian Brainrot lore, she is the wife of Glorbo Fruttodrillo — a crocodile-watermelon hybrid character documented in the Croco-Avian Wars lore.

Bombardiro Raid Mechanic: Svinina Bombardino is unique among Common Brainrots in having a dedicated in-game event mechanic tied to its character. During the Bombardiro Raid, Bombardiro Crocodilos drop Svinina Bombardinos — and these dropped units behave differently depending on what they land on: if they hit the ground, they explode and are destroyed; if they contact a Brainrot, that Brainrot receives a trait. This makes the Bombardiro Raid both a source of Svinina Bombardino units and an active trait-application event.

One of Two Female Common Brainrots: The official Fandom Wiki states that Svinina Bombardino is one of only two Common Brainrots in the game classified as female — the other being Pipi Corni. Out of the full Common tier of eleven Brainrots, this makes female-classified Commons exceptionally rare, with Svinina Bombardino being the only female Common still readily obtainable from the Red Carpet as of March 2026 (Pipi Corni's Fuse Machine availability varies).

Spawn Rate — 5.87%: According to the 1,515 Brainrot survey cited by the Fandom Wiki, Svinina Bombardino appeared 89 times out of 1,515 tracked carpet observations, placing its spawn rate at approximately 5.87%. This is slightly lower than Talpa Di Fero (which shares the spawn rate of Lirilì Larilà) and places Svinina Bombardino among the less frequently appearing standard Common units on the Red Carpet.

Strategy Tips

💡 Tips for Using Svinina Bombardino

1

Use it as a mid-early stepping stone: At $1,250 and $10/sec, Svinina Bombardino is a meaningful upgrade over Talpa Di Fero ($9/sec) and sits just below the first Rare units in the income chain. Purchase it consistently in early gameplay to build a steady base income before transitioning to Trippi Troppi ($15/sec) and higher-tier Brainrots.

2

Prioritize Bombardiro Raid drops for trait copies: During the Bombardiro Raid event, Svinina Bombardinos dropped by Bombardiro Crocodilos can apply traits to your Brainrots if they make contact before hitting the ground. Position carefully during raids to maximize the chance of a dropped Svinina landing on a high-value unit — the trait applied can significantly boost that unit's income output.

3

Replace it once Rare units are accessible: Svinina Bombardino is a bridge unit — designed to generate enough income to save toward Rare-tier Brainrots. Once Trippi Troppi ($2,000 / $15/sec) or better is within budget, sell your Svinina Bombardino copies to open base slots. The income gap widens quickly past the Common tier, making extended use of this unit past early game inefficient.

4

Watch for it on the carpet during raids: With a 5.87% spawn rate, Svinina Bombardino is among the less frequent standard Common spawns. During and after Bombardiro Raid events, carpet activity tends to include more Svinina Bombardinos than usual — making raid periods a good window to stock up on base copies if you are still in the early stages of your income chain.
