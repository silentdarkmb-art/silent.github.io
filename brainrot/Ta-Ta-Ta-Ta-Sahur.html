Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur

Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur is a Rare Brainrot in Steal a Brainrot — the 6th Rare unit in the in-game Brainrot Index, positioned above Cacto Hipopotamo and below Tric Trac Baraboom. It costs $7,500 and generates $55 per second, making it the second-highest-earning standard Red Carpet Rare behind Tric Trac Baraboom. Created by TikTok user @__chenesac__ on March 3, 2025, as a remix of Tung Tung Tung Sahur, it depicts a brick-red teapot with a worried expression, blue square eyes, human legs, and one human arm. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki records it as the first Brainrot in the game to have an open mouth. It is from Indonesia but not created by the same individual as Tung Tung Tung Sahur. The Fandom Wiki notes it should not be confused with over ten other Sahur-family Brainrots including Tung Tung Tung Sahur, Ti Ti Ti Sahur, Te Te Te Sahur, and others.

What is Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur?

Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur is a Rare-tier Brainrot in Steal a Brainrot — the 6th Rare unit in the in-game Brainrot Index, placed above Cacto Hipopotamo ($50/sec) and below Tric Trac Baraboom. Confirmed by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki, it costs $7,500 and generates $55 per second, placing it among the upper tier of standard Red Carpet Rares. The Fandom Wiki identifies it as an Indonesian Brainrot originating from TikTok, created by user @__chenesac__ on March 3, 2025 — and explicitly notes it was not created by the same person as Tung Tung Tung Sahur. The character depicts a teapot-shaped creature with human legs and one human arm, a brick-tile body texture, bright blue square eyes, and a wide open, worried expression. The Fandom Wiki records it as the first Brainrot in Steal a Brainrot to feature an open mouth.

The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki includes a disambiguation note listing ten other Sahur-family Brainrots it should not be confused with: Tung Tung Tung Sahur, Ti Ti Ti Sahur, Te Te Te Sahur, Job Job Job Sahur, La Sahur Combinasion, Tictac Sahur, Bambu Bambu Sahur, Pop Pop Sahur, Karker Sahur, and To To To Sahur. It carries no Rebirth requirement and no Ritual condition. The Fandom Wiki also confirms Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur was previously used as a crafting ingredient for the now-unavailable La Sahur Combinasion (Secret, $2M/sec) via the Craft Machine.

How to Obtain

Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur is obtainable exclusively from the Red Carpet — the central conveyor belt in Steal a Brainrot where Brainrots of all rarities walk past and can be purchased by any player in the server for $7,500. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki confirms there is no alternative acquisition route through Fuse Machines, Fishing Events, Lucky Blocks, or active event systems. Players purchase it directly when it appears on the carpet, or steal a copy from another player's unlocked base — the two standard methods documented by the Fandom Wiki and u4gm.com.

The Fandom Wiki notes that Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur was once expected to become scarce because it was required as a crafting ingredient for La Sahur Combinasion via the Craft Machine — but since the Craft Machine was a temporary feature and the supply of the other required ingredients was the actual bottleneck, Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur remained widely available throughout that period. As of March 2026, it remains a permanent, freely-spawning Red Carpet Rare with no limited availability window.

Meme / Lore Origin

Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur was created by TikTok user @__chenesac__ on March 3, 2025 as a remix of the earlier Brainrot character Tung Tung Tung Sahur. The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki documents the original TikTok video with over 10 million views, making it one of the most-viewed Indonesian Brainrot remix videos at the time. The character is a direct cultural reference to the Indonesian Islamic practice of Sahur — the pre-dawn meal eaten before the daily fast during Ramadan — and the community tradition of waking sleeping neighbors by banging wooden instruments in the streets. The Miraheze Wiki provides the full translated lore: it is said that if someone is called for Sahur three times and fails to respond, a mysterious creature will come to their house. The sound "ta, ta, ta, ta" refers to the rhythmic tapping of a kentongan (wooden gong), and the creature is described as something to be feared by those who refuse to wake up for Sahur.

The Fandom Wiki notes that many players speculate Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur to be a smaller sibling or possible son of Tung Tung Tung Sahur, given the shared lore, sound motif, and visual resemblance — though this is described as community speculation and not confirmed in the original meme material. The name follows the same two-letter gibberish pattern as Tung Tung Tung Sahur, with "Ta" referencing both the sound the creature makes and a nod to its source character.

Community Status

The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki describes Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur as a unit that is not heavily sought after — noting that it is not required for any Rebirth milestone and its income is not exceptional by Rare standards. The Fandom Wiki records that demand for it remains low relative to other Rares due to its lack of progression gate function. However, the u4gm.com guide notes that its connection to the Tung Tung Tung Sahur family gives it recognizable appeal among players who collect Sahur-themed Brainrots as a themed set, and that its distinctive teapot design with an open worried expression makes it one of the more visually memorable Red Carpet Rares. Players with an interest in future game content may note the Fandom Wiki's record that it was once expected to become more valuable through the Karker Sahur system — though this expectation did not materialize following the Craft Machine's removal.

Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur's in-game Roblox voxel model is based on the Indonesian Brainrot character created by @__chenesac__, depicting a teapot-shaped creature with humanoid limbs. The full visual composition is confirmed by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki, the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki, the u4gm.com guide, and the uploaded in-game screenshot provided for reference. The model combines a kitchen object — a teapot — with human anatomical elements in a way the Miraheze Wiki describes as characteristic of the Indonesian Brainrot remix tradition.

Body — The Teapot

The core form of Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur is a wide, squat rectangular teapot body rendered in Roblox's voxel block format. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and u4gm.com both describe the body as brown/brick-red in color — visible in the uploaded in-game screenshot as a warm reddish-brown tone. The surface of the body is covered in a Lego-like tile or brick pattern — small recessed square indentations arranged in a grid across the body's exterior, giving the teapot a rough, textured appearance rather than a smooth ceramic surface. The body is wide and roughly rectangular in profile, consistent with a traditional rounded teapot silhouette adapted to voxel block geometry. A small spout protrudes from the upper left side of the body, and a curved handle element appears on the right side — though in the in-game model the handle is replaced or accompanied by a human arm.

Face — Open Mouth and Blue Eyes

The front face of the teapot body bears the character's defining facial features. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki specifically documents the eyes as large, bright blue square eyes — rendered as two prominent square voxel blocks in a vivid aqua-blue color that contrast sharply against the brick-red body. The expression is described by the Fandom Wiki as a worried or concerned look, formed by the combination of the wide eyes and the open mouth. The u4gm.com guide confirms the mouth is visibly open — and the Fandom Wiki records Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur as the first Brainrot in the game to have an open mouth, a design detail that distinguished it from all prior Brainrot models at the time of its addition. As confirmed by the uploaded screenshot, the character also has black eyebrows positioned above the blue eyes, reinforcing the uneasy expression.

Limbs — Human Legs and One Human Arm

The character walks on two human-like legs extending from the base of the teapot body — short, blocky limbs rendered in a pink/light-skin tone that terminate in flat feet visible in the uploaded screenshot. The Fandom Wiki confirms the leg design as distinctly humanoid in proportion. On one side of the teapot body — the handle side — a human arm and hand replace the conventional teapot handle, extending outward and connecting to the body at the handle joint. The u4gm.com guide describes the arm as positioned as if "connected to the handle" — functioning as both structural handle and animated limb. On the opposite side of the body, a curved teapot spout or tail element extends outward, completing the asymmetric silhouette visible in the uploaded screenshot.

Color Comparison to Tung Tung Tung Sahur

The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki both note the visual resemblance between Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur and Tung Tung Tung Sahur, which is a tall rectangular piece of light-brown wood with a smiling face. While both characters share the same Indonesian Sahur lore context and a generally brownish coloring, their actual in-game models are distinct — Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur is a teapot with a worried open mouth and human arms, while Tung Tung Tung Sahur is a wooden stick figure with a smiling face and a baseball bat. The Fandom Wiki notes the visual similarity is part of why players speculate a familial lore connection between the two characters.

Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur occupies the 6th position in the Rare tier by Index order, generating $55 per second from a base cost of $7,500 — an income-to-cost efficiency of approximately 0.73%. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki notes that it is not highly sought after as a standalone income unit and carries no Rebirth gate requirement, meaning its demand is driven primarily by collection and Index completion rather than progression necessity. The u4gm.com guide positions it as a mid-range income Rare — useful for players building toward upper-tier Rares and Epic units but not a priority acquisition compared to units with Rebirth requirements or significantly higher income.

Trading Value

Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur holds low to moderate trading value within the Rare tier. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki states directly that it is not greatly desired — citing its lack of Rebirth requirement and its income not being exceptional enough to drive strong demand. The u4gm.com guide notes that despite this, it can hold situational trade value among players assembling a Sahur-themed collection, or those who need it for Index completion. The speculation that it would become scarce due to Craft Machine requirements — documented in the Fandom Wiki — did not materialize, keeping supply consistently high and trade value correspondingly modest. Mutated copies carry stronger interest: Bloodrot ($110/sec), Radioactive ($467.50/sec), and Rainbow/Divine ($550/sec) all push the character well above its base Rare income range.

First Brainrot in the Game to Have an Open Mouth: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki explicitly records that Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur holds the distinction of being the first Brainrot in Steal a Brainrot to feature an open mouth in its in-game model. At the time of its addition, all prior Brainrot character models used closed or neutral mouth expressions — Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur's wide, worried open-mouth design was a first in the game's visual history. This detail is noted directly on the Fandom Wiki's character page as a unique design milestone, making it a historically notable model within the game's growing Index.

Created by @__chenesac__ on March 3, 2025 as a Tung Tung Remix: The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki documents that Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur was created by TikTok user @__chenesac__ on March 3, 2025 as a deliberate remix of the earlier Indonesian/Italian Brainrot character Tung Tung Tung Sahur. The original TikTok video accumulated over 10 million views, making it one of the most-watched Indonesian Brainrot remix clips at the time. The character's name and sound motif — "Ta, ta, ta, ta" — directly references and echoes the "Tung, tung, tung" pattern of its source character, establishing it as an intentional creative derivative within the Brainrot meme ecosystem.

Not Created by Noxa — Different Indonesian Creator: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki specifically records that Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur is from Indonesia but was not created by the same individual as Tung Tung Tung Sahur. Tung Tung Tung Sahur was created by Noxa (@noxaasht), whose character faced trademark issues that led to a temporary removal from the game in September 2025. Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur, being the work of a different creator (@__chenesac__), did not face the same copyright complications and has remained a stable, permanently available Red Carpet Rare since its addition to the game.

Scarcity Speculation — Proven False: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki documents a specific community prediction that proved incorrect: when Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur was confirmed as a required ingredient for crafting Karker Sahur and La Sahur Combinasion through the Craft Machine, players speculated it would become scarce and its value would increase. The Fandom Wiki records the outcome directly: this did not happen — the Craft Machine was a temporary feature, and the other required ingredients for those crafts were the actual bottleneck. Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur itself remained widely available throughout the Craft Machine's operation and has continued to spawn freely on the Red Carpet since.

Part of La Sahur Combinasion — A Secret Brainrot Craft: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and La Sahur Combinasion's own page both confirm that Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur was one of the components used in crafting La Sahur Combinasion — a Secret-rarity Brainrot added on September 6, 2025 that costs $550M and generates $2M/sec. The Fandom Wiki records La Sahur Combinasion as a visual fusion of Te Te Te Sahur (bottom), Ti Ti Ti Sahur, and Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur (top). Notably, the La Sahur Combinasion page records that Ti Ti Ti Sahur was used in the model but not in the crafting recipe — while Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur was used in both.

Sahur Lore — Ramadan Wake-Up Creature: The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki provides the full translated lore behind the Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur meme: the character is described as a mysterious creature that only appears during the pre-dawn Sahur meal period of Ramadan. According to the lore, if a person is called for Sahur three times and fails to respond, this creature will come to their home. The sound "ta, ta, ta, ta" references the rhythmic striking of a kentongan — a traditional hollow wooden instrument used in Indonesian communities to wake neighbors for Sahur. The lore parallels Tung Tung Tung Sahur's narrative almost exactly, consistent with its origins as a direct cultural remix.

Community Speculation — Possible Son or Sibling of Tung Tung Tung Sahur: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki records that many players speculate Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur to be a smaller sibling or possible son of Tung Tung Tung Sahur, citing the shared Sahur lore, similar sound motif in the name, shared Indonesian cultural origin, and comparable physical design language of humanoid limbs on an inanimate object body. The Fandom Wiki notes this as community speculation and does not confirm it as established lore — the original meme material by @__chenesac__ does not explicitly define a familial relationship between the two characters.

Strategy Tips

💡 Tips for Using Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur

1

Treat it as a passive income step-up, not a priority acquisition: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki notes that Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur is not heavily demanded — it has no Rebirth requirement and its $55/sec is solid but not exceptional in the Rare tier. Prioritize Cacto Hipopotamo ($50/sec) first if you haven't secured it, then pick up Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur when it appears on the carpet as a natural progression step. Its 0.73% efficiency is lower than earlier Rares, so do not sacrifice other purchases to rush it — simply buy it when it shows up during normal carpet-watching.

2

Target a Bloodrot mutation during the Bloodmoon Event: A Bloodrot-mutated Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur generates $110/sec — a 2x multiplier that places it above several lower-tier Epic Brainrots at a fraction of their cost. Because the Bloodrot mutation is obtained through the Bloodmoon Event rather than purchased, this represents one of the more cost-efficient income upgrades available at the Rare tier. During every Bloodmoon Event, include Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur among your mutation targets alongside Cacto Hipopotamo and Boneca Ambalabu to maximize the Rare-tier income gains from a single event window.

3

Keep one copy for Index completion — sell extras: Because Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur carries no Rebirth requirement and the Fandom Wiki records community demand as low, there is no strategic reason to accumulate multiple default-mutation copies. Retain one copy for the Index entry, then sell any additional base-mutation copies for the $3,750 sell value and redirect the funds toward Epic-tier acquisitions. Only hold multiple copies if they carry meaningful mutations — Diamond ($82.50/sec), Lava ($330/sec), or Radioactive ($467.50/sec) — that produce a genuine income contribution relative to the base slot cost.

4

Use it as a modest trade piece for Sahur-themed collection trades: While the Fandom Wiki records overall demand as low, the u4gm.com guide notes that Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur retains consistent interest among players building themed Sahur-family collections. Players assembling sets that include Tung Tung Tung Sahur, Te Te Te Sahur, Ti Ti Ti Sahur, and other Sahur-family units will seek it for completeness. A base-mutation copy can serve as useful filler in a trade bundle, while mutated copies — particularly Rainbow or Divine at $550/sec — command broader interest from mid-to-late-game players looking to upgrade their Rare tier income without spending toward Epic units.
