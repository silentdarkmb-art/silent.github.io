Noobini Santanini

Noobini Santanini is a Common Brainrot in Steal a Brainrot — a festive Christmas variant of Noobini Pizzanini, built on the classic Roblox Noob avatar body and dressed in full Santa Claus attire with colorful holiday light strings wrapped around its torso and arms. Released during the Christmas update on December 7, 2025, it is obtainable via the limited-time Santa's Fuse machine by inserting four Common Brainrots, or by stealing a copy from another player's base. It costs $1,100 and generates $11 per second, making it the highest-earning Common Brainrot obtainable through an event machine at its base cost. The character was created by BRAZILIAN SPYDER and is the lowest-tier output of the Santa's Fuse system — also serving as the primary fusion ingredient for crafting higher Christmas-tier Brainrots like Frogo Elfo.

What is Noobini Santanini?

Noobini Santanini is a Common-rarity Brainrot in Steal a Brainrot — a holiday-themed variant of Noobini Pizzanini introduced during the Christmas update on December 7, 2025. Created by BRAZILIAN SPYDER, the character retains the iconic Roblox Noob avatar body structure of its base counterpart while swapping out the pizza delivery aesthetic for a full Santa Claus costume. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and steal-a-brainrot.wiki both confirm it costs $1,100 and generates $11 per second, placing it 7th in the Common-tier income ranking — directly above Svinina Bombardino ($10/s) and below Raccooni Jandelini ($12/s) in the Index.

The steal-a-brainrot.wiki notes that the character is marginally larger than the standard Noobini Pizzanini model, features Lego-style studs on its arms, legs, and head that are absent from the base version, and runs cleaner animations. The Fandom Wiki also confirms that for a brief window after release, Noobini Santanini had no walking animation — a bug that creator Sammy subsequently resolved. The character is the first Common Brainrot to receive an event variant in the game's history, according to the Fandom Wiki.

🎯

How to Obtain

Noobini Santanini was originally obtainable through the Santa's Fuse machine — a limited-time fusion system that replaced the Brainrot Trader in the game lobby for the duration of the Christmas event. The machine was added on December 7, 2025 and operated until December 12, 2025. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki and Sportskeeda guide confirm it required inserting four Common-rarity Brainrots into the machine to begin a fusion — with a fuse time of 15 minutes for Common-through-Epic inputs — and a high probability (approximately 67%) of receiving Noobini Santanini when the lowest-tier Common inputs such as four standard Noobinis were used.

The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki confirms that the Santa's Fuse machine has since been replaced by the Duels Machine, meaning Noobini Santanini is no longer craftable and is now obtainable only by stealing a copy from another player's base — the secondary acquisition method documented by both the Fandom Wiki and u4gm.com. Any copies in circulation at the end of the Christmas event became legacy holdings, making theft the only active path to acquisition as of March 2026.

🎅

Santa's Fuse Event Context

The Santa's Fuse machine was the central mechanic of the December 2025 Christmas update — a limited-time crafting system housed in a snow-covered, LEGO-style festive workshop area of the game map built from red and green bricks with candy cane smokestacks. The ssegold.com and steal-a-brainrot.wiki guides both confirm the machine could produce 15 exclusive Christmas-themed Brainrots across all rarity tiers, with Noobini Santanini as the lowest-tier output. Higher-rarity Christmas Brainrots required progressively more valuable fusion inputs: fusing four Common inputs gave the highest probability of receiving Noobini Santanini, while inserting high-income Brainrots such as Epic or Legendary units raised the probability of receiving rarer outputs like Penguin Tree (Epic, $270/s), Sealo Regalo (Legendary, $1,800/s), or the Secret-tier Chicleteira Noelteira.

Community Status

Within the Steal A Brainrot community, Noobini Santanini holds modest but consistent standing as a recognizable collectible from the game's first major holiday event. The u4gm.com guide notes that because it is a Common unit and was obtainable in large quantities during the event through repeated four-Noobini fusions, it does not carry high trading value — most players treat base-mutation copies as filler or early-game income fillers rather than trade priorities. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki highlights its role as fusion fodder during the event: mass-producing Noobini Santaninis from cheap Noobini inputs and then re-inserting them into the machine was the standard player strategy for climbing toward higher Christmas-tier units like Frogo Elfo. Following the end of the event, its status shifted to that of a legacy seasonal collectible, with its only acquisition route being theft from players who retained copies.

Noobini Santanini's in-game Roblox voxel model is built on the same base as Noobini Pizzanini — the classic Roblox Noob avatar rendered in block form — with a complete costume swap replacing the pizza delivery outfit with holiday attire. The design is confirmed by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki, steal-a-brainrot.wiki, u4gm.com guide, and the uploaded in-game screenshot provided. Three structural differences from the base Noobini Pizzanini are documented: the body is marginally larger, studs are present on the arms, legs, and head, and the animations are described as cleaner.

Body and Skin

The base body of Noobini Santanini follows the standard Roblox Noob avatar silhouette — a yellow-skinned, blocky humanoid form with a rectangular torso, cylindrical arms, and brick-like legs. The u4gm.com guide confirms the body color is yellow on the head and upper limbs, with bright green pants covering the lower half. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki notes the model is slightly larger than Noobini Pizzanini and uniquely features Lego-style circular studs on the surface of the arms, legs, and head — a design detail absent from the base Noobini Pizzanini model. These studs give the character a more toy-like, constructed appearance consistent with its festive doll theme.

Santa Costume and Hat

Over the yellow Noob body, Noobini Santanini wears a light blue torso covering — visible in the uploaded screenshot — rather than the red coat described in some sources, as the uploaded in-game image is the primary reference. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki documents the character wears a Santa hat — a red conical hat with a white fluffy brim — positioned on top of the blocky Noob head. The Fandom Wiki confirms the character also sports a white beard below the face, completing the Santa Claus visual identity alongside the hat. The u4gm.com guide notes the overall costume gives the character an immediately recognizable holiday profile that stands out clearly on the Red Carpet and in player bases.

Christmas Light Chain and Aura

The most visually distinctive feature of Noobini Santanini is the colorful Christmas light string wrapped around its torso and arms — confirmed by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and visible in the uploaded screenshot as green wire with small multicolored bulb ornaments in red, pink, cyan, and yellow. The lights spiral across the front of the torso and extend along both arms in a diagonal wrap pattern. Beyond the physical model, the Fandom Wiki documents that Noobini Santanini also emits a surrounding aura of colorful light bulbs — a particle effect that projects floating multicolored bulb shapes around the character, reinforcing the Christmas light theme as an ambient visual effect rather than just a model accessory.

Animation Notes

The steal-a-brainrot.wiki records that Noobini Santanini runs cleaner animations than Noobini Pizzanini and uses an R6-style animation framework consistent with standard Roblox Noob movement. The Fandom Wiki documents a launch bug: for a brief period after the December 7, 2025 release, the character had no walking animation — it would appear to glide without leg movement. The game's creator Sammy identified and corrected this issue in a subsequent patch.

Noobini Santanini occupies the 7th position in the Common-tier income ranking, generating $11 per second from a base cost of $1,100. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki confirms it delivers a 1.00% income-to-cost efficiency ratio — the highest achievable for a $1,100-cost Common unit and notably stronger than several other Common Brainrots in the same price bracket. The u4gm.com guide describes its primary economic function during the Christmas event as serving two roles simultaneously: a functional income unit for newly starting players, and the primary ingredient for climbing the Santa's Fuse output chain toward higher-tier Christmas Brainrots.

Trading Value

Noobini Santanini carries low trading value among the broader Steal A Brainrot player base. The u4gm.com guide notes that because it was producible in large quantities during the Christmas event through repeated four-Noobini fusions at minimal cost, the supply of base-mutation copies remained high throughout the event window. Most players treat default-mutation copies as filler items in trades rather than leverage assets. Post-event, its value as a legacy seasonal collectible gives it modest interest among completionists and holiday-themed collection builders — particularly for players who missed the Christmas event entirely. Mutated copies, especially Rainbow ($110/s) and Radioactive ($93.50/s), hold stronger trade interest since the mutation itself represents the primary incremental value above the low base income.

First Common Brainrot to Receive an Event Variant: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki confirms that Noobini Santanini holds the distinction of being the first Common-rarity Brainrot in the game to receive a variant. Before it, character variants existed at higher rarity tiers, but no Common Brainrot had been given a seasonal or event-specific alternate version. This made Noobini Santanini a milestone unit within the game's design history, establishing a precedent for Common-tier variants that could follow in future seasonal updates.

Created by BRAZILIAN SPYDER: The steal-a-brainrot.wiki and Fandom Wiki both attribute Noobini Santanini to the creator BRAZILIAN SPYDER — one of the game's credited character designers. BRAZILIAN SPYDER is also the credited creator of Noobini Pizzanini, the base character on which Noobini Santanini is modeled. The shared designer connection between the base unit and its Christmas variant reflects a deliberate design continuity, with the holiday version serving as a direct extension of the original Noobini concept under the same creator's direction.

Launch Bug — No Walking Animation: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki documents that when Noobini Santanini was first released on December 7, 2025, the character had no walking animation — it moved across the carpet and base floor without any leg movement, appearing to slide or glide rather than walk. This was confirmed as a bug. Game creator Sammy addressed the issue in a subsequent patch, restoring standard R6-style bipedal movement. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki notes the corrected animations are notably cleaner than those of Noobini Pizzanini.

Santa's Fuse Machine Released December 7, 2025: The ssegold.com and Sportskeeda guides both document that the Santa's Fuse machine was introduced as part of the December 7, 2025 Christmas update — positioned in the main game lobby at the location previously occupied by the Brainrot Trader. Noobini Santanini was the lowest-tier output of the machine, obtainable at approximately 67% probability when four standard Noobinis were used as inputs. The system ran until December 12, 2025, after which it was shut down as a limited-time event feature.

Costs Less than Svinina Bombardino but Earns More: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki records a specific stat comparison: Noobini Santanini costs $1,100 and generates $11/sec, while Svinina Bombardino costs $1,200 and generates only $10/sec. This means Noobini Santanini is both cheaper and higher-earning than Svinina Bombardino — an adjacent Common Brainrot — giving it a superior income-to-cost efficiency ratio of 1.00% versus Svinina Bombardino's 0.83%. The Fandom Wiki notes this comparison directly, marking it as one of the few Common units that offers a visible stat advantage over its neighboring Index entry.

Primary Fusion Fodder for the Christmas Event Chain: The steal-a-brainrot.wiki identifies Noobini Santanini's main in-game role during the Christmas event as fusion fodder — not as a held income unit, but as a crafting ingredient for ascending the Santa's Fuse output tier. Players who mass-produced Noobini Santaninis by feeding four-Noobini batches into the machine would then re-insert the resulting Santaninis as inputs for the next fusion cycle, statistically targeting the Rare-tier output Frogo Elfo ($67/sec). This chain-crafting strategy was the standard documented approach for reaching Christmas Brainrots above the Common tier.

Now Steal-Only — Santa's Fuse Replaced by Duels Machine: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki confirms that the Santa's Fuse machine no longer exists in the game as of the current version — it was removed after the Christmas event window closed and subsequently replaced by the Duels Machine. Noobini Santanini is therefore no longer craftable through any in-game system as of March 2026, and the only remaining acquisition method is stealing a copy from another player's base. This transition from event-craftable to steal-only status makes all existing copies legacy holdings from the December 2025 event window.

Strategy Tips

💡 Tips for Using Noobini Santanini

1

During the event, mass-fuse rather than hold: The steal-a-brainrot.wiki identifies Noobini Santanini's primary value during the active Santa's Fuse event as fusion fodder — not as a base income unit. The most efficient event strategy documented by the guide was to continuously fuse four standard Noobinis to produce Santaninis, then immediately re-use those Santaninis as inputs in the next fusion cycle to target Frogo Elfo (Rare, $67/sec) or higher. Holding multiple base-mutation Santaninis as permanent base units during the event produced diminishing returns compared to using them as stepping stones toward higher Christmas-tier Brainrots.

2

Post-event: only acquire via theft if you need the Index entry: Since the Santa's Fuse machine no longer exists, the only way to obtain Noobini Santanini is by stealing it from another player's base. The u4gm.com guide notes this is rarely worth the risk of base exposure for income purposes alone — at $11/sec, the income gain from a successful theft is minimal. The steal is only strategically worthwhile if you specifically need the unit for Index completion, a Christmas-themed collection, or a trade involving a player who requests it.

3

Its base income is adequate for event-era early-game starts: New players who joined Steal a Brainrot during the December 2025 Christmas event and received a Noobini Santanini as their first or second Brainrot benefited from its 1.00% efficiency — the highest ratio available at the $1,100 price point. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki notes this made it one of the most cost-effective income units for players starting specifically during the event, as standard non-event Common Brainrots at similar costs offered lower efficiency ratios. Base copies should still be replaced as soon as Rare-tier units become accessible.

4

Mutated copies are the only version worth retaining long-term: A default-mutation Noobini Santanini at $11/sec offers minimal late-game income contribution and should be sold once the player has access to Rare and Epic units. However, highly mutated copies — particularly YinYang ($82.50/sec) and Radioactive ($93.50/sec) — represent meaningful income for a Common-tier unit and can be retained as slot fillers while building toward higher-rarity acquisitions. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki mutation table confirms these higher-tier mutations push Noobini Santanini's income well above its base Common-tier standing.
