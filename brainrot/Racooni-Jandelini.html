What is Racooni Jandelini?

Racooni Jandelini is a Common-rarity Brainrot in the Roblox game Steal A Brainrot. It costs $1,300 and generates $12 per second in passive income. Despite being classified as Common — the lowest rarity tier in the game — Racooni Jandelini is widely acknowledged as the most valuable Common Brainrot in Steal A Brainrot, with the Fandom Wiki noting that some players have traded it for a Dragon Cannelloni, one of the game's most coveted Secret-tier Brainrots.

Its extraordinary value stems entirely from its unobtainable status. The character was introduced during the Jandel vs Sammy Admin Abuse War on August 23, 2025 — a mock conflict event staged between SpyderSammy and Jandel, the developer of Grow a Garden. It was not created as a standard Brainrot but as a deliberate joke at Jandel's expense, directly parodying his Roblox avatar and mocking the extreme rarity of the Raccoon pet in his game. According to the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki, Racooni Jandelini is the in-game enemy of Sammyini Spyderini — the Brainrot representing Sammy himself within the same event universe.

How to Obtain

Racooni Jandelini is no longer obtainable from the Red Carpet conveyor belt or any Lucky Block. It appeared on the Red Carpet only during the August 23, 2025 Admin Abuse War event, when SpyderSammy spawned large quantities of the Brainrot accompanied by the in-game broadcast: "Grow a Garden's raccoons are so rare." That event window has since closed and the character has not been re-spawned since, according to Sportskeeda's guide updated in October 2025.

As of March 2026, the only two ways to acquire Racooni Jandelini are through stealing — taking it from another player's unlocked base — or through a negotiated trade. Given its rarity, the Fandom Wiki and Sportskeeda both note that obtaining it through trade may require offering a Brainrot God or Secret-tier Brainrot in exchange. SpyderSammy can also re-spawn it at any time during future Admin Abuse events, though no further spawning of this character has been confirmed since August 2025.

Community Status

Racooni Jandelini holds a unique position in the Steal A Brainrot community as the rarest Common Brainrot in the game, despite its technical classification. The Fandom Wiki records it as the most valuable Common, with documented trades against Dragon Cannelloni. The August 23 Admin Abuse War event that created Racooni Jandelini was also historically significant: it drove Steal A Brainrot to surpass 20 million concurrent players, making it the second Roblox game ever to do so, and simultaneously pushed the entire Roblox platform to a new all-time CCU record of 47.4 million players, as documented by Wikipedia's Steal A Brainrot article.

Racooni Jandelini's design is a direct parody of Jandel's Roblox avatar, adapted into the voxel blocky style of Steal A Brainrot. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki describes it as featuring the body of a raccoon combined with the head, legs, and feet of a monkey — a hybrid specifically chosen to mock the rarity of the Raccoon pet in Grow a Garden. Looking at the in-game model and confirmed wiki descriptions:

Head

The head is that of a monkey, directly referencing Jandel's Roblox avatar. It is rounded and proportionally large, rendered in a warm tan or brown skin tone consistent with monkey coloring in the voxel style. The face has a wide expression with visible eyes. According to the Fandom Wiki, the head represents Jandel himself — the character's design is explicitly described as placing Jandel's avatar head onto a raccoon body as a joke. The Fandom Wiki also describes Racooni Jandelini as the smallest Brainrot in the game due to its raccoon-scale body proportions.

Hat and Crown

Sitting on top of the monkey head is a large red top hat — tall, wide-brimmed, and blocky in the Roblox voxel style. Around the base of the hat rests a golden crown — described by both the Fandom Wiki and the steal-a-brainrot.wiki as a yellow or gold-colored crown on the brim of the red hat. The Sportskeeda guide confirms these two accessories — the red tophat and golden crown — as the character's most visually defining features and as the primary symbols referencing Jandel's avatar.

Body

The torso belongs to a raccoon — the dark-furred, compact body of the animal in blocky form. This is the primary raccoon element of the hybrid design and the core visual joke: Jandel's avatar head placed on a raccoon body, directly mocking how difficult raccoons are to obtain in Grow a Garden.

Legs, Arms, and Feet

Despite the raccoon torso, the arms, legs, and feet are those of a monkey, as confirmed by the steal-a-brainrot.wiki. This creates a full hybrid — raccoon body, monkey limbs, monkey head — with the red tophat and golden crown completing the Jandel avatar reference. The overall silhouette is small and compact, consistent with the Fandom Wiki's claim that this is the smallest Brainrot in the game.

Racooni Jandelini holds the Common rarity classification in the in-game Index but carries the highest real trading value of any Common Brainrot in the game — driven entirely by its unobtainable, Admin Abuse–exclusive status rather than its income output.

Trading Value

The Fandom Wiki states that Racooni Jandelini is the most valuable Common Brainrot in the game, with some players trading it for a Dragon Cannelloni — a Secret-tier Brainrot. The u4gm guide notes its collector value skyrockets due to three factors: its unobtainable status post-event, its unique tie to a specific historical in-game event, and the irony of being labeled Common while having fewer existing copies than most Mythic-tier Brainrots. Sportskeeda reports that obtaining one through trade may require offering a Brainrot God or Secret unit in exchange.

The August 23 Admin Abuse War: Racooni Jandelini was created and spawned during the Jandel vs Sammy Admin Abuse War — a mock conflict event that took place on August 23, 2025. During the event, SpyderSammy flooded the game with Racooni Jandelinis while broadcasting: "Grow a Garden's raccoons are so rare." The event was historically significant — Wikipedia documents that it drove Steal A Brainrot to surpass 20 million concurrent players for the first time, and simultaneously set an all-time Roblox platform CCU record of 47.4 million players across both Steal A Brainrot and Grow A Garden combined.

Second Brainrot Based on a Roblox User: The Fandom Wiki records that Racooni Jandelini is the second Brainrot in the game based on a real Roblox user. The character's design directly parodies the Roblox avatar of Jandel — the creator of Grow a Garden and Plants vs Brainrots — placing his avatar's head on a raccoon body and dressing it in his signature red tophat and golden crown. The first Brainrot based on a Roblox user is Sammyini Spyderini, based on SpyderSammy himself, who is also listed as Racooni Jandelini's in-game enemy according to the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki.

Mocking Grow a Garden's Raccoon Rarity: The character's entire concept is a direct parody of how difficult raccoons are to obtain in Grow a Garden. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki confirms the Brainrot was created as a joke targeting both Jandel and the notorious scarcity of the Raccoon pet in his game. TikTok community posts as of early 2026 document players noting that Racooni Jandelini itself has become rarer than Dragon Cannelloni — a dark irony given the joke's original intent.

Smallest Brainrot in the Game: The official Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki states that Racooni Jandelini is the smallest Brainrot in the entire game, owing to its raccoon-scale body. Despite its compact size, its admin-exclusive origin and connection to one of Roblox's most-watched live events gives it a cultural footprint far larger than its physical model dimensions suggest.

Only Common Ever Marked Admin-Exclusive: According to the u4gm collector guide, Racooni Jandelini is the only Common Brainrot ever classified as Admin Abuse–exclusive, and the second unobtainable Common added to the game (after Pipi Corni's temporary Fuse Machine removal periods). Its Common classification is a deliberate joke — it was labeled the lowest rarity intentionally as part of the Jandel mockery, while in reality its existing copy count is lower than most Mythic-tier Brainrots.

Most Valuable Common in the Game: The Fandom Wiki directly states that Racooni Jandelini is the most valuable Common Brainrot in Steal A Brainrot. Documented trades include players exchanging it for a Dragon Cannelloni — a Secret-tier Brainrot. Sportskeeda notes that acquiring one through trade realistically requires parting with a Brainrot God or Secret-tier unit, placing its practical trade value far above its nominal Common classification.

Yin Yang and Firework Trait Copies: The Fandom Wiki's blog section documents community posts as of February 2026 asking about obtaining a Racooni Jandelini with the Yin Yang mutation and Firework trait — confirming that mutated and trait-bearing copies of this Brainrot circulate in the trading community and are among the most sought-after collector variants in the game. The Fandom Wiki also notes the character is likely to receive a stat buff in a future update.

Strategy Tips

💡 Tips for Using Racooni Jandelini

1

Never sell it — hold it as a collectible: At $12/sec, Racooni Jandelini's income output is modest for a mid-progression Common. Do not sell it for $650 cash. Its value as a historical Admin Abuse collectible far exceeds what its base income or sell price represents. Hold every copy you acquire, regardless of how many you have — mutated copies especially retain long-term collector value.

2

Use it as a high-leverage trade piece: The Fandom Wiki confirms that Racooni Jandelini has been traded for Dragon Cannelloni — a Secret-tier Brainrot. If you hold a copy, especially a mutated or trait-bearing one, use it as leverage in trades for units that would otherwise be far out of reach for your income level. Its Common classification masks a trade value that competes with Brainrot God and Secret-tier units.

3

Target it during server hopping: The most consistent way to acquire Racooni Jandelini post-event is by server hopping and checking other players' bases for undefended copies. Because many players who obtained one during the August 2025 event do not fully understand its value — or because some simply hold it in their base without extra defense — patient server hopping remains a documented acquisition method, as referenced in multiple TikTok community posts.

4

Watch for future Admin Abuse events: SpyderSammy has the ability to re-spawn Racooni Jandelini at any time during Admin Abuse events, which occur on Tuesdays and Saturdays. While the character has not been re-spawned since August 23, 2025, monitoring Admin Abuse event sessions remains the only legitimate way to obtain one fresh from the source rather than through the secondary trading market.
