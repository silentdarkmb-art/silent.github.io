Pipi Corni

⭐ Common

Pipi Corni is a small, curious member of the Pipi Family who finds comfort in the golden fields of the digital world. Like his relatives, he believes that technology is no substitute for the bond of family, wandering the game to ensure no Pipi is left behind.

💰 Base Cost $1.7k
⚡ Income / Second $14
📈 Efficiency 0.82%
🎉 Event Regular

🎯 How to Obtain Pipi Corni

📖

Overview

Pipi Corni is a rodent-type Brainrot belonging to the Pipi Family. He is primarily recognized as an OG Fuse Machine unit, as he was originally designed to be obtained by combining other units rather than being bought or found naturally.

🎨

Appearance

Following the Pipi Family design language, Pipi Corni is a hamster-like rodent hybrid. He is distinguished by yellow, corn-themed visual accents and a small, rounded silhouette that matches the aesthetic of Pipi Kiwi and Pipi Avocado.

📊

Value

Classified as a Common rarity, Pipi Corni's market value is relatively stable because he is a required unit for the Pipi Index. While his base value is low, his rarity can increase if he possesses special mutations or event-specific traits like the "Taco" trait.

🧠

Trivia

  • • The OG: He is one of the original units associated with the Fuse Machine mechanics.
  • • Family Tree: He is a core member of the Pipi Family, which includes Pipi Kiwi, Pipi Avocado, Pipi Potato, and Pi Pi Watermelon.
  • • Audio: He has a specific sound file in the game code titled PipiCorni.mp3.
  • • Unobtainable Status: At certain points in the game's history, he became impossible to get through normal means and could only be acquired via the Fuse Machine.
