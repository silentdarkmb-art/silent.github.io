Tric Trac Baraboom

Tric Trac Baraboom is a Rare Brainrot in Steal a Brainrot — the 7th Rare unit in the in-game Brainrot Index, positioned above Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur and below Cupcake Koala. It costs $9,000 and generates $65 per second, making it the highest-earning standard Red Carpet Rare in the game. Created by TikTok user @__chenesacc__ on February 28, 2025, the character depicts a giant zucchini-like plant creature with two stubby legs, no arms, a long snout, and a bush or leaf crown concealing the top of its head. It was introduced in Update 5 and is documented by the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki as the son of Brr Brr Patapim and father of Los Triktrackitos. The Fandom Wiki records it as possibly related to Brr Brr Patapim, and notes it was previously used as a trading ingredient for Doi Doi Do through the Brainrot Trader.

What is Tric Trac Baraboom?

Tric Trac Baraboom is a Rare-tier Brainrot in Steal a Brainrot — the 7th and final standard Red Carpet Rare in the in-game Brainrot Index, placed directly above Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur ($55/sec). Confirmed by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki, steal-a-brainrot.wiki, and the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki, it costs $9,000 and generates $65 per second — the highest income of any standard Red Carpet Rare Brainrot in the game. The character is based on an Italian Brainrot created by TikTok user @__chenesacc__ on February 28, 2025, and was introduced to Steal a Brainrot in Update 5. It depicts a giant zucchini-like plant creature with two legs, no arms, a long snout, and a leafy bush crown atop its head. The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki documents it as the son of Brr Brr Patapim and the father of Los Triktrackitos.

The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki notes that Tric Trac Baraboom might be related to Brr Brr Patapim — the Epic-tier Brainrot required for the 2nd Rebirth. The character carries no Rebirth requirement of its own and no Ritual condition. The Fandom Wiki also documents that Tric Trac Baraboom was previously used as a trading ingredient through the Brainrot Trader (active November 15 to December 6, 2025) — serving as a component in multiple trade recipes including as a standalone alternative for crafting Cupcake Koala ($60/s), and as part of ingredient pools producing higher-income units. Four Tric Trac Barabooms were confirmed as one combination for crafting a $260/sec unit during the Trader's operation.

How to Obtain

Tric Trac Baraboom is obtainable exclusively through the Red Carpet — the central conveyor belt in Steal a Brainrot where Brainrots of all rarities pass through and can be purchased by any player for $9,000. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and steal-a-brainrot.wiki both confirm there is no alternative acquisition route: it cannot be obtained through Fuse Machines, Fishing Events, Lucky Blocks, or active event systems. Based on the large-scale community survey documented in the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki — tracking 1,515 Red Carpet Brainrot appearances — Tric Trac Baraboom appeared 44 times, yielding an observed spawn rate of approximately 2.9% per carpet cycle.

This spawn rate is the second-lowest among the standard Red Carpet Rares after Boneca Ambalabu (2.83%), making Tric Trac Baraboom a moderately patient acquisition for players watching the carpet. Players purchase it directly upon appearance, or acquire a copy by stealing from another player's unlocked base — the two standard methods documented by the Fandom Wiki and u4gm.com. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki classifies it as obtainable without extended farming despite the lower spawn probability, noting it remains a permanent fixture of the Red Carpet pool.

Meme / Lore Origin

Tric Trac Baraboom was created by TikTok user @__chenesacc__ on February 28, 2025 as part of the Italian Brainrot meme wave. The character accumulated over 10 million TikTok posts referencing it across social media platforms, and is documented by the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki as initially going unnoticed at the beginning of the Brainrot craze — similar to Orangutini Ananasini and Triki Traka Telas — before gaining significant traction that led to its widespread appearance across Roblox games, fan art, and remixes. The Miraheze Wiki records it as existing in two distinct forms: the original forest/vine-covered version resembling Brr Brr Patapim with a more human nose and slender legs, and the widely-recognized zucchini version featuring a giant zucchini plant body with large feet, beady eyes, and a green leaf crown.

The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki provides the full lore: the character's actual name is Barabum (also spelled Baraboom), while "Tric Trac" refers to the sound made by a blue squirrel named Rum who became trapped inside a woolen cloak that Barabum found one day. After the squirrel refused to leave, Barabum sought help from a wizard named Mago Pasticciù, who instructed him to clap his hands. The clap produced a "boom" that launched Rum into the sky — explaining the "baraboom" portion of the name. The Miraheze Wiki notes the words "tric" and "trac" are onomatopoeias referencing branches cracking, consistent with the character's forest and plant-based design.

Community Status

Tric Trac Baraboom holds a recognized position in the Steal A Brainrot community as the highest-earning standard Red Carpet Rare — the natural endpoint of the Red Carpet Rare acquisition chain before players transition to non-carpet Rares like Cupcake Koala and Frogo Elfo, and eventually to Epic-tier units. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki and u4gm.com both identify it as a priority mid-game acquisition, citing its $65/sec income as a strong bridge unit between the Rare tier and the lower Epic Brainrots. The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki records the character as having grown from obscurity at the start of the Brainrot craze into a recognized fan-favorite — one of the few Rare Brainrots to inspire direct counterparts, remixes, and family members within the Italian Brainrot lore ecosystem through its connection to Brr Brr Patapim.

Tric Trac Baraboom's in-game Roblox voxel model is based on the Italian Brainrot character's widely-known zucchini version — an anthropomorphic plant creature adapted from the original meme imagery created by @__chenesacc__. The full design is confirmed by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki, steal-a-brainrot.wiki, the u4gm.com guide, the Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki, and the uploaded in-game screenshot provided for reference. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki describes the overall character as resembling a giant zucchini creature — blending organic plant characteristics with an absurdist anthropomorphic figure consistent with Italian Brainrot visual language.

Body — Zucchini Plant Form

The main torso of Tric Trac Baraboom is a large, rectangular plant-like body rendered in Roblox's voxel block format. The Fandom Wiki and steal-a-brainrot.wiki both describe the body as a giant zucchini-like plant, rendered in a green and tan/sandy color palette visible in the uploaded in-game screenshot. The surface of the body appears with a textured pattern of crosshatch or lattice-like markings — consistent with the voxel interpretation of the zucchini skin seen in the original meme. The body is tall and roughly rectangular from a front-facing view, narrowing slightly toward the lower section where the legs connect. The u4gm.com guide notes the overall form has a "pixelated or handcrafted quality that fits within the game's whimsical style."

Head and Leaf Crown

The upper portion of Tric Trac Baraboom features a head concealed by a bush or crowned with green leaves — documented by the Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki and steal-a-brainrot.wiki as a defining visual element. As visible in the uploaded in-game screenshot, the top of the character is covered with a cluster of leafy fronds that fan outward in multiple directions, consistent with palm-leaf or tropical plant foliage. The Miraheze Wiki describes the head as "concealed by a bush," while the steal-a-brainrot.wiki describes it as "topped with leafy fronds resembling palm leaves that fan out from its head." The overall effect is that the character appears to have no visible upper head — just a mass of green leaves where the skull would be.

Snout

The most recognizable facial feature of Tric Trac Baraboom is its long, prominent snout — described by the u4gm.com guide as reptilian or resembling "a crocodile's or anteater's" profile. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki confirms the snout is long relative to the body and is "dotted with square patterns reminiscent of its blocky skin texture." Visible in the uploaded screenshot, the snout protrudes significantly forward from the base of the leaf crown, tapering toward the tip. The Fandom Wiki describes it as a "big snout" in its character summary. The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki's lore describes the character as having "a long nose like a zucchini, kinda mole, kinda tangerine" — a reference that informs the snout's elongated, botanical appearance.

Legs — No Arms

Tric Trac Baraboom walks on two legs — thick, blocky limbs that extend from the base of the torso. The Fandom Wiki explicitly states the character has no arms, which is confirmed by the uploaded screenshot and distinguishes it from most other anthropomorphic Brainrots. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki describes the legs as "two stubby legs" with large feet, while the Miraheze Wiki's iconic version description notes "big feet." In the uploaded screenshot, the legs and feet are rendered in a warm tan/sandy-brown coloration that contrasts with the darker green of the upper body, and the feet are flat and wide relative to the leg width. The u4gm.com guide notes the limb design as "root-like" in some visual representations, consistent with the character's dual forest and plant-based identity.

Tric Trac Baraboom holds the top position in the standard Red Carpet Rare income bracket, generating $65 per second from a base cost of $9,000 — an income-to-cost efficiency of approximately 0.72%. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki identifies it as the definitive endpoint of the Red Carpet Rare chain, describing its $65/sec as "one of the stronger mid-range income sources before stepping into Epic-tier Brainrots." The u4gm.com guide notes it represents solid efficiency for its price point, providing a meaningful passive income buffer while players accumulate the cash needed to pursue Epic-tier Brainrots starting at $10,000+.

Trading Value

Tric Trac Baraboom holds moderate to strong trading value within the Rare tier — the highest of any standard Red Carpet Rare. The u4gm.com guide notes that as the top-income standard Rare, it consistently attracts interest from mid-game players building toward Epic-tier acquisition targets who want to maximize their passive income floor. Unlike lower Rares, it carries no Rebirth gate requirement, meaning its demand comes from income optimization and collector interest rather than progression necessity. The Fandom Wiki's Brainrot Trader documentation confirms Tric Trac Baraboom was a commonly-used ingredient during the Trader's operational window — its value in those trade recipes reflected its position as the ceiling Rare. Mutated copies carry strong trading interest: Bloodrot ($130/sec), Radioactive ($552.50/sec), and Rainbow/Divine ($650/sec) position a mutated copy firmly in the income range of base-level Epic Brainrots.

Actual Name is Barabum — "Tric Trac" is the Squirrel's Sound: The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki and steal-a-brainrot.wiki both document that the character's real name within the Italian Brainrot lore is Barabum (also spelled Baraboom), not Tric Trac Baraboom. The prefix "Tric Trac" is not the creature's name — it is the onomatopoeic sound made by a blue squirrel named Rum who became trapped inside a woolen cloak that Barabum found one day. The Miraheze Wiki further notes that "tric" and "trac" are onomatopoeias referencing branches cracking, consistent with the character's forest habitat and plant-based body.

Son of Brr Brr Patapim — Father of Los Triktrackitos: The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki explicitly documents Tric Trac Baraboom as the son of Brr Brr Patapim (the Epic-tier Brainrot required for the 2nd Rebirth in Steal a Brainrot) and as the father of Los Triktrackitos. The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki independently records that Tric Trac Baraboom "might be related to Brr Brr Patapim." The Miraheze Wiki also notes the character was inspired by Brr Brr Patapim, inheriting similar forest-based DNA while developing its own distinct zucchini-plant identity and dancing personality.

Created by @__chenesacc__ on February 28, 2025 — 10M+ TikTok Posts: The Italian Brainrot Miraheze Wiki documents that Tric Trac Baraboom was created by TikTok user @__chenesacc__ on February 28, 2025, in collaboration with @reisonbs. The Miraheze Wiki records the character as accumulating over 10 million TikTok posts referencing it across the platform, establishing it as one of the most widely-referenced Italian Brainrot characters. It is the same creator responsible for Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur (@__chenesac__, slightly different handle spelling documented by different sources), making @__chenesacc__ one of the most productive creators represented in the game's Rare tier.

Added in Update 5 — One of Six New Brainrots: The steal-a-brainrot.wiki confirms that Tric Trac Baraboom was introduced to Steal a Brainrot as part of Update 5, added alongside five other new Brainrots in the same content drop. The Fandom Wiki records this directly on its character page. Update 5 represented an expansion of the Rare tier with multiple new units during a period of rapid player growth for the game, and Tric Trac Baraboom's addition as the new top-income standard Red Carpet Rare made it an immediate acquisition target for mid-game players at the time of its release.

Used as Brainrot Trader Ingredient — 4 Copies for $260/sec Unit: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki's Brainrot Trader documentation confirms that Tric Trac Baraboom was used as a crafting ingredient during the Brainrot Trader's operational window (November 15 to December 6, 2025). Specifically: one Tric Trac Baraboom was an alternative requirement for Cupcake Koala ($60/s); two Tric Trac Barabooms combined with two Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahurs could produce a $235/sec unit; and four Tric Trac Barabooms were one of the confirmed combinations for obtaining a $260/sec unit. This made Tric Trac Baraboom a frequently consumed Rare ingredient during the Trader's limited operation period.

Was Traded for Doi Doi Do — An Epic From the Brainrot Trader: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki records a specific trivia note: Tric Trac Baraboom was used to trade for Doi Doi Do, an Epic-tier Brainrot that was obtainable through the Brainrot Trader. This is one of the documented historical trade uses of Tric Trac Baraboom recorded by the Fandom Wiki, reflecting the unit's role as a high-value Rare trade asset during the Brainrot Trader's active period. Doi Doi Do is no longer obtainable through the Trader as the machine was removed on December 6, 2025.

Spawn Rate — 2.9% (44/1,515) — Second-Lowest Among Red Carpet Rares: The Steal A Brainrot Fandom Wiki's community tracking data — based on 1,515 observed Red Carpet appearances — recorded Tric Trac Baraboom appearing 44 times, yielding an approximate spawn rate of 2.9%. This is the second-lowest spawn rate among the standard Red Carpet Rares, after Boneca Ambalabu (2.83%). The steal-a-brainrot.wiki classifies it as attainable without extended farming despite the lower rate, noting that consistent active play sessions are sufficient to secure it. The u4gm.com guide describes the spawn rate as meaning players need "a bit of patience or luck" but that it is "not extremely difficult to find."

Strategy Tips

💡 Tips for Using Tric Trac Baraboom

1

Prioritize it as the final Red Carpet Rare before transitioning to Epic: Tric Trac Baraboom is the highest-income standard Red Carpet Rare at $65/sec, and the steal-a-brainrot.wiki identifies it as the natural endpoint of the carpet Rare chain. Once you have secured Boneca Ambalabu, Cacto Hipopotamo, and Ta Ta Ta Ta Sahur, redirect all carpet-watching effort toward Tric Trac Baraboom before spending toward Epic-tier units. Its $9,000 cost is the highest of the standard Rares, so do not rush it at the expense of leaving earlier Red Carpet Rares uncollected — each prior Rare step-up builds the income base needed to afford the $9,000 purchase when it appears.

2

Target Bloodrot during the Bloodmoon Event for Epic-level output: A Bloodrot-mutated Tric Trac Baraboom generates $130/sec — a figure that exceeds several base Epic Brainrots. Because the Bloodrot mutation is obtained through the Bloodmoon Event rather than purchased directly, this is among the most income-efficient upgrades achievable at Rare rarity without Epic-tier expenditure. The steal-a-brainrot.wiki specifically identifies this as a priority Bloodmoon target. During every Bloodmoon Event, expose your Tric Trac Baraboom first in the mutation window, alongside Boneca Ambalabu ($80/sec Bloodrot) and Cacto Hipopotamo ($100/sec Bloodrot), to maximize the total Rare-tier income gain from a single event.

3

Sell default-mutation extras once you have stronger units: Because Tric Trac Baraboom carries no Rebirth requirement, there is no functional reason to retain multiple base-mutation copies once your base has access to Epic-tier or mutated Rare units that outperform it. The $4,500 sell value of each default copy is best redirected toward Cappuccino Assassino ($75/sec, the first Epic) or higher. Retain copies only if they carry meaningful mutations — Diamond ($97.50/sec), Lava ($390/sec), Radioactive ($552.50/sec) — that deliver genuine sustained income contribution above a slot's opportunity cost.

4

Use it as a strong trade anchor in Rare-tier trades: As the highest-income standard Red Carpet Rare, Tric Trac Baraboom carries the most consistent trade leverage of any unit in the carpet Rare pool. Players who have not yet secured it will actively trade for it, and the u4gm.com guide notes its $65/sec income makes it one of the "more popular Rare Brainrots to collect" due to its combination of visual distinctiveness and income output. A base-mutation copy can be leveraged for multiple lower-tier Rares in a bundle trade, while mutated copies — particularly Radioactive ($552.50/sec) and Rainbow/Divine ($650/sec) — command trade interest from a wide range of mid-to-late-game players seeking Rare-tier income upgrades.
