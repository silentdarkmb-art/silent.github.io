What is Noobini Pizzanini?

Noobini Pizzanini is a Common-rarity Brainrot in the Roblox game Steal A Brainrot. With a base cost of only $25 and a reliable $1/sec passive income, it serves as the perfect entry point for new players. Its iconic noob-pizza hybrid design — featuring the classic Roblox noob merged with a cheesy pizza slice — makes it instantly recognizable and a fan favorite.

Noobini Pizzanini plays a crucial role in early-game progression: its $1/sec output may seem small, but its 4% efficiency rating is among the highest in the game. This makes it an excellent investment for beginners learning the ropes. As a Common unit, it has a high spawn chance on the Red Carpet, ensuring new players can quickly start building their collection.

How to Obtain

Noobini Pizzanini is obtainable through two verified methods:

1. Red Carpet: Noobini Pizzanini spawns frequently on the Red Carpet conveyor belt at the center of the map. As a Common-rarity unit, it has one of the highest appearance rates in the game. Players can purchase it directly for just $25 in in-game cash when it appears — making it one of the most accessible brainrots for beginners.

2. Stealing: As a core game mechanic, players can also acquire Noobini Pizzanini by raiding other users' bases and stealing it from an unlocked base. While less reliable than the Red Carpet for Common units, this method remains viable and adds a fun competitive element to acquisition.
