🎉 About Events

Steal A Brainrots regularly hosts limited-time events where players can earn exclusive Brainrots, special currency, and unique rewards.

📅 How to Participate

  • Log in during the event period to automatically qualify.
  • Complete event-specific tasks or challenges to earn rewards.
  • Redeem event codes posted on developer socials for bonus items.
  • Check BLOXBURST for the latest event codes before they expire.

💡 Event Tips

  • Log in every day during events — daily rewards stack up fast.
  • Event Brainrots are usually limited and won't come back after the event ends.
  • Follow the developer's socials to know when events start.

🔬 What Are Mutations?

Mutations are special upgrades that can be applied to your Brainrots to enhance their stats and abilities.

🧬 Types of Mutations

  • Speed Mutation — Increases movement speed for faster stealing.
  • Power Mutation — Boosts attack and stealing strength.
  • Shield Mutation — Adds a protective layer to your Brainrots.
  • Luck Mutation — Increases the chance of stealing rare Brainrots.
  • Aura Mutation — Rare cosmetic + stat boost combined.

💡 Mutation Tips

  • Apply mutations to your rarest Brainrots first for maximum impact.
  • Luck mutations are great for farming rare Brainrots efficiently.
  • Some mutations are only available during specific events.

🔁 What Is Rebirth?

Rebirth is a prestige mechanic that allows you to reset your progress in exchange for permanent bonuses and exclusive rewards.

✅ Rebirth Requirements

  • Reach the required level or Brainrot collection threshold.
  • Have enough in-game currency to initiate the rebirth.
  • Click the Rebirth button in the main menu.
  • Confirm the rebirth — your progress resets but bonuses are permanent.

🎁 Rebirth Rewards

  • Rebirth Multiplier — Earn more currency per steal after rebirth.
  • Exclusive Brainrots — Some Brainrots are only available after rebirthing.
  • Rebirth Badge — Show off your rebirth count to other players.
  • Bonus Starting Stats — Start stronger each time you rebirth.

💡 Rebirth Tips

  • Don't rebirth too early — make sure you have strong Brainrots first.
  • Use all active codes before rebirthing to maximize pre-rebirth rewards.
  • Rebirthing during an event can reward you with exclusive event items.
