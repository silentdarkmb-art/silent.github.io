How to Redeem Roblox Promo Codes

The official 5-step guide to claiming free items and rewards on Roblox. Works on all platforms in 2026.

📅 Updated: Feb 22, 2026
✅ Type: Official Guide
⏱ Read time: ~3 min

📋 Quick Navigation

Step-by-Step

🖥️ Redeem on PC / Browser

01
Open roblox.com/redeem

Navigate to the official promo code redemption page on any desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, etc.).

Open Redeem Page →
02
Log In to Your Account

Click "Log In" at the top right if you're not already signed in. Rewards go to the logged-in account.

03
Paste Your Promo Code

Use Ctrl+V to paste your code into the input field. Avoid typing manually to prevent typos.

04
Click the Redeem Button

Press the green Redeem button. Make sure no extra spaces are before or after the code.

05
Check Your Avatar Editor

A success message confirms your reward. Find it in Avatar Editor → Wardrobe within minutes.

📱 Redeem on Mobile Browser

01
Copy Your Code First

Long press the code on this page and tap "Copy" — this avoids manual typing errors on small keyboards.

02
Open Mobile Browser

Open Safari, Chrome, or any browser on your phone. Go to roblox.com/redeem.

Open Redeem Page →
03
Log In if Needed

Tap "Log In" and enter your credentials. Make sure you're on the correct account.

04
Long Press → Paste Code

Tap the input field, then long press and select Paste to enter your code without typos.

05
Tap Redeem & Check Inventory

Tap the Redeem button. Check your Avatar in the Roblox app to confirm the reward arrived.

📲 Redeem via Roblox App

01
Note: Promo Codes ≠ In-App

Roblox promo codes cannot be redeemed inside the app directly — you must use a browser at roblox.com/redeem.

02
Open Your Phone Browser

Switch to Safari or Chrome, go to roblox.com/redeem, and log in with the same account as your app.

Open Redeem Page →
03
Paste & Redeem

Paste your code into the input and tap Redeem. The reward syncs to your account automatically.

04
Back to the App

Open the Roblox app, go to Avatar tab. Your new item should appear in your wardrobe.

05
Not Showing? Refresh!

If the item doesn't appear, fully close and reopen the Roblox app or log out and back in.

Important Notes

✅ What to Know

  • 🔑Most promo codes can only be redeemed once per account.
  • 📱Works on browser — PC, mobile, and tablet.
  • 🎁Rewards appear in Avatar Editor or your Inventory.
  • ⏰Codes are time-limited — redeem as soon as possible.
  • 📋Always copy-paste codes to avoid typos.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • ❌Trying to redeem game-specific codes on roblox.com/redeem.
  • ❌Redeeming while logged out — no reward will be granted.
  • ❌Typing codes manually — always copy-paste to prevent errors.
  • ❌Using an already-redeemed code — each code is one-time only.
