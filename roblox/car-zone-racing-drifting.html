How to Redeem Codes

  1. Launch Car Zone on Roblox and wait for the game to fully load.
  2. Click the MENU button at the top right corner of the screen.
  3. Click MAIN MENU from the dropdown, then click the Twitter/X bird icon at the top right of the Main Menu.
  4. Type or paste your code exactly into the ENTER CODES field — codes are case-sensitive.
  5. Click Redeem to claim your free Cash reward instantly.
💬 If the code doesn't work, feel free to let us know in the comments so we can update the list!
🏎️ About the Game

About Car Zone Racing & Drifting

Developed by CODIV Studio • Roblox Open World Racing Simulator

Car Zone Racing & Drifting is an open-world racing simulator on Roblox developed by CODIV Studio. Players explore a large realistic map, collect and customize over 100 cars, and race with friends across varied terrain. Driving earns in-game Cash, which funds new car purchases and upgrades. Codes are case-sensitive and released through official channels alongside major updates and visit milestones.

🚗 Gameplay

Drive around the open world to earn Cash passively, then spend it on new cars, performance parts, and cosmetic upgrades. Players can invite friends for a bonus earnings multiplier — each friend invited adds 5% to your earnings rate up to 25%. Exploring the map and testing different vehicles across its varied terrain is the core loop.

🎁 Code Drops

CODIV Studio releases codes alongside major game updates, when the game hits likes and visit milestones, and on special occasions. Codes are shared in the official Discord server and the CODIV Studio Roblox group. All codes are case-sensitive — capitalization must match exactly — and expire without advance notice.

⚠️ Troubleshoot

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues & how to fix them fast
🗺️

Wrong Menu Path

The code redemption field in Car Zone is not on the main gameplay screen. You need to click MENU at the top right, then MAIN MENU, then the bird icon at the top right of the Main Menu screen. Players who look for a codes button on the HUD directly will not find it — it is only accessible through this specific path.

🔡

Case-Sensitive Typo

Car Zone codes are fully case-sensitive. A single wrong capital or lowercase letter causes the Redeem button to reject the code. Always copy and paste directly from BLOXBURST rather than typing manually — this eliminates input errors entirely, including the special characters found in some older codes.

🔄

Already Redeemed

Each code is one-time use per Roblox account. If the game returns a message indicating the code was already used, that Cash reward was successfully collected at a prior point — the code worked, it just cannot be claimed twice on the same account.

📋

Conflict Between Sources

Some codes appear active on one tracker but expired on another. BLOXBURST cross-checks multiple major sources before listing any code as active — if a code appears expired or is in a "Check" status on any source, we move it to the expired section immediately to protect you from wasted attempts.

🔧

Server Not Updated

Codes released alongside a new update can fail on older servers still running a prior build. Leaving and rejoining Car Zone places you on a fresh server with the current version — this resolves the issue in most cases where a recently released code refuses to register.

⏰

Code Has Expired

Car Zone codes expire without advance notice. Attempting an expired code returns an error even if it still appears on some sites as active. BLOXBURST moves any code flagged as expired or in "Check" status immediately — always use the confirmed active list at the top of this page to avoid failed redemption attempts.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for Car Zone

Build your garage faster and squeeze the most out of every code
01
Redeem all active codes before spending any Cash

All current active codes award Cash directly. Redeeming everything before making any purchases gives you the maximum wallet balance for your first car or upgrade decision — a clean stack of Cash from codes means better buying options from the very start of your session.

02
Invite friends to boost your earnings rate

Car Zone awards a 5% earnings bonus for each friend invited, stacking up to 25% at five friends. Filling that friend bonus before grinding for a new car means every minute of driving earns more Cash — the multiplier applies passively and stacks with any other income modifiers.

03
Keep driving between purchases to let Cash accumulate

Cash in Car Zone is earned by driving — the longer you stay on the road without stopping to spend, the larger your reserve grows. Setting a target amount before each purchase and driving straight toward it prevents the impulse buys that leave you short for the next car tier.

04
Upgrade performance before cosmetics for better race results

Cosmetic modifications change how your car looks but do not affect speed or handling. Spending available Cash on performance parts first makes races and open-world driving more competitive — cosmetics can wait until your car performs well enough to justify the investment.

05
Explore the full map to find all driving zones and shortcuts

Car Zone features a large open world with multiple terrain types and areas to discover. Different zones can offer varied driving challenges and visibility on the road — familiarizing yourself with the map layout early helps you find better grinding routes and race lines before competing against other players.

06
Check back at milestone intervals for new codes

The naming pattern of past codes shows consistent releases tied to likes and visit counts. Watching the game's current stats and comparing them against previous thresholds lets you anticipate the next code drop — this way you are ready to redeem the moment a new one goes live rather than finding it days later.
