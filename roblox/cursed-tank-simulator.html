🛡️ About the Game

About Cursed Tank Simulator

Developed by tanmk • Roblox PvP Tank Builder

Cursed Tank Simulator is a Roblox PvP experience developed by tanmk. Players construct custom tanks by mixing and matching modules — hull, turret, gun, tracks, and more — then deploy their machines against other players in live combat. Every part combination changes the tank's stats and behavior, enabling a wide range of builds from fast scout tanks to heavily armored siege machines. Defeating enemies and completing in-game missions earns Gold, Crates, and Materials used to acquire new parts. The game operates on a no-pay-to-win philosophy and supports mobile and tablet play. All codes are case-sensitive.

⚠️ Troubleshoot

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues & how to fix them fast
🔤

Code Includes a Space or Punctuation

Several Cursed Tank Simulator codes contain spaces or special characters that are part of the code and must be typed exactly — W DavadMouse2 has a leading space after "W", Battle Rating 5.0 has spaces between all words and a decimal point, and yea? ends with a question mark. Typing these without the spaces or punctuation causes the Check button to return "invalid code" even though the code is working and active. Always copy and paste directly from BLOXBURST to capture the full formatting of each code string.

🔡

Case-Sensitive Typo

Cursed Tank Simulator codes are fully case-sensitive. Codes like LastQNA2026 (mixed with caps), HappyBdayBlu (camelCase), WeAreSoBack (camelCase), and LockedInAlien (mixed) all require exact capitalisation. A single wrong capital or lowercase letter will cause the Check button to reject the code. Copy and paste each code directly from BLOXBURST rather than typing manually, and check the entry field for any accidental leading or trailing spaces before hitting Check.

🔁

Already Redeemed

Each code in Cursed Tank Simulator is single-use per Roblox account. If the game returns a "code already redeemed" message, the rewards were added to your account in a previous session and cannot be claimed again. With 20 active codes currently available, check whether you are attempting to re-enter a code you have already claimed — especially if you used a batch of codes from an older list that may overlap with the current active set.

📋

Conflict Between Sources

Six codes in the expired section — including VALENTINES_CLOUD26, Finals 2025, Advanced Crew Escape Suit, pleach, LOOOL, and 67 — appear as active on some trackers but are absent from multiple other major verified sources. BLOXBURST moves any code that is not confirmed across at least two independent major sources to the expired section immediately, protecting you from attempting codes that have likely expired but haven't been cleared from every tracker yet.

🔧

Outdated Server After a New Update

Codes released with a new Cursed Tank Simulator update may fail on servers still running the previous build. If a confirmed active code does not register on first attempt — particularly on the day of an update announcement — exit the game entirely and rejoin. Landing in a server running the current version typically resolves the issue immediately. This is most common in the first hour after a patch while servers update asynchronously across the player pool.

⬆️

Codes Button Location

The Codes button in Cursed Tank Simulator is located at the top right of the garage screen, next to the Shop button — not inside the Shop panel itself. Players who click Shop instead of Codes will not see a code entry field. Click specifically on the Codes button (separate from Shop), type or paste your code into the text box that appears, then press the green Check button to complete the redemption.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for Cursed Tank Simulator

Build a stronger tank faster with smarter module choices and code use
01
Redeem all 20 active codes before entering your first match

All 20 active codes add Gold, Tokens, Crates, or Materials directly to your account with no prerequisites. Claiming everything — especially W DavadMouse2 (670 of each Crate), HappyBdayBlu and HappyBdayHanna (75,000 Gold + 20 Mystical Crates each), and Jolly (multi-material bundle) — before your first match gives you a resource base that would normally take dozens of combat rounds to accumulate. Open your Crates immediately after to expand your available module pool before designing your first build.

02
Open Crate code rewards before building your first tank

Several active codes award Crates directly — W DavadMouse2 alone grants 670 of every Crate type, and HappyBdayBlu and HappyBdayHanna each add 20 Mystical Crates. Opening all of these before designing your starting build gives you a much wider module selection to work with immediately. A first build constructed from a large module pool produces a significantly stronger tank than one assembled from only the default starter parts — the early Crate pool from codes alone can unlock rare modules that would otherwise take hours of combat to acquire.

03
Press F1 or tap HELP to learn module interactions before your first match

Cursed Tank Simulator has an in-game help system accessible by pressing F1 on keyboard or tapping the HELP button. With 20 active codes giving you a large starting module inventory, spending a few minutes in the garage reviewing how hull, turret, gun, and track combinations interact helps you turn that resource advantage into an effective first build. Understanding which module types contribute most to your preferred playstyle — whether high mobility, strong armor, or maximum firepower — lets you use your code-funded Gold on the right shop purchases from the start.

04
Spend Tokens last — prioritize Gold purchases first from the shop

Tokens are scarcer than Gold in normal play, and the 18 free Tokens from active codes represent a significant premium currency bonus. Before spending any Tokens, exhaust all Gold-funded purchases first — most module acquisitions are achievable with Gold alone, and Tokens are best reserved for items only available through the Token-exclusive sections of the shop. The LastQNA2026 and compensate codes each grant 6 Tokens, with HappyBdayBlu and HappyBdayHanna adding 3 each — saving these for intentional high-value purchases extends their impact considerably.

05
Use crafting material codes to upgrade existing modules rather than replace them

Codes like Jolly, code, and the material portions of other active codes award crafting resources including Aluminum, Coal, Iron Ore, Titanium, and Chromium. These Materials are used in the crafting system to upgrade or modify existing modules rather than purchasing new ones outright. Applying Material rewards to your best current modules — rather than holding them — is more efficient than spending Gold on replacements that Materials could improve at a lower cost. Higher-grade materials like Chromium and Titanium should be prioritized for your most-used modules first.

06
Watch for Roblox Premium benefits — 50% bonus on Gold, XP, and Crate rewards

Cursed Tank Simulator grants Roblox Premium subscribers a 50% bonus on Gold earned in matches, XP gained, and Crate rewards received. Combined with the large Gold and Crate totals from active codes, Premium subscribers start from a significantly higher resource baseline and continue earning at a faster rate per match. If you already have Roblox Premium, ensuring you are logged in before redeeming codes or entering matches maximizes the total value of every reward — including the Crate drops and Gold rewards from the active code pool.
