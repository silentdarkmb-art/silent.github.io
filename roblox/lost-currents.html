✨ About the Game

About Lost Currents

Developed by flMindworks • Co-op deep sea survival on Roblox

Lost Currents is a co-op survival and exploration game on Roblox, developed by flMindworks and currently in Alpha. Players sail a boat across the AZR Sea with a crew of friends, where one player dons an old diving helmet and descends into the dark ocean depths in search of gold bars — while the others remain on the boat watching for threats from above and below. The game blends teamwork, tension, and mystery as players work together to survive predators lurking in the water, unravel the fate of the missing divers, and push deeper into increasingly dangerous ocean zones. Currently on Update v0.10.3, Lost Currents features multiple boat types including a Sailboat, a crafting system with items like the Defibrillator for reviving teammates, a class system unlocked with Gold, and NPC trader Rustbeard who exchanges salvaged items for Cash. The game supports both co-op expeditions and solo play introduced in a dedicated update.

🤿 Gameplay

The core gameplay loop centers on cooperative diving expeditions. One player equips the diving helmet by pressing E near it — a second player must lower them into the water by pulling the red lever on the boat. Underwater, the diver collects gold bars and treasures while managing a limited air supply. Pressing Q signals the surface crew to pull the diver back up. Sharks patrol the depths and can kill unequipped divers instantly — a Harpoon Gun is essential for safe diving. Pirates can attack the boat from the surface, requiring the crew to stay armed. Collected Gold is used to unlock new Classes that provide distinct perks including the Diver class (1.5x air capacity) and seasonal limited classes like the Drowner and Giftbringer.

🎁 Code Drops

flMindworks releases Lost Currents codes tied to major updates, visit milestones, community goals, and server downtime compensation. Past codes have been named directly after the content they accompanied — HARPOONGUN, LEVIATHAN, ISLANDS, and BIOLUMINESCENT — reflecting specific game features or updates. Codes are announced through the official Lost Currents Discord server and the flMindworks Roblox group. All codes reward Gold — the currency used to unlock classes — making every active code a direct shortcut toward expanding your available loadout options before your next dive.

What Do Codes Give You?

Free Gold to unlock new classes and gear for your diving expeditions
💰

Gold

Gold is the primary progression currency in Lost Currents and the only reward awarded through codes. It is spent on unlocking new Classes that give your diver or crew member distinct combat and survival advantages — including the Diver class with 1.5x air capacity for longer underwater runs. Without enough Gold, players are locked to the default class with no perks, making code-awarded Gold one of the most direct progression boosts available. Newer codes have awarded up to 50 Gold per redemption, while earlier codes like LOSTCURRENTSALPHA and FREEGOLD each awarded 20 Gold — a meaningful jump in class unlock currency from a single free code.

Class Unlocks

While codes award Gold rather than classes directly, each Gold reward from a code brings you measurably closer to unlocking the next class in the roster. Classes in Lost Currents define your role on the boat — the Diver class extends your air supply for deeper runs, while support and combat classes give the surface crew better tools for defending against sharks and pirates. Limited seasonal classes like the Drowner (Halloween 2025) and Giftbringer (Christmas 2025) were available only during their event windows, making the standard Gold-unlockable classes the primary progression target for most players outside of event seasons.

Boat & Gear Upgrades

Gold accumulated through codes and gameplay funds upgrades at the boat shop — including new boat types like the Sailboat that introduces an alternative sail-based movement mechanic. Shop upgrades also include weapons and equipment that dramatically improve survival rates on deeper dives, such as the Harpoon Gun for fending off sharks underwater and the Assault Rifle for repelling pirate attacks from the surface. Code-awarded Gold reduces the grind required to reach these critical defensive upgrades, shortening the window between a new player's first dive and their first fully equipped expedition into the more dangerous ocean zones.

Craftable Items

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues & how to fix them fast

Code Expired After Latest Update

Lost Currents codes are closely tied to specific updates and milestones — once the next update launches, codes from previous patches can be deactivated without any announcement. With over 24 expired codes in the game's Alpha history, the turnover rate is high relative to the total code pool. BLOXBURST moves any code confirmed expired on any verified source to the expired section immediately, and treats all "Check" status codes as expired per our conflict resolution policy — protecting you from wasting time entering codes that no longer work.

🔡

Spelling Error — Codes Are Case-Sensitive

Lost Currents codes are case-sensitive — entering a code in the wrong capitalization returns an invalid error even when every other character is correct. Extra spaces before or after the code text also cause failures that are easy to miss when typing manually. Always paste directly from BLOXBURST using the Copy button on each code card rather than typing codes manually. This eliminates both capitalization errors and spacing issues simultaneously, which are the most common sources of invalid code errors for correctly-timed redemptions.

🔁

Already Redeemed

Each Lost Currents code is single-use per Roblox account. If the game indicates the code has already been claimed, the Gold was already added to your account in a previous session. With Lost Currents' growing code history across its Alpha development, it is easy to lose track of which codes were redeemed across multiple play sessions. Checking your current Gold balance against your expected total is the fastest way to confirm whether an "already redeemed" error is accurate before reporting the code as broken.

🌊

Code Only Valid During Specific Event

Some Lost Currents codes have been tied to specific seasonal events — including the Halloween 2025 and Christmas 2025 event windows that introduced limited classes like the Drowner and Giftbringer. Event-tied codes like SPOOKY (Halloween) and SNOWFLAKE (Christmas) may only have been redeemable during their active event period. Attempting to redeem a seasonal code outside its active window returns an error that can be mistaken for a standard expired code — if a code fails despite being recently listed as active, check whether it was tied to a now-concluded seasonal event.

Server Running Old Build

Codes released alongside a Lost Currents update can fail on servers still running the previous build. If a freshly released code does not register on first attempt — particularly one that dropped within the last few hours of a new update patch — exit Lost Currents completely and rejoin to land on a current-build server. This is especially relevant in the first few hours after each new update goes live. Switching servers rather than retrying on the same one resolves this issue in most cases and is the recommended first troubleshooting step for any brand-new code.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for Lost Currents

Dive deeper, survive longer, and uncover the secrets of the AZR Sea
01
Redeem all active codes and join the flMindworks group before your first dive

Active codes award Gold that directly funds class unlocks — redeeming them before your first expedition means your starting session already has Gold working toward the Diver class (1.5x air capacity) or another class that fits your preferred crew role. Joining the flMindworks Roblox group connects you to the developer's official update and code announcement channel, ensuring you never miss a new code drop that could fund your next class upgrade. Both steps take under a minute and cost nothing.

02
Never dive alone — Lost Currents requires at least two players to function

Almost all meaningful actions in Lost Currents require two players — one to wear the diving helmet and one to operate the red lever on the boat that lowers and raises the diver. Solo players physically cannot dive without a partner managing the surface rope. Attempting to play alone will lock you out of the core gameplay loop entirely. Always queue with at least one friend or join a public server with an active crew before attempting any dive. Communication between the diver and the surface crew is as important as any equipment upgrade for successful Gold recovery.

03
Save Gold for classes — do not exchange all of it for Cash at Rustbeard's boat

Rustbeard is an NPC who appears periodically on a rusty boat and trades your salvaged items for Cash — but Gold itself can also be exchanged. New players often make the mistake of trading all their Gold for Cash early, then finding themselves unable to unlock any new classes. Gold funds permanent class unlocks while Cash funds consumables and boat upgrades. Maintain a Gold reserve specifically for the next class you are targeting, and only trade excess Gold for Cash once your class unlock goal is funded. Codes that award Gold are most valuable when saved for class purchases rather than traded immediately.

04
Press Q to signal your crew the moment you see a shark — do not wait

Sharks underwater can kill an unequipped diver almost instantly, and the time between spotting a shark and being killed is far shorter than most new players expect. The moment a shark appears in your field of view, press Q immediately to signal the surface crew to pull you up. Do not try to swim away from a shark — they are faster than the diver and will overtake you before you reach a safe distance. If you have not yet unlocked or purchased a Harpoon Gun from the boat shop, treat every shark encounter as an emergency extraction situation rather than a fight.

05
Prioritize buying a Harpoon Gun from the boat shop as your first upgrade

The Harpoon Gun is the most critical early-game survival item in Lost Currents — without it, any shark encounter underwater forces an emergency extraction that cuts the dive short and wastes the air supply invested in reaching the dive depth. With a Harpoon Gun, divers can neutralize sharks independently without panicking the surface crew. Upgrading to a Harpoon Gun before investing in decorative boat upgrades or consumables dramatically extends the average dive duration and Gold collection per session. Classes that spawn with a Harpoon Gun as a starting item are among the most valuable Gold expenditures available.

06
Enable Discord notifications for the Lost Currents server — update names hint at new codes

Lost Currents has a consistent pattern of naming codes after the content they accompany — ISLANDS dropped with the island expansion, HARPOONGUN with the harpoon update, LEVIATHAN with the leviathan content. Monitoring the Discord #codes channel and #announcements channel means you see new codes at the exact moment the developer posts them — often with context about what triggered the code drop. Enabling notifications on both channels costs nothing and provides the most reliable early-warning system for new code drops in a game where codes expire without advance notice.
