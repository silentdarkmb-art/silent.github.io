✨ About the Game

About Fisch

Developed by Fisching • Top-ranked fishing simulator on Roblox

Fisch is one of the most popular games on Roblox, developed by Fisching. It is a relaxed open-world fishing simulator where players cast lines across diverse environments — from calm coastal shores to deep ocean trenches — in pursuit of over 400,000 unique fish variations. Caught fish are sold to merchants for Cash, which is reinvested into better rods, bait, and crab cages that improve the catch rate for rare and exotic species. The game features an ever-expanding world with multiple distinct biomes, a progression system driven by player level and gear quality, and recurring seasonal events that introduce limited-time fish, cosmetics, and Hunt Totems. New content including expanded maps, new fish species, and balance updates arrives on a weekly schedule, keeping the active player base continuously engaged.

🎣 Gameplay

Players cast their line by selecting a cast power, then manage an active minigame that mirrors the tension and timing of real fishing. Successfully landing a catch rewards Cash based on the fish's rarity — common catches provide baseline income while rare and event-exclusive species yield significantly higher payouts. Cash funds upgrades at the merchant store, including rod tier upgrades that improve casting distance and catch probability, bait varieties that target specific fish types, and boats that unlock access to remote fishing zones unavailable from shore.

🎁 Code Drops

Fisching releases codes alongside major updates, milestone celebrations, and occasionally as compensation for server maintenance or unexpected downtime. Codes are announced in the official Discord server across the announcements, sub-announcements, and updates channels. Fisch has one of the largest code histories on Roblox with over 90 expired codes — the active list changes rapidly with each weekly update, making consistent monitoring of the Discord or a reliable code tracker essential for catching each new drop before it expires.

What Do Codes Give You?

Free Bobbers, Rod Skins, Coins, Totems, Cash, and exclusive gear
🎣

Bobbers

Bobbers are cosmetic floats that attach to the fishing line and are visible throughout every cast. The Carbon Bobber currently available from the active code is a permanent cosmetic unlock that requires no further spending. While bobbers do not affect fishing mechanics directly, they are among the most visible items in every session — and high-quality bobbers awarded by codes are often unavailable through normal merchant purchases, making code-exclusive bobbers some of the rarest cosmetics in the game.

Cosmetic
💰

Cash (C$)

Cash is the primary progression currency in Fisch, spent at merchants to purchase bait, rod upgrades, and equipment upgrades that expand access to higher-rarity fish. Codes that award Cash provide an immediate head start on the upgrade loop — particularly valuable for new players who receive 200 Cash as a joining bonus and need to accumulate significantly more before accessing mid-tier fishing zones. Cash codes have historically awarded anywhere from 67 C$ to over 10,000 C$ per redemption depending on the update milestone they celebrate.

Primary Currency
🏆

Rod Skins

Rod skins are cosmetic overlays applied to fishing rods that change their visual appearance without altering their fishing stats. Past codes have awarded exclusive rod skins tied to seasonal events, developer milestones, and special collaborations — many of which are permanently unavailable after their associated code expires. Expired codes from past seasons like the Frigid Beauty Skin, Forestwing Skin, and several event-themed skins can no longer be obtained through any other means, making currently active skin codes worth claiming immediately before the next update cycle replaces them.

Exclusive Cosmetic
🗺️

Hunt Totems & Event Items

Hunt Totems are special consumables used to activate rare fish hunt events that spawn specific legendary-tier fish within the active fishing zone. Past codes have awarded Colossal Dragon Hunt Totems, Megalodon Hunt Totems, and randomly assigned Totems — all of which are substantially harder to obtain through standard gameplay. Event items like Thorn Clusters, Clover Clusters, and Mini Pirate Ships have also appeared in past code rewards, providing materials and decoratives exclusively available through code redemption windows tied to seasonal event periods.

⚠️ Troubleshoot

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues & how to fix them fast
⏰

Code Expired After Weekly Update

Fisch updates on a weekly schedule, and codes tied to the previous week's content — particularly seasonal event codes — often expire alongside or shortly after the next update drops. This is the most common reason a recently-seen code no longer works. With over 90 expired codes in the game's history, the turnover rate is high. BLOXBURST moves any code confirmed expired on any verified source to the expired section immediately, and treats all "Check" status codes as expired per our conflict resolution policy.

🔡

Spelling Error (Despite Not Being Case-Sensitive)

Fisch codes are not case-sensitive — you can enter them in any capitalization pattern and they will register correctly. However, spelling errors, extra spaces, and missing punctuation such as exclamation marks or question marks still cause invalid code errors. Some past codes included special characters that are easy to omit when typing manually. Pasting directly from BLOXBURST using the Copy button on each card eliminates both case and spelling errors simultaneously and is always the more reliable approach over manual entry.

🔁

Already Redeemed

Each Fisch code is single-use per Roblox account. If the game indicates the code has already been claimed, the reward was added to your inventory in a previous session. With Fisch's large rotating code library and frequent update-tied drops, it is easy to lose track of which codes have been redeemed across multiple sessions. Keeping a note of redeemed codes or checking your inventory for the associated item is the fastest way to confirm whether a "Code already redeemed" error is accurate before reporting it as broken.

🎓

Level Requirement Not Met

Several Fisch codes in the game's history have required a minimum player level before they can be redeemed — past examples included level 50 and level 500 thresholds. Attempting to redeem a level-gated code before reaching the required level returns an error that can be mistaken for an expired or invalid code. If a code fails despite being confirmed active, check whether it carries a level requirement and verify your current level against that threshold before reporting the code as broken.

🔧

Server Running Old Build

Codes released alongside a weekly update can fail on servers still running the previous build. If a freshly released code does not register on first attempt — particularly one that dropped within the last few hours of an update — exit Fisch completely and rejoin to land on a current-build server. This is especially relevant in the first two hours after each weekly update goes live, while servers gradually transition to the new version across all active instances.

📋

Source Conflict — Code Moved to Expired

Several codes that appear active on other tracking pages are listed as expired here because they have been confirmed inactive on at least one additional verified source or carry a "Check" status that indicates unresolved redemption issues. BLOXBURST moves any conflicted code to the expired section immediately. Fisch's high code turnover rate means the gap between a code being listed as active on a slower-updating site and it actually being expired in the game can span several days — this page reflects the most conservative and up-to-date known status.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for Fisch

Catch rarer fish, earn more Cash, and unlock better gear faster
01
Redeem the active code and join the Fisching group before your first cast

The active code awards a Carbon Bobber immediately upon redemption, and joining the Fisching group through the in-game codes menu unlocks an additional chest reward. Both take under a minute to claim and cost nothing. Doing both before your first session means your starting inventory already includes exclusive items that are unavailable from the merchant store, setting you ahead of players who skip code redemption during their first play.

02
Complete quests consistently — they are the fastest source of rods and bobbers

Quests in Fisch reward rods and bobbers at a rate that outpaces merchant purchasing for most early and mid-game progression. Checking the quest board at the start of each session and targeting quests that align with the fish available in your current zone lets you accumulate gear upgrades without depleting your Cash reserves. Players who ignore quests in favor of pure merchant spending consistently fall behind on rod tier progression compared to those who blend both approaches.

03
Match your bait to the fish type in the zone you are fishing

Different baits attract different fish families, and using the correct bait for a zone's dominant fish type significantly increases your catch rate for higher-value species in that area. Using generic bait in a specialized zone catches a higher proportion of common low-value fish, which slows Cash accumulation per session. Checking the merchant's bait descriptions before moving to a new zone and stocking the matching bait type before casting is the single most efficient way to maximize Cash per hour of fishing time.

04
Get a boat as early as possible to unlock remote deep-water zones

Many of Fisch's rarest fish species are only accessible from open-water zones that require a boat to reach. Shore fishing is the starting point for all new players, but the fish density and rarity distribution in boat-accessible deep-water areas is substantially higher. Prioritizing boat acquisition over additional bait or minor rod upgrades early in progression shortens the path to catching the high-value fish that generate the Cash needed to fund every subsequent upgrade tier.

05
Participate in seasonal events for the best exclusive cosmetics

Fisch's seasonal events — including past Halloween, Christmas, Lunar New Year, St. Patrick's Day, and Jungle expansion events — have consistently offered the game's most exclusive rod skins, bobbers, and event fish that cannot be obtained outside the event window. Many of these items are permanently unavailable after the event ends. Participating actively in the current event rather than saving it for later ensures you collect all limited-time rewards before they are removed from circulation alongside the next weekly update.

06
Enable Discord notifications for all three announcement channels

Fisch codes drop across three Discord channels — announcements, sub-announcements, and updates — and not every code appears in all three. Enabling notifications on all three rather than just the main announcements channel is the only way to guarantee you catch every new code the moment it posts. Given that event-tied codes can expire within days of release, the window between a code posting in Discord and appearing on external tracking pages is often long enough that Discord-first redemption provides a meaningful advantage over waiting for code aggregators to update.
