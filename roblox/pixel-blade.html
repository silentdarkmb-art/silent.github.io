How to Redeem Pixel Blade Codes

  1. Join the Frost Blade Games Roblox Group (required for code redemption).
  2. Launch Pixel Blade on Roblox and wait for the Lobby to load.
  3. Find the yellow Bank circle and text in the lobby, then press E to interact.
  4. Type or paste a working code into the 'Enter Code…' text field exactly as shown (codes are case-sensitive).
  5. Hit Claim to receive your free Coins, Equipment, Chests, or other rewards instantly.
  6. If the code doesn't work, try rejoining the game to refresh the server, or check if it has already expired.
⚠️ Note: Pixel Blade codes expire quickly. Redeem them as soon as they're released for best results. You MUST join the Frost Blade Games group before redeeming!
🌀 About the Game

About Pixel Blade

Dungeon Crawler RPG by Frost Blade Games • Clear, Upgrade, Dominate

Pixel Blade is an addictive dungeon-crawling RPG on Roblox where players clear rooms of enemies, pick from three upgrade options after each victory, and grow stronger with every run. Gather Coins, Weapons, and Chests as you progress, then return to the Lobby to unlock powerful Skills, upgrade your gear, and prepare for increasingly difficult dungeon challenges ahead.

Designed for fans of roguelite progression and pixel-art action, Pixel Blade rewards strategic upgrade choices and efficient combat. Regular code drops grant free Coins, Equipment, and Chests—essential resources used to unlock new Skills, enhance your weapon loadout, and accelerate your dungeon-clearing speed. Starting with extra rewards from codes gives new blades a crucial head start in building a powerful character, allowing you to tackle harder worlds and climb the leaderboards faster.

⚔️ Core Gameplay Loop

Clear dungeon rooms by defeating enemies, choose one of three upgrades (damage, defense, or utility) after each victory, and collect Coins and Chests along the way. Return to the Lobby to spend earnings on permanent Skill unlocks and gear upgrades. Master enemy patterns and upgrade synergies to push deeper into progressively harder worlds.

💰 Coins, Equipment & Progression

Coins earned through gameplay or codes let you unlock new Skills and purchase gear upgrades in the Lobby. Equipment and Chests provide immediate stat boosts or rare items. Save Coins for high-value Skill unlocks, and use free code rewards to accumulate resources for optimal build paths without excessive grinding.

Match the Exact Letter Case

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for Pixel Blade

Clear smart and maximize your Coins and Upgrades from day one!
01
Redeem Codes Before Spending Coins

Claim all active codes before using your earned Coins randomly. Free Coins and Equipment from codes give you extra upgrade power without depleting your earned resources. This quick step can significantly boost your early Skill options and let you find optimal builds sooner.

02
Choose Upgrades Strategically

After each room, you'll pick one of three upgrades. Instead of choosing randomly, prioritize upgrades that synergize with your current build (e.g., damage + crit, or defense + heal). This focused approach helps you clear rooms faster and earn more rewards to fuel your growth.

03
Save Coins for Key Skill Unlocks

Instead of spending Coins on minor gear upgrades early, prioritize unlocking permanent Skills in the Lobby that provide lasting benefits. These Skills compound over multiple runs and give you a stronger foundation for tackling harder worlds.

04
Learn Enemy Patterns

Each dungeon enemy has telltale attack patterns. Spend your first few runs observing rather than rushing—learning when to dodge, block, or strike will dramatically improve your clear speed and reduce resource waste from unnecessary deaths.

05
Check for Codes After Game Updates

Frost Blade Games often releases fresh codes following patches, milestone celebrations, or holiday events. Make it a habit to check BLOXBURST or official Discord after updates for new free Coins or Equipment that can freshen up your blade's loadout.
