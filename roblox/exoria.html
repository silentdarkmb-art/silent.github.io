🌑 About the Game

About Exoria

Developed by OzelBlox • Roblox Anime Open-World RPG Game

Exoria is a dark anime open-world RPG developed by OzelBlox on Roblox, inspired by the Blue Exorcist franchise. Players explore cursed zones filled with demons and spirits, battle formidable bosses in unique dungeons, and progress through a distinctive Purity Path alignment system — where your moral choices directly shape the skills you unlock and how the world reacts to your presence. The game features a skill tree, card collecting, and flashy combat across PC, Mobile, and Console. Rolls are the key resource for rerolling your Race and Race Style to optimize your build, and codes provide free Rolls instantly — giving you the best possible start without grinding.

Crowns

Crowns are a premium currency in Exoria used to fund key aspects of your character's growth and progression through the game's open world. Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues and how to fix them fast

Typos & Case-Sensitive Input

Exoria codes are case-sensitive — even a single wrong character will invalidate the code. Copy and paste directly from BLOXBURST to avoid input errors entirely. On mobile, autocorrect may silently alter a character after pasting, so always verify the text field before hitting Claim Code.

Code Has Expired

Codes in Exoria can expire without any advance notice. BLOXBURST monitors multiple sources and removes any code the moment it shows expired or conflicting status. If a code is no longer on the active list here, it has already been flagged and will not work.

🔄

Already Redeemed

Each Exoria code is single-use per Roblox account. If the game returns an already-redeemed message, the Rolls or Rerolls were added to your account in a previous session. Check the active list before attempting codes to avoid repeated failed attempts.

🔧

Old Server After Update

Codes released alongside a new update can fail on servers still running the previous build. If a freshly announced code returns invalid — especially within the first hour or two of a patch — exit the game entirely and rejoin to land on a fresh server running the current version.

Wrong Menu Location

Exoria codes can only be redeemed from the loading screen — click the Codes button before entering the game world, paste your code into the text field, and press Claim Code to confirm. The Codes button will not appear once you are already inside the game, so redeem before launching in.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for EXORIA

Choose your path wisely, master your abilities, and become a legendary exorcist from your very first session
01
Redeem all active codes on the loading screen before entering the world — click Codes, paste each code, and hit CLAIM CODE to collect free Rolls, Crowns, and XP Boosts

The code redemption screen appears right at the start of Exoria before you even load into the game world, so you do not need to be inside to claim rewards. Enter each active code exactly as shown — Exoria codes are case-sensitive and a single wrong character causes a failure. Codes reward Rolls for rerolling your race and abilities, Crowns, and XP Boosts. Rolls in particular are most valuable early when you are still deciding your preferred race and build, making day-one code redemption the single most impactful free action available to any new player.

02
Use Rolls from codes immediately to lock in a strong race before investing time into your skill tree — your race shapes your entire progression curve

In Exoria, your race determines core stat multipliers and interacts directly with your Purity Path alignment, influencing which skills unlock earlier or later in your skill tree. Committing significant playtime to a race you later want to change means spending Rolls to reset — a resource that is scarce through normal play. Use the Rolls from codes on your first session to reroll into a race that fits your intended playstyle, then begin investing in your skill tree from a foundation you are satisfied with rather than one you settled for.

03
Decide your Purity Path alignment early — choosing between cleansing the world or embracing darker power gates different skills and quests throughout your entire run

The Purity Path is Exoria's moral alignment system and the feature that most distinguishes it from other Roblox anime RPGs. Your choices during Purity Quests push your character toward a cleansing or darker alignment, and that alignment determines which skills unlock earlier or later in your skill tree. Two players with identical races and playtime can end up with completely different builds depending on their Purity Path decisions. Understand which direction you want to go before committing to your first Purity Quests, because heavy commitment in one direction gates off certain abilities from the other side.

04
Level up by doing whatever you enjoy — fighting, exploring, doing quests, or hunting bosses all award XP, so there is no single correct grind path in this game

Exoria is designed so that XP flows from all activities equally, meaning you never need to force yourself into a specific grind loop. Fighting enemies, completing quests, exploring cursed zones, and hunting bosses in dungeons all contribute to your progression. Be aware that certain high-purity areas can attract stronger enemies to you as your alignment shifts, so pay attention to how the world reacts to your Purity level as you explore deeper into the map. Activate XP Boost codes from codes right before a dedicated session to maximize their timed value.

05
Check the official Exoria Trello board for mechanics, boss information, and future content — it is publicly maintained by the developers and updated with each patch

HADEZ_STUDIO maintains a public Trello board for Exoria that covers game mechanics, Purity Path details, boss dungeon information, and upcoming content. For a game with a unique alignment system and evolving skill tree, this is far more useful than relying on community speculation. Bookmark the Trello board alongside the Discord server to stay informed about changes that could affect your build decisions, especially after major updates which frequently adjust Purity Path thresholds and skill unlock timing.

06
Join the HADEZSTUDIO Discord and follow @h4dezstudio509 on YouTube — codes drop during updates and milestones with no fixed schedule and expire without warning

HADEZ_STUDIO releases new Exoria codes alongside major game updates, when the game hits Like and visit milestones, and during community events.
