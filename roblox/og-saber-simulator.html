⚔️ About the Game

About OG Saber Simulator

Developed by ND Game • Roblox Sword Training Simulator

OG Saber Simulator is a nostalgic sword-swinging simulator developed by ND Game on Roblox, created by developers @NominoDev and @EternityDev out of pure nostalgia for the original Saber Simulator experience. Players swing their saber to accumulate Strength, battle other players, and sell their Strength for Coins to invest in better gear and upgrades. The game features a weekly x2 Strength bonus to keep grinding rewarding. Progression centers around acquiring stronger sabers with higher multipliers, unlocking Auras, and collecting Charms — with Auto Swing and Shiny Charms from codes giving new players a crucial early advantage to climb the leaderboards faster.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for OG SABER SIMULATOR

Swing smarter, upgrade faster, and dominate the leaderboards from your very first session
01
Join the ND Game Roblox Group before redeeming codes — group membership is required to unlock most codes in OG Saber Simulator

Before entering any code, join the ND Game Roblox Group first. Most codes in OG Saber Simulator are locked behind group membership, meaning they will simply not work without it. Once you are in the group, press the Discord button in the bottom right corner of the screen, enter your code in the text box, and hit the green Redeem button. Codes reward Auto Swing, Shiny Charms, Swings, and other boosts that give you a head start — all of which expire quickly, so claim them the moment you load in.

02
Keep swinging your saber consistently — every swing builds Strength, and Strength is the currency that drives every upgrade in the game

OG Saber Simulator is built around a single core loop: swing your saber to generate Strength, sell that Strength for Coins, and use those Coins to upgrade your gear. The Auto Craft feature — which gives the game its current name tag — automates part of this process once unlocked, but consistent manual swinging in your early sessions is the fastest way to build up your initial Strength stockpile. Never let your character sit idle when you can be swinging, because every swing compounds directly into your Coin income.

03
Sell your Strength regularly — holding onto it does not increase its value, and Coins sitting unspent in your wallet are upgrades you are not making

A common beginner mistake in OG Saber Simulator is hoarding Strength without selling it. Strength does not grow in value the longer you hold it — selling it frequently and reinvesting those Coins immediately into better sabers and shop upgrades is how you maintain forward momentum. The faster your upgrade cycle turns over, the faster your per-swing Strength generation increases, which compounds into a much higher Coin income over the course of a session.

04
Use Charms from codes strategically to improve your chances of hatching rarer pets — Shiny, Rainbow, and Void pets multiply your stats significantly more than base pets

Pets in OG Saber Simulator provide passive stat boosts to your Strength and Coin generation. While base pets are useful, the real power comes from higher-tier variants: Shiny pets give x2 stats, Rainbow pets give x2.5, and Void pets give x3. Charms increase your odds of hatching these rare tiers directly from eggs, skipping the crafting grind of combining ten base pets. Save Charms from codes for dedicated hatching sessions where you can maximize the value of every boost they provide.

05
Craft pets from duplicates using the Auto Craft system — combining 10 of the same pet creates a higher-tier version with significantly better stat multipliers

OG Saber Simulator's Auto Craft feature allows you to combine ten copies of the same pet into a single Golden-tier pet, which provides x1.5 the original stats. Further crafting upgrades this all the way through Shiny, Rainbow, and Void tiers. Rather than holding dozens of weak duplicate pets that do little for your progression, set Auto Craft to run on your common duplicates so the system constantly pushes your pet collection toward higher stat multipliers without requiring manual attention during every session.

06
Watch the ND Game Discord for new codes — they drop during major updates, milestones, and events with no fixed schedule and expire fast

ND Game releases new OG Saber Simulator codes alongside major game updates, when the game hits community milestones like Like and visit counts, during holiday events, and occasionally as compensation when servers experience bugs or downtime. Codes are time-limited and disappear without advance notice. Joining the official Discord server ensures you see new code announcements the moment they go live. If a code fails after entering it correctly, try restarting the game and joining a fresh server — some codes only activate on updated server instances.
