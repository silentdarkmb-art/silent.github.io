🌀 About the Game

About SCP Architect X

SCP Facility Management Simulator by bWare Studios • Build, Hire, Contain

SCP Architect X is a strategic facility management simulator on Roblox where players design and operate their own SCP containment site from the ground up. You'll plan room layouts, hire staff like scientists and guards, manage Class-D personnel, and test dangerous anomalies—all while preventing riots and maintaining order. The game emphasizes tactical planning, resource allocation, and crisis response—every decision impacts your facility's efficiency and security rating.

Built for fans of base-building and simulation games, SCP Architect X rewards foresight and adaptive management. Regular code drops grant free Cash (Money)—the essential currency used to construct new rooms, hire additional personnel, purchase security upgrades, and unlock advanced anomaly testing protocols. Starting with extra Cash gives new Site Directors a crucial head start in establishing robust containment procedures before tackling high-risk anomalies and large-scale containment breaches.

🏗️ Core Gameplay Loop

Design your facility layout by placing containment chambers, offices, and utility rooms. Hire staff to manage operations, assign Class-D personnel to testing duties, and monitor anomaly behavior. Prevent riots through proper staffing and security measures, then expand your site to accommodate more dangerous SCPs and increase your Foundation's reputation.

💰 Cash & Facility Progression

Cash earned through gameplay or codes funds every aspect of your facility—from basic room construction to advanced security systems. Prioritize spending based on current threats: early-game focus on staffing and basic containment, mid-game on riot prevention, and late-game on high-level anomaly testing. Free Cash from codes accelerates this progression, letting you skip early grind and dive into complex facility designs faster.
