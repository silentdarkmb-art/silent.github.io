About Jujutsu Beatdown

Jujutsu Kaisen RPG by VIVE Studios • Master Cursed Techniques, Battle Curses, Rise Through Ranks

Jujutsu Beatdown is an action-packed Jujutsu Kaisen-inspired RPG on Roblox where players master cursed techniques, battle powerful curses, and climb the ranks of the jujutsu world. With a deep progression system, unique character abilities, and intense PvP combat, the game rewards strategic team-building and consistent practice.

Designed for fans of anime combat and incremental progression, Jujutsu Beatdown features regular code drops that grant free Gems and exclusive rewards—valuable resources that accelerate your early-game growth. Gems are used for gear, upgrades, and unlocking new cursed techniques. Codes are essential for new players to build momentum before tackling high-level curses or ranked PvP matches.

⚔️ Core Gameplay Loop

Train cursed techniques to earn XP → Level up to unlock new abilities → Battle curses and players for Gems → Unlock domain expansions and special moves → Customize your character build → Compete in ranked matches for exclusive rewards.

💎 Gems & Progression System

Gems are earned through gameplay and spent on gear, upgrades, and cursed technique unlocks. Free Gems from codes help you progress faster without grinding. Save high-value rewards for end-game content like domain expansions or legendary cursed techniques to maximize your progression.
