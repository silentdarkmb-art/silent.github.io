✨ About the Game

About Street Brawlers

Developed by StreetBrawlin • Fast-rising Roblox street fighting PvP game

Street Brawlers is a free-for-all street fighting PvP game on Roblox, developed by StreetBrawlin and supported by the Street Brawlers Community group with over 7,800 members. Set in a living, active city environment, the game places players in an open urban battleground where they can choose to hang out peacefully or throw hands with anyone around them. The core experience is built around skill-based combat — players learn unique Fighting Styles, unlock rare Abilities and Flows through the Spin system, and compete to dominate designated fighting zones across active servers. The game is fully supported on PC, Console, and Mobile, and receives regular updates that introduce new abilities, balance improvements, and fresh content drops accompanied by new codes. Street Brawlers launched as a relatively new title and has been growing rapidly, with the developer community actively engaging players through Discord and the official Roblox group.

🎁 Rewards

What Do Codes Give You?

Free Cash, Spins, rare Abilities, and exclusive Flows
🎰

Spins

Spins are the most valuable reward from Street Brawlers codes — each Spin rolls the RNG system for a chance to unlock a new Ability or Flow that permanently expands your fighting style. Rare Abilities and Flows are only obtainable through Spins, and the rarest variants have extremely low drop rates that require multiple rolls to land. Code-awarded free Spins provide rolls without spending your accumulated Cash balance, making them the fastest way to expand your ability pool early without grinding through fights to build up enough currency for paid rolls.

RNG Unlock
💰

Cash

Cash is the primary in-game currency in Street Brawlers, used to purchase Spins for new Abilities and Flows and to fund other in-game transactions across the city. Codes that award Cash — like the active UPDATETWO and RELEASE codes — provide an immediate head start on the Spin economy without requiring hours of combat grinding to accumulate. For new players still learning the fighting system, a Cash-awarding code can meaningfully accelerate early access to higher-tier Abilities that make a measurable difference in PvP encounters against more experienced fighters.

Primary Currency
⚡

Abilities

Abilities are special combat moves that define your fighting style in Street Brawlers — each Ability has unique animations, hitboxes, and damage profiles that complement different playstyles. Abilities are tiered by rarity, with the rarest variants providing significantly stronger combat utility than common drops. While Abilities are primarily obtained through Spins, occasional code drops have directly awarded specific Abilities outside of the RNG system — making those codes among the most valuable in the game's history since they bypass the randomness of the Spin roll entirely and guarantee a specific combat move.

Combat Move
🌊

Flows

Flows are movement and combat style modifiers that layer on top of your core Abilities to create a fully personalized fighting identity in Street Brawlers. Like Abilities, Flows are tiered by rarity and obtained primarily through the Spin system — but the rarest Flows fundamentally change how your character moves and chains attacks in ways that standard Abilities cannot replicate. Code-awarded Spins represent your best free opportunity to land high-rarity Flows without depleting your Cash reserves on paid rolls, making every active code with Spin rewards worth claiming before the next update cycle removes it.

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues & how to fix them fast
⏰

Code Expired After Update

Street Brawlers codes are tied closely to specific game updates — the RELEASE code dropped with the game's launch, and FIRSTUPD and UPDATETWO dropped with subsequent patches. Once the next update launches, codes tied to previous versions can be deactivated without any announcement. This is the most common reason a recently-seen code no longer works. BLOXBURST moves any code confirmed expired on any verified source to the expired section immediately, and treats all "Check" status codes as expired per our conflict resolution policy.

🔡

Spelling Error — Double Check Capitalization

Street Brawlers codes can be case-sensitive — entering a code in the wrong capitalization pattern may return an invalid error even if the spelling is otherwise correct. Extra spaces before or after the code also cause failures that are easy to miss when typing manually. Always paste directly from BLOXBURST using the Copy button on each code card rather than typing codes manually. This eliminates both capitalization errors and accidental spacing issues simultaneously, which is the most common source of invalid code errors for correctly-timed redemptions.

👥

Not a Member of Street Brawlers Community Group

Street Brawlers requires players to join the Street Brawlers Community Roblox group AND like the game before any code will register successfully. Attempting to redeem a code without completing both steps returns an error that can be mistaken for an expired or invalid code — even when the code itself is perfectly active. If a code fails on first attempt, verify your group membership status before reporting the code as broken. Joining the group takes under a minute and permanently unlocks code redemption for all future drops.

🔁

Already Redeemed

Each Street Brawlers code is single-use per Roblox account. If the game indicates the code has already been claimed, the Cash and Spins were added to your account in a previous session. With Street Brawlers' small but growing code pool and update-tied drops, it is easy to lose track of which codes were redeemed across multiple play sessions. Checking your current Cash balance and Spin count against your expected totals is the fastest way to confirm whether an "already redeemed" error is accurate before reporting the code as broken.

🔧

Server Running Old Build

Codes released alongside a Street Brawlers update can fail on servers still running the previous build. If a freshly released code does not register on first attempt — particularly one that dropped within the last few hours of a new update — exit Street Brawlers completely and rejoin to land on a current-build server. This is especially relevant in the first few hours after each new update goes live, while servers gradually transition to the updated version across all active instances. Switching servers rather than retrying on the same one resolves this issue in most cases.

📋

Source Conflict — Code Moved to Expired

Several codes that appear active on other tracking pages are listed as expired here because they have been confirmed inactive on at least one additional verified source or carry a "Check" status that indicates unresolved redemption issues. BLOXBURST moves any conflicted code to the expired section immediately. The gap between a code being listed as active on a slower-updating site and it actually being expired in-game can span several days — this page reflects the most conservative and up-to-date known status to protect you from wasting time on dead codes.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for Street Brawlers

Win more fights, earn more Cash, and unlock rare Abilities faster
01
Join the Street Brawlers Community group and like the game before anything else

Both steps are mandatory before any code will register in Street Brawlers — attempting to redeem a code without group membership returns an error regardless of how correct the code is. Completing both in your first minute costs nothing and permanently unlocks code redemption for every future drop. Once done, redeem all active codes immediately for free Cash and Spins that give you a head start on ability unlocks before your first fight.

02
Spend your starting Spins on Abilities before investing in Flows

New players receive a small number of starting Spins, and the temptation is to roll everything immediately. Prioritize Ability rolls first — Abilities directly determine your combat effectiveness in fights, while Flows are style modifiers that enhance movement and chaining. A strong Ability set wins fights even with a basic Flow, but a rare Flow with a weak Ability set will consistently lose to experienced fighters who have invested in combat moves. Once you have a solid Ability foundation, redirect Cash earnings toward Flow rolls to refine your fighting identity.

03
Control fighting zones for bonus Cash — don't just roam and brawl

Street Brawlers rewards players who actively contest and hold designated fighting zones with bonus Cash payouts on top of standard fight rewards. New players who roam freely and fight randomly earn significantly less Cash per session than those who identify the nearest contested zone and focus their fights there. Holding a zone even briefly generates meaningful passive income that compounds into more Spins over time. Learn the city layout early and always fight with zone control in mind rather than treating every encounter as an isolated duel.

04
Learn ability cooldowns — fighting on cooldown is the fastest way to lose

Every Ability in Street Brawlers has a cooldown period after use, during which you are limited to basic attacks. New players who burn all their Abilities in the opening seconds of a fight are left with no special moves when the opponent's own Abilities are still available — a situation that almost always results in a loss. Practice using one Ability at a time, observing the cooldown before committing the next, and saving your strongest Ability for when the opponent is already in a vulnerable position rather than using it as an opener.

05
Avoid fighting multiple opponents simultaneously until your Ability set is strong

Street Brawlers is a free-for-all environment — anyone can join an ongoing fight at any moment. New players with limited Ability sets who engage multiple opponents simultaneously burn through cooldowns faster than they can recover, leaving them defenseless against follow-up attacks. Until your Ability and Flow combination is strong enough to handle burst situations, disengage from fights that attract additional opponents and reset on a single target. Experienced players specifically look for overextended beginners to chain into multi-hits — awareness of your surroundings during every duel is as important as the duel itself.

06
Enable Discord notifications and check the game page after every update

Street Brawlers codes drop with updates and expire fast — the developer has confirmed new codes in both the official Discord #announcements channel and directly in the Roblox game page description. Enabling notifications on the Discord announcements channel and checking the game page description after every update is the only reliable two-channel approach to catching every code on release day. Given that codes are tied to specific update versions, the window between a code dropping and expiring can be as short as the time until the next patch — making same-day redemption the only safe strategy.
