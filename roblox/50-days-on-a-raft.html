🌊 About the Game

About 50 Days on a Raft🌊

Developed by Ocean Survival • Roblox Survival Game

50 Days on a Raft is a Roblox ocean survival game by Ocean Survival where players are stranded at sea after a plane crash. Starting with only a small raft, you gather drifting resources, build and expand your platform, craft tools, manage hunger and thirst, and survive increasingly dangerous nights. The ultimate goal is to locate a flare gun and signal for rescue before day 50 ends. The ocean hides secrets, underwater wrecks, and a lurking monster that grows more dangerous over time.

⚠️ Troubleshoot

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues & how to fix them fast
⏰

Code Has Expired

50 Days on a Raft codes expire without warning, often within days or weeks of release. If you receive an invalid code error, the code has likely expired — return to BLOXBURST for an updated working list.

🔡

Wrong Capitalization

All codes are case-sensitive. For example, snow and SNOW are treated as different codes. Always copy and paste directly from BLOXBURST to avoid capitalization errors.

🔄

Already Redeemed

Each code can only be claimed once per Roblox account. If you have already used the code on your current account, the game will show an already redeemed message with no reward issued.

🔧

Outdated Server

Freshly released codes can fail on older game servers that haven't refreshed. Exit 50 Days on a Raft completely and rejoin to load a fresh server where the new code will be recognized.

🖊️

Extra Space in Code

An accidental space before or after the code triggers an invalid error even when the code itself is correct. Check both ends of the text field before pressing Redeem to clear any hidden spaces.

📋

Wrong Code Box

Make sure you are entering the code in the ENTER PROMOCODE box specifically — not a search field or another menu. Click the Codes button on the left side of the screen to open the correct input.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for 50 Days on a Raft🌊

Survive longer and spend your Caps wisely from day one
01
Redeem codes before your first session

Free Wheel Spins and Caps from codes give you a meaningful head start before you even craft your first tool. Redeeming them immediately means you enter the ocean with extra resources and a chance at a rare class right away.

02
Prioritize food and water before expanding your raft

Empty hunger and thirst meters drain your health passively. Before spending materials on raft expansion, craft a fishing rod and at least one water barrel — survival basics take priority over building size in the early days.

03
Hook floating debris every time it passes

Wood, plastic, and scrap metal drifting past your raft are your primary resource income. Staying active and hooking every piece of passing debris in the early days builds up your material stockpile far faster than exploring alone.

04
Fortify your raft before nightfall on later days

Night difficulty increases as days progress. Sea creatures and the lurking monster become more aggressive after day 10 — use daylight hours to reinforce your raft edges and craft weapons before darkness falls.

05
Dive for underwater wrecks to find rare materials

Submerged debris sites contain materials and items not available from surface drifting. Craft a diving mask early and explore underwater wrecks during calm daytime periods when the monster is less active near the surface.

06
Save the flare gun for day 50 — don't use it early

The flare gun is the win condition of 50 Days on a Raft. Finding it early is a major milestone, but firing it before day 50 wastes the game's primary objective. Keep it in your inventory as motivation and use it only when the time is right.
