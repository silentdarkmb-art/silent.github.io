About Reborn As SkillMaster

Developed by Star Ocean 2024 • Roblox Anime Action RPG Game

Reborn As SkillMaster is an anime-inspired action RPG developed by Star Ocean 2024 on Roblox. Players step into an exciting world where they are reborn as a hero, battling cool anime characters across vibrant maps. As you progress, you train hard, discover new skills, and unlock increasingly powerful areas filled with better weapons and companions. Potions serve as a key resource for boosting your hero's performance, and codes provide a fast way to earn them for free — giving you an immediate edge in combat and progression.

Explore the world as a reborn hero, battling anime enemies across unlockable maps with progressively stronger swords and gear. Earning Potions through gameplay or codes accelerates your hero's combat effectiveness. Skills are trained and upgraded as you fight your way through encounters, while discovering new companions and weapons deepens your character build. Progress is rewarded with access to better maps and stronger opponents — keeping the adventure loop fresh and challenging.

Reborn As SkillMaster codes are redeemed by pressing the Shop button at the bottom of the screen — select the Code button within the Shop menu, enter the code in the text box, and click Get Reward to confirm. New codes are released through the official Star Ocean 2024 Discord and Roblox group page. Each code is single-use per Roblox account.

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues and how to fix them fast
🔡

Typos & Case-Sensitive Input

Reborn Skills Master codes are case-sensitive — even a single wrong character will invalidate the code. Copy and paste directly from BLOXBURST to avoid any input errors. On mobile, autocorrect may silently change the first character after pasting, so double-check the text field before hitting Get Reward.

⏰

Code Has Expired

Codes in Reborn Skills Master can expire without any advance notice. BLOXBURST monitors multiple sources and removes any code the moment it shows expired or conflicting status. If a code is no longer on the active list here, it has already been flagged and will not work.

🔄

Already Redeemed

Each Reborn Skills Master code is single-use per Roblox account. If the game returns an already-redeemed message, the Potions or freebies were added to your account in a previous session. Check the active list before attempting codes to avoid repeated failed attempts.

🔧

Old Server After Update

Codes released alongside a new update can fail on servers still running the previous build. If a freshly announced code returns invalid — especially within the first hour or two of a patch — exit the game entirely and rejoin to land on a fresh server running the current version.

🛍️

Wrong Menu Location

Codes in Reborn Skills Master can only be redeemed through the Shop button, then the Code button inside it. Enter your code in the text box and press Get Reward to confirm. Using any other menu or pressing keyboard Enter instead of the button can cause the redemption to fail.

📅

When New Codes Release

Star Ocean 2024 typically drops new codes alongside major game updates, holidays, special events, and community milestones. Server outages or in-game bugs may also prompt the developers to release compensation codes. Join the Discord and Roblox Group to catch new codes the moment they drop.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for REBORN AS SKILLMASTER

Train smarter, master your skills, and dominate every map from your very first session
01
Redeem all active codes immediately when you first load in — Potions give you a head start before your first fight

Before engaging any enemies, open the Shop button and navigate to the Code section. Type or paste each active code into the text box and hit Get Reward. The Potions you receive from codes like welcome and the latest update codes give you a meaningful boost right from the start, letting you push through early training thresholds faster than players who skip the code step entirely.

02
Focus on training dummies and consistent grinding first — your Power stat is the foundation for everything else

The core gameplay loop of Reborn As SkillMaster is built around training your Power by hitting dummies and fighting enemies repeatedly. Rushing into harder maps before your Power is ready will stall your progress. Spend dedicated time at each training zone until you feel comfortable handling the enemies on that map before moving forward. A strong Power foundation makes every subsequent upgrade and skill feel far more impactful.

03
Perform Rebirths as soon as the option is meaningful — each one upgrades your HP at the cost of Coins and Attack Points

Rebirth resets your Coins and Attack Points in exchange for a permanent HP upgrade. In the early maps this tradeoff feels painful, but in higher-level maps the extra HP is essential for surviving the stronger enemy attacks you will face. Treat Rebirths as a long-term investment rather than a short-term loss — players who delay Rebirths too long find themselves unable to survive in areas where properly-reborn characters advance comfortably.

04
Visit the Treasure Cave early and complete chest puzzles carefully — Mounts and Crystals inside directly boost your attributes

The Treasure Cave functions as a mini dungeon with chests that require puzzle completion to unlock their rewards. Mounts obtained here increase both your movement speed and attack damage, making them highly valuable for progression. The most common reward from cave chests is Crystals, which directly raise your character's attributes. Prioritizing the Treasure Cave early means your base stats grow faster than players who overlook it in favor of pure combat grinding.

05
Always equip your best available Pet and Mount — both increase your attack power and the difference is not minor

Pets and Mounts in Reborn As SkillMaster are not cosmetic. They actively boost your combat performance, and the difference between an equipped and unequipped setup is noticeable even in early maps. Whenever you obtain a new Pet or Mount, check whether it outperforms what you currently have equipped and swap immediately. Many beginners leave stronger Pets and Mounts sitting in their inventory while fighting with outdated ones, losing significant passive damage in the process.

06
New codes drop with every major update — join the Star Ocean 2024 Discord and check announcements immediately after any patch

The developer consistently releases new update codes alongside content patches and map additions. The pattern is predictable: each significant update brings at least one new working code. Joining the official Star Ocean 2024 Discord server ensures you see announcements the moment they go live. Codes in this game can expire, so claiming them within the first hours of an update is the safest way to guarantee you receive the rewards before they disappear.
