🎯 About the Game

About Throwing Simulator

Developed by HoiAndBai Studio • Roblox Incremental Simulator Game

Throwing Simulator is an incremental simulator developed by HoiAndBai Studio on Roblox. Players launch various objects as far as possible to earn Money based on throwing distance, then invest their earnings into Strength training and better throwables to push their distance even further. Rare pets can be hatched to provide powerful stat boosts, while Enchant Gems are used to upgrade throwing items for even greater range. Potions — including Money, Strength, and Luck variants — provide temporary multipliers that supercharge your grinding sessions. Codes hand out free Potions and Enchant Gems instantly, giving you a strong boost to climb the leaderboards without the slow early grind.

Throwing Simulator codes are case-sensitive — even a single wrong character will invalidate the code. Copy and paste directly from BLOXBURST to avoid input errors entirely. On mobile, autocorrect may silently alter a character after pasting, so always verify the text field before hitting Redeem.

Codes in Throwing Simulator can expire without any advance notice. BLOXBURST monitors multiple sources and removes any code the moment it shows expired or conflicting status. If a code is no longer on the active list here, it has already been flagged and will not work.

Already Redeemed

Each Throwing Simulator code is single-use per Roblox account. If the game returns an already-redeemed message, the Potions or Enchant Gems were added to your account in a previous session. Check the active list before attempting codes to avoid repeated failed attempts.

Old Server After Update

Codes released alongside a new update can fail on servers still running the previous build. If a freshly announced code returns invalid — especially within the first hour or two of a patch — exit the game entirely and rejoin to land on a fresh server running the current version.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for THROWING SIMULATOR

Train smarter, throw farther, and unlock new worlds from your very first session
01
Redeem all active codes immediately — press the Shop button on the left, open the Codes section, enter each code, and hit Redeem to collect free Potions and Enchant Gems

Before your first throw, open the Shop on the left side of the screen, navigate to the Codes section, and redeem every active code. Codes in Throwing Simulator reward Potions and Enchant Gems — Money Potions give 50% more money per throw for 5 minutes, Strength Potions boost your training gains, and Luck Potions increase your chances of hatching rare high-multiplier pets. These are some of the most impactful early-game items available, and they expire without warning, so claim them the moment you load in.

02
Train your Strength at the training wall consistently — Strength directly determines how far your throws travel, and distance is the only thing that generates Money

Every throw in Throwing Simulator earns Money based purely on how far the object travels. That distance is determined entirely by your Strength stat. Players who spend dedicated time at the training wall between throws build a compounding Strength advantage that makes every subsequent session more profitable than the last. Never skip training sessions in favor of just throwing repeatedly — the two activities work together, and neglecting either one slows your overall progression significantly.

03
Activate Money Potions right before a dedicated throwing session — never pop one passively, as the 5-minute timer runs even when you are not actively throwing

Money Potions provide a 50% income boost for exactly 5 minutes from the moment they are activated. Popping one and then switching to training, browsing the shop, or doing anything other than throwing wastes the majority of the boost window. Save your Money Potions for focused throwing sessions where you can launch objects back to back for the full duration, maximizing the total Money earned before the boost expires. The same rule applies to Strength and Luck Potions — activate them only when you are ready to immediately benefit.

04
Hatch pets from eggs as early as possible — pets stack passive Money and Strength multipliers that compound with every training session and every throw you make

Pets in Throwing Simulator provide passive multipliers to both your Money income and your Strength gains, meaning they benefit every single throw and every training session for as long as they are equipped. The sooner you hatch your first pets, the sooner every action you take in the game pays out more than it would without them. Spend your early Money on eggs before buying heavier throw items, because the multiplier advantage from a good pet outpaces the distance benefit of a better item in the early game.

05
Use Rebirth once your progress stalls — each Rebirth gives a permanent +10% Strength bonus that carries forward and makes every future run faster than the last

Rebirth in Throwing Simulator resets your current stats in exchange for a permanent +10% Strength bonus that stacks across every future run. This permanent bonus means each rebirth cycle starts stronger than the previous one and reaches the same progression milestones in less time. Do not rebirth too early — wait until training feels slow and buying new items no longer provides a noticeable jump in throw distance. At that point, a Rebirth is the highest-value action available and will immediately revitalize your progression speed.

06
Join the HoiAndBai Studio Discord and watch for new codes after every update — codes drop during updates, milestones, and events with no fixed schedule

HoiAndBai Studio releases new Throwing Simulator codes alongside major game updates, when the game hits Like and visit milestones, during holiday events, and occasionally as compensation for server downtime or bugs. There is no fixed release schedule and expiry dates are never announced in advance. Joining the official Discord server and checking the codes channel is the fastest way to catch new drops the moment they go live — before they expire or fill their usage limit.
