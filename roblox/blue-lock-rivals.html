How to Redeem Codes

🚗 About the Game

About Blue Lock Rivals

Developed by Blue Lock Rivals Unofficial Fans • Roblox Anime Soccer RPG

Blue Lock Rivals is a fan-made anime soccer RPG on Roblox developed by Blue Lock Rivals Unofficial Fans, inspired by the Blue Lock anime and manga series. Players compete in 5v5 soccer matches, choosing from character classes with unique abilities tied to the show. Styles and Flows are the core gacha mechanics — Styles determine a player's movement and attack set, while Flows grant passive bonuses and special abilities during matches. Lucky Style Spins and Lucky Flow Spins are the primary code rewards, used to pull new Styles and Flows from the gacha pool. Cash earned through matches is spent on upgrades and stat improvements. The 3v3 update added a new match format alongside new codes. Redemption requires joining the official Roblox group, liking the game, and reaching Level 10 before the Codes button becomes active.

🎁 Rewards

What Do Codes Give You?

Free rewards claimable directly on your account
🎨

Lucky Style Spins

The most common Blue Lock Rivals code reward. Each Lucky Style Spin pulls one Style from the gacha pool — Styles define the character's movement set, attacks, and special abilities during matches. Higher-rarity Styles are only obtainable through spins and are not purchasable with Cash. Code batches typically award between 5 and 20 Lucky Style Spins depending on the occasion.

Most Common Reward
🌊

Lucky Flow Spins

Lucky Flow Spins pull Flows from the gacha pool — passive ability sets that activate during matches to grant bonuses like speed bursts, stamina recovery, and offensive modifiers. Flows are separate from Styles and use their own dedicated spin pool. Code batches frequently include both Lucky Style and Lucky Flow Spins together, as the two mechanics complement each other during match play.

Secondary Reward
💵

Cash

Cash is the in-game currency spent on stat upgrades and character progression between matches. Some Blue Lock Rivals codes award Cash directly — past codes have awarded up to 7,500 Cash in a single redemption. Cash from codes stacks with Cash earned through match wins and daily login rewards, making early redemption the fastest way to fund early progression before grinding matches.

In-Game Currency
🎁

Festive & Event Spins

During seasonal events — Halloween, Snowlock, New Year — Blue Lock Rivals replaces standard Lucky Spins with event-specific spin types that pull from limited seasonal gacha pools. Past events introduced Festive Spins and Halloween Spins, each pulling exclusive seasonal Styles unavailable outside the event window. Event codes awarding these spins are among the most time-sensitive drops as they expire when the event ends.

⚠️ Troubleshoot

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues & how to fix them fast
⏰

Not in the Roblox Group

Joining the official Blue Lock Rivals Unofficial Fans Roblox group is a hard redemption requirement. The Codes button returns an error for any player who has not joined the group, regardless of level or like status. If redemption fails immediately on every code you try, confirm your group membership status first before checking code validity or capitalization.

🔡

Case-Sensitive Typo

Blue Lock Rivals codes are case-sensitive. Mixed-case codes and codes ending in exclamation marks must be entered exactly as shown — including punctuation. Copy and paste directly from BLOXBURST to eliminate all manual input errors. If a code still fails after pasting, check for a leading or trailing space that may have been included when copying.

🔄

Haven't Liked the Game

Liking the Blue Lock Rivals game on Roblox is a required condition alongside group membership and Level 10. If codes fail consistently across multiple entries and your group membership is confirmed, check whether the game is liked on your account. The like button is on the Roblox game page — a missing like blocks all code redemptions until it is added.

📋

Conflict Between Sources

Multiple sources showed significant disagreement on active codes — some trackers listed many more active codes than others. Nine codes that appeared active on one tracker were confirmed expired on another — BLOXBURST moved all nine to expired per the conflict rule. The remaining confirmed active codes were retained as they did not appear in any tracker's expired section.

🔧

Below Level 10

Level 10 is a hard redemption threshold in Blue Lock Rivals — the Codes button in the lobby does not function for accounts below this level. If the button appears but codes consistently fail, check your current level on the lobby screen. Play matches and complete daily quests to progress — quests award significantly more level XP per minute than standard match play alone.

⚙️

Already Redeemed

Each Blue Lock Rivals code is one-time use per Roblox account. With new batches dropping weekly, it is easy to lose track of which codes from recent updates have already been claimed. Check your Lucky Spin count in the lobby inventory against the total expected from all active codes to confirm whether any were previously redeemed before troubleshooting further.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for Blue Lock Rivals

Score more and get the most from every Blue Lock Rivals code reward
01
Meet all three redemption requirements before codes drop

Blue Lock Rivals codes require group membership, a game like, and Level 10 simultaneously. New codes release every Saturday or Sunday — players who have not met all three conditions cannot redeem that week's batch. Setting up group membership, liking the game, and reaching Level 10 before the next weekend update ensures you can claim every code on the same day it drops.

02
Save Lucky Flow Spins for new Flow releases, not existing ones

Lucky Flow Spins pull from the current active Flow pool. When a new Flow is added to the pool with a major update — like the 3v3 update — the probability of pulling it is at its highest relative to the total pool size before future additions dilute it. Saving accumulated Lucky Flow Spins from codes to use immediately after a new Flow is confirmed added to the pool gives you the best statistical chance of landing the new addition.

03
Complete daily quests every session to reach Level 10 faster

Daily quests award substantially more XP per minute than standard 5v5 match play. New players who need to reach Level 10 to unlock code redemption should prioritise completing all available daily quests before queuing for open matches — the combined XP from quests can reduce the grind to Level 10 by several sessions compared to match play alone.

04
Use Lucky Style Spins on update days to target newly added Styles

Each major Blue Lock Rivals update introduces new Styles to the gacha pool. The probability of pulling a specific new Style is highest on the day it launches — before future updates add more Styles to the same pool and reduce its individual odds. Using Lucky Style Spins earned from the update's accompanying codes on the same day the new Styles launch maximises the chance of landing them before pool dilution occurs.

05
Play 3v3 matches to learn individual positioning before 5v5

The 3v3 format uses smaller team sizes on the same pitch dimensions, which forces faster individual decision-making and one-on-one duel situations more frequently than 5v5. New players who learn positioning, ability timing, and one-on-one mechanics in 3v3 matches first typically transfer those skills into 5v5 faster than players who begin in the larger format where individual mistakes are easier to mask.

06
Check the Discord patch notes channel every Saturday and Sunday

Blue Lock Rivals codes are released every Saturday or Sunday alongside patch notes in the official Discord server. Enabling notifications on the patch notes channel gives you first access to new codes before they are separately reposted — useful if a code from a batch has a short validity window.
