🚗 About the Game

About Build an Arcade

Developed by Our Arcade • Roblox Arcade Tycoon Simulator

Build an Arcade is a Roblox tycoon game developed by Our Arcade. Players start with an empty space and fill it with arcade machines — everything from claw machines to racing cabinets — to attract NPCs who pay to play. Each machine earns passive income, which funds new machines, layout expansions, and decorations. Offline earnings let your arcade grow even while you're away. VIP Customers are high-value NPCs who spend significantly more per visit, making them the most sought-after reward from codes. Codes are released alongside major updates and community milestones, and all codes are case-sensitive.

⚠️ Troubleshoot

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues & how to fix them fast
⏰

Group Membership Required

Build an Arcade requires players to join the official Our Arcade Roblox group before the Codes button becomes active in the Rewards menu. If the Redeem button is greyed out or does nothing, joining the group and rejoining the game is the fix — this is the most common reason valid codes fail to register.

🔡

Case-Sensitive Typo

Build an Arcade codes are fully case-sensitive. A single wrong capital or lowercase letter will cause the Redeem button to reject the code. Always copy and paste directly from BLOXBURST rather than typing manually — this eliminates typos entirely.

🔄

Already Redeemed

Each code is one-time use per Roblox account. If the game shows a "code already redeemed" response after you enter it, that reward was successfully collected on your account at a previous point.

📋

Conflict Between Sources

Some codes appear active on one site but expired on another. BLOXBURST cross-checks multiple major trackers before listing — if a code shows as unconfirmed or appears in any source's expired section, we move it to expired immediately to protect you from wasted attempts.

🔧

Server Not Updated

Fresh codes can fail on older servers that haven't received the latest patch. Close Build an Arcade completely and rejoin — landing in a new server running the current build usually resolves the issue immediately.

⚙️

Wrong Menu Location

The code input in Build an Arcade is inside the Rewards section, not the main Shop view. Open Shop at the bottom of the screen, then press the yellow Rewards button or scroll down to the Rewards section — the Codes button is there, not on the main page.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for Build an Arcade

Grow your arcade and make the most of every reward code
01
Redeem all codes before placing new machines

Claim every active code first to maximize your VIP Customer count before expanding. VIP Customers immediately start earning on any machine you have placed, so having them active while you build compounds your early income advantage.

02
Join the Roblox group before launching

Group membership is required before the Codes button becomes active. Join the Our Arcade group on Roblox before your first session — if you forget, you will need to rejoin the game after joining, which costs time and occasionally causes you to miss a limited-use code window.

03
Gather resources before building your vehicle

Prioritize machines that generate the highest passive income per tile of floor space. Larger machines earn more but take up more room — in the early game, filling your space with smaller high-frequency machines and upgrading to larger ones after your first Rebirth gives you a faster income curve than starting big immediately.

04
Prioritize weapons for nighttime survival

Higher-tier machines earn significantly more per visit but also wear down faster, requiring more frequent maid or mechanic attention. Balancing your hiring budget against your machine upgrade pace keeps your arcade running at peak efficiency without constant manual repairs.

05
Keep enough fuel in reserve before long stretches

Decorations do not directly earn income but significantly increase customer satisfaction, which keeps NPCs in your arcade longer per visit. Placing decorations between machines rather than clustering them in a corner gives you the widest coverage for the least floor space used.

06
Join the Discord to catch codes early

Our Arcade announces codes in the official Discord server before they appear anywhere else. Joining and enabling notifications in the announcements channel is the fastest way to catch new codes — milestone codes can be claimed immediately on release before any external tracker has listed them.
