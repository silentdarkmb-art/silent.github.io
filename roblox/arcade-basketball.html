🏀 About the Game

About Arcade Basketball

Fast-Paced Basketball Simulator • Shoot, Score, and Climb the Leaderboard

Arcade Basketball is a skill-based basketball simulator on Roblox where players compete in 1v1 or 2v2 matches to score points and climb the ranked leaderboard. With simple controls (hold E to charge your shot, release at the perfect moment), the game rewards timing, practice, and strategy over complex mechanics.

Designed for fans of competitive sports games and arcade-style action, Arcade Basketball features regular code drops that grant free Coins, Crates, XP, and exclusive cosmetics—valuable resources that accelerate your early-game growth. Coins let you unlock new basketballs, auras, and clothing, while Crates contain random cosmetic rewards. Codes are essential for new players to build momentum before tackling high-level ranked matches or unlocking premium cosmetics.

🏀 Core Gameplay Loop

Play 1v1 or 2v2 matches → Score points by shooting with perfect timing → Earn Coins and XP → Unlock new basketballs, auras, and clothing → Climb the ranked leaderboard → Customize your baller's look and dominate the court.

💎 Coins & Crate System

Coins are earned through gameplay and spent on basketballs, auras, clothing, and cosmetic upgrades. Crates (Limited, Green Effects, Lucky Badge) contain random cosmetic rewards. XP helps you level up and unlock new features. Save high-value rewards from codes for premium cosmetics to maximize your customization options.
