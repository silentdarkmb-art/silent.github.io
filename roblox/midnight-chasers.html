🚗 About the Game

About Midnight Chasers: Highway Racing

Developed by Midnight In-dev • Roblox Highway Racing Game

Midnight Chasers: Highway Racing is a high-speed highway racing game developed by Midnight In-dev on Roblox. Players jump behind the wheel of a wide range of vehicles — from regular cars and motorcycles to powerful supercars and hypercars — and tear through traffic-filled highways without hesitation. The game blends free-roam cruising with competitive races, letting players swerve through traffic, push their car to its limits, host meetups to showcase their garage, and go head-to-head against other drivers. Cash is the primary currency for expanding your vehicle collection, and codes provide thousands of free Cash instantly — letting you get into better cars without grinding from scratch.

Cash

⚠️ Troubleshoot

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues and how to fix them fast
🔡

Typos & Case-Sensitive Input

Midnight Chasers codes are case-sensitive — even a single wrong character will invalidate the code. Copy and paste directly from BLOXBURST to avoid input errors entirely. On mobile, autocorrect may silently alter a character after pasting, so always verify the text field before hitting Redeem.

⏰

Code Has Expired

Midnight Chasers releases a new code with every update or likes milestone — and when a new one drops, older codes tend to expire. BLOXBURST monitors multiple sources and removes any code the moment it shows expired or conflicting status. If a code is no longer on the active list here, it has already been flagged and will not work.

🔄

Already Redeemed

Each Midnight Chasers code is single-use per Roblox account. If the game returns an already-redeemed message, the Cash was added to your account in a previous session. Check the active list before attempting codes to avoid repeated failed attempts.

🔧

Old Server After Update

Codes released alongside a new update can fail on servers still running the previous build. If a freshly announced code returns invalid — especially within the first hour or two of a patch — exit the game entirely and rejoin to land on a fresh server running the current version.

🛍️

Wrong Menu Location

Midnight Chasers codes can only be redeemed through the Store button in the bottom left corner of the screen — click the Codes tab inside the Store menu, enter your code in the text box, and press the Redeem button to confirm. The Codes tab will not appear unless you open the Store first.

📅

When New Codes Release

Midnight In-dev releases new codes with every major update and likes milestone — codes typically follow the format ThanksForXXXk as the game hits new player counts. Join the Midnight Chasers Discord and the Midnight In-dev Roblox Group to catch new codes the moment they drop.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for MIDNIGHT CHASERS: HIGHWAY RACING

Earn cash faster, build your garage smarter, and own the highway from your very first drive
01
Redeem all active codes immediately — open the Store button on the left, go to the Codes tab, enter each code exactly, and hit Redeem to collect free Cash

Before taking your first drive, tap the Store button on the left side of the screen, navigate to the Codes tab, and redeem every active code. Midnight Chasers codes are case-sensitive and reward thousands of Cash per code — the current ThanksFor750k code gives you a significant head start toward your first vehicle upgrade. Codes follow a consistent ThanksForXXXk milestone pattern and expire at unpredictable times, so claiming them the moment you load in guarantees you never miss free starting capital.

02
Join the Midnight In-dev Roblox Group immediately — group members permanently earn 25% more Cash on every drive at absolutely no cost

Joining the official Midnight In-dev Roblox Group is completely free and gives you a permanent 25% Cash boost on all earnings while driving. This passive multiplier applies to every close call, every traffic swerve, and every kilometer you drive across all maps. Combined with code rewards, this group bonus compounds into a significantly larger Cash income over any session compared to players who skip the join step. It is one of the highest-value free actions available to any new player before their first drive.

03
Swerve in and out of traffic constantly — close calls with other vehicles are the primary source of Cash income and the fastest way to climb the leaderboard

Cash in Midnight Chasers is earned by driving around and making close calls with traffic, not from completing set objectives. The tighter your swerves past other vehicles, the more Cash you accumulate per minute. Players who drive cautiously and avoid traffic earn far less than those who actively cut through it. Embrace the no-hesi style the game is built around — threading through gaps between cars and bikes at high speed is both the core gameplay loop and the most efficient money-making method available.

04
Choose your map carefully before loading in — the Shutoko Expressway is the main map with full features, while Boneville Salt Flats is newer and still being developed

Midnight Chasers currently features two maps: the Shutoko Expressway and the Boneville Salt Flats. The Shutoko Expressway is the main and most developed map, featuring denser traffic, more features, and a better overall experience. The Boneville Salt Flats is a newer addition that is still being refined and has known bugs. Critically, your map selection is locked in once a session starts and cannot be changed mid-game — choose the Shutoko Expressway for your early sessions until you are comfortable with the game's mechanics.

05
Invest Cash into vehicle upgrades and customization before buying a new car — upgrading your current vehicle's Nitros, Tires, and Drivetrain increases your traffic-cutting ability significantly

The Customization Shop at the spawn point Dealership offers 11 upgrade types including Paint, Nitros, Aspiration, Tires, Brakes, Drivetrain, Suspension, Rims, License Plate, Police Lights, and Underglow. Most upgrades are purchasable with in-game Cash. Investing in Nitros and Drivetrain first improves your speed and handling, which directly increases how many close calls you can make per minute and therefore how much Cash you earn. Upgrading before buying an entirely new vehicle is almost always the better return on your Cash investment early on.

06
Follow the Midnight In-dev Discord and the @MidnightIndev X account — new codes drop at every favor milestone with no fixed schedule and expire in days

Midnight In-dev releases new codes consistently whenever the game hits a new favorites or visit milestone, following the ThanksForXXXk naming pattern. Smaller milestone codes can expire within just a few days, while larger celebration codes tend to last longer. Joining the Discord server and following the official X account gives you the fastest access to new codes the moment they go live. You can also host meetups and find other players to drive with through the Discord — a great way to enjoy the free-roam side of the game while your Cash builds up.
