18 FLOORS codes are case-sensitive — mixing up uppercase and lowercase letters is one of the most common reasons a redemption fails. Copy and paste directly from BLOXBURST every time.

Each code can only be claimed once per account. If you receive an "already redeemed" response, that reward was successfully collected on your account at an earlier point.
