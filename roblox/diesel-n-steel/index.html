? About the Game

About Diesel n' Steel

Developed by Diesel n' Steel Official Group • Original Jeepney Simulator on Roblox

Diesel n' Steel is the original Jeepney simulator on Roblox, developed by the Diesel n' Steel Official Group. Set across a recreation of the bustling province of Bulacan in the Philippines, the game puts players inside the everyday hustle of Philippine public transportation. Players choose between several roles — Jeepney Driver, Operator, Barker, Conductor, or Citizen — and work to earn Cash by competing for passengers, calculating fares, and completing routes. The goal is to accumulate enough Cash to buy, upgrade, and fully customize your own Jeepney, ultimately becoming the King of the Road in a game that blends simulation, strategy, and Filipino cultural identity in one unique experience on Roblox.

?? Gameplay

Pick your role and jump into the Bulacan road network where real players compete for passengers on fixed routes. As a driver, you spawn your Jeepney at the terminal, pick up waiting passengers, and drive them to their stops to collect fares. Cash earned from successful routes lets you repair vehicles, purchase new ones, and apply cosmetic customizations. The economy is balanced around consistent driving and smart route management — every peso counts toward upgrading to a faster, better-equipped ride.

?? Code Drops

Diesel n' Steel releases codes during major updates, milestone celebrations, community events, and occasionally as compensation for server disruptions or bugs. New codes are typically announced through the official Discord server and the developer's Roblox group page. The game has an extensive code history — most codes remain active for an extended period, making it one of the more code-friendly Roblox experiences available. All codes are case-sensitive and require 500 EXP before they can be redeemed.

?? Troubleshoot

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues & how to fix them fast
??

500 EXP Requirement Not Met

Diesel n' Steel locks the code redemption system behind a minimum of 500 in-game EXP. Attempting to use the Gift Box before reaching this threshold returns an error regardless of whether the code itself is valid. This is the most common reason first-time players cannot redeem codes — complete a few passenger routes to accumulate the required EXP, then return to the Gift Box to use your codes.

??

Case-Sensitive Input Error

All Diesel n' Steel codes are fully case-sensitive — codes contain a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. A single incorrect character is enough to trigger an "Invalid Code" error even if every other character is correct. Copy and paste directly from BLOXBURST to eliminate this entirely. Also check for accidental spaces before or after the code text in the entry field, which causes errors even when the code itself is properly entered.

??

Already Redeemed

Each code in Diesel n' Steel is single-use per Roblox account. If the game returns a message that the code has already been claimed, the rewards were added to your account in a previous session. There is no way to redeem the same code twice on the same profile — verify that you are not re-entering a code that was already used during an earlier play session before reporting it as broken.

??

Source Conflict — Code Moved to Expired

Several codes that appear as active on other tracking websites are listed as expired here because they have been flagged as inactive on at least one additional verified source. BLOXBURST moves any code to the expired section the moment a conflict is detected — this is especially important for Diesel n' Steel, which has an unusually large number of older codes that some trackers still list as active despite being confirmed expired elsewhere.

??

Server Not Updated After Patch

Codes released alongside a new update can fail on servers still running the previous build. If a fresh code does not register on first attempt — especially one released within the last few hours — exit Diesel n' Steel completely and rejoin to land on an updated server. This is most common in the first 30–60 minutes after each update while servers gradually roll over to the new version.

??

Can't Find the Gift Box

The code redemption menu in Diesel n' Steel is accessed through the white Gift Box icon on the left side of the screen — not through the main menu or settings. If you do not see the Gift Box, check that you have reached 500 EXP, as the icon only becomes visible after crossing this threshold. On mobile, the icon may be partially obscured by the interface — scroll or zoom the left side of the screen to locate it.

? Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for Diesel n' Steel

Earn Cash faster, level up quicker, and dominate the Bulacan road
01
Reach 500 EXP as your very first goal

The code redemption system in Diesel n' Steel is locked until you accumulate 500 EXP from in-game activity. Prioritize reaching this threshold in your first session before anything else — completing even a handful of passenger routes is enough to unlock the Gift Box. Once unlocked, redeem all active codes immediately to claim your Cash and Booster rewards before starting your main driving grind.

02
Activate all Booster codes at the start of a long session

Cash and EXP Boosters from codes are time-limited multipliers — they begin counting down from the moment of activation. To get the maximum value out of each booster, redeem and activate them right before a long driving session rather than immediately after claiming them. A session where you complete multiple full passenger routes under an active booster yields significantly more total Cash and EXP than the same routes without one.

03
Choose busy servers for higher passenger volume

Passenger spawn rates in Diesel n' Steel are tied to server activity — busier servers with more active players tend to generate more waiting passengers at stops, which means more fares per route and faster Cash accumulation. When joining a new session, check the player count and prefer servers with higher activity, particularly during peak hours when the commute simulation is at its most competitive and lucrative.

04
Repair your Jeepney before each session, not during a route

Vehicle damage in Diesel n' Steel affects your Jeepney's speed and performance, which directly reduces fare earnings per route over time. Repair at the terminal at the start of each session rather than mid-route — stopping during a route to repair causes you to miss passenger queues and lose the time advantage over competing drivers. A fully repaired Jeepney at session start runs every route at peak efficiency from the first fare collected.

05
Learn the Bulacan route layout before driving competitively

Diesel n' Steel's Bulacan map has multiple distinct routes with different passenger densities and distance lengths. Shorter routes cycle faster but earn less per run, while longer routes earn more per trip but take longer to complete. Spending time in lower-traffic sessions to memorize the road layout, stop locations, and route lengths before driving on busy servers prevents wrong turns and missed stops that cost you both time and passenger fares.

06
Join the Discord to catch compensation codes immediately after bugs

Diesel n' Steel has a history of releasing higher-value Cash compensation codes after server outages, spawn bugs, or major game-breaking issues. These codes are typically announced in Discord first and sometimes expire within a short window as the developer moves to normalize the game economy. Being in the Discord with notifications enabled for the codes channel is the single most reliable way to catch these drops before they expire.
