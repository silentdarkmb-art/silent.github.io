🌀 About the Game

About Dying Days Zombies

Developed by Voxq Enterprises • Roblox Survival Game

In Roblox Dying Days Zombies, players face the challenge of surviving a world overrun by zombies. An ancient necromancer has been resurrected, leading an army of the undead to destroy your village. The goal of the game is to protect the last remaining village and defeat the necromancer within 31 days. Players must craft barricades, buy weapons, and fend off waves of zombies and bosses to stay alive.

To succeed, you’ll need to strategize and survive for as long as possible. The game requires you to gather resources, strengthen your defenses, and plan your attacks carefully. Each day, the necromancer’s power grows, and the zombies become more dangerous. Will you last long enough to defeat the necromancer, or will your village fall to the undead horde?
