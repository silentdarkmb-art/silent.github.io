🧙 About the Game

About Sorcerer Incremental

Developed by Blumea Productions • Roblox Jujutsu Kaisen Incremental RPG

Sorcerer Incremental is a Jujutsu Kaisen-inspired incremental RPG developed by Blumea Productions on Roblox. Players train to become the most powerful sorcerer by battling cursed spirits across increasingly difficult waves of enemies. The game features a deep gacha-style rolling system for Cursed Techniques, Weapons, Ally units, Clan abilities, and Energy types — each combination shaping a unique build suited to different playstyles. Advancing through the story requires defeating challenging bosses, and prestige mechanics allow players to restart their journey in exchange for greater long-term power. Scrolls and Rerolls from codes provide a significant head start toward finding the strongest possible technique combination from the first session.

What Do Codes Give You?

Technique & Weapon Rolls

Technique and Weapon Rolls are the most impactful code rewards in Sorcerer Incremental, directly increasing the number of attempts at obtaining rare Cursed Techniques and high-tier Weapons without spending in-game resources. A single code can award multiple rolls across both categories — giving new players an immediate chance at rare abilities that would otherwise require extensive grinding to attempt. Specific codes like COOPRAIDS award up to 4 Technique Rolls and a Weapon Roll in a single redemption.

📜

Scrolls & Clan Rewards

Scroll codes award Energy Scrolls and Clan Scrolls — two resources that expand your build options beyond the base Technique and Weapon rolls. Energy Scrolls determine your energy type's combat properties, while Clan Scrolls assign clan bonuses that passively enhance your sorcerer's stats and abilities. Clan and Energy assignments from early scroll redemptions can define your entire build direction, making these rewards among the most strategically significant items available through codes.

⚠️ Troubleshoot

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues and how to fix them fast
🔡

Case-Sensitive Input

All Sorcerer Incremental codes are fully case-sensitive. Entering any character incorrectly — including capitalisation — produces an immediate rejection. Copy and paste directly from BLOXBURST to eliminate input errors entirely. On mobile, autocorrect can silently alter the first character of a pasted code — verify the text field content after pasting before pressing Redeem.

⏰

Code Has Expired

Sorcerer Incremental codes can expire without advance notice. BLOXBURST cross-checks multiple sources before updating the active list — any code showing expired or conflicting status is moved immediately. If a code does not appear in the active list here, it has been flagged and will not work.

👥

Not in the Roblox Group

Sorcerer Incremental requires all players to be members of the Blumea Productions Roblox Group before any code can be redeemed. Attempting to redeem without group membership will result in an immediate failure regardless of whether the code is valid. Join the group first, then relaunch the game and attempt the redemption again — this resolves the vast majority of first-time redemption failures.

🔧

Old Server After Update

Codes released with a new Sorcerer Incremental update can fail on servers still running the previous build. If a newly announced code returns invalid on the first attempt — particularly within the first hour or two of a patch — exit the game entirely and rejoin to land on a fresh server running the current version. This resolves almost all build-related redemption failures immediately.

🚫

Extra Space in the Code Field

Copying a code can include a hidden leading or trailing space that triggers an invalid response even when all visible characters are correct. After pasting, click at the very start and end of the code field to check for extra spaces before pressing Redeem. On mobile, clipboard paste behaviour varies between apps and spaces are easily introduced without any visible indicator.

🔄

Already Redeemed

Each Sorcerer Incremental code is single-use per Roblox account. If the game returns an already-redeemed message, the Scrolls or Rolls were added to your account in a previous session. Players returning after a gap may have claimed codes they no longer remember redeeming — checking the active list against memory before attempting codes prevents repeated failed attempts.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for Sorcerer Incremental

Roll better techniques, defeat harder bosses, and progress faster from day one
01
Join the Blumea Productions Roblox Group before anything else — it's required for codes

Before launching the game for the first time, join the Blumea Productions Roblox Group. Group membership is a hard requirement for code redemption in Sorcerer Incremental — no code will work without it. Joining takes seconds and unlocks access to all 27 active codes immediately, providing a substantial number of free rolls and scrolls before your first battle.

02
Redeem all active codes before your first roll — the milestone codes stack with specific roll rewards

After joining the group, redeem all active codes before spending any in-game resources on rolls. Specific codes like COOPRAIDS, SORRYFORWAIT, and the like milestone codes award Technique Rolls, Weapon Rolls, Clan Scrolls, and Energy Scrolls that stack directly — giving you a significantly broader starting roll pool than beginning with only base resources.

03
Lock your best rolls before rerolling — don't accidentally overwrite a rare technique

Sorcerer Incremental allows you to lock specific rolls before attempting new ones. Always lock your best current Cursed Technique and Weapon before using a reroll to avoid overwriting a rare result with a common one. Many players lose strong early rolls by forgetting to lock before spending a Technique Reroll — the lock step should be the first action before every reroll attempt.

04
Focus on Clan and Energy Scrolls early — they define your build direction before Technique investment

Clan bonuses and Energy type assignments provide passive stat improvements that affect every fight regardless of your technique. Establishing a strong Clan and Energy combination early means every subsequent Technique Roll and boss fight benefits from the passive foundation. Waiting until late progression to address Clan and Energy assignments means many early sessions are played at sub-optimal efficiency.

05
Prestige as soon as the power multiplier outweighs the reset cost — don't delay it

Prestige in Sorcerer Incremental resets your current progress but applies a permanent power multiplier to all future scaling. The break-even point — where the multiplier benefit exceeds the cost of rebuilding — arrives earlier than most new players expect. Delaying prestige past this point means playing at diminishing returns while a reset would immediately accelerate progression through content you can now clear much faster.

06
Check the Discord after every like milestone — codes follow a consistent pattern

Blumea Productions releases codes consistently at like milestones — the 900, 1300, 2000, 2500, 3000, 4000, 5500, 7000, and 9000 like codes are direct evidence of this pattern. As the game continues to grow, new milestone codes will follow the same trigger. Watching the like count on the Roblox game page and checking the Discord announcements channel when it approaches a round number is the most reliable way to catch new codes immediately.
