🔧 About the Game

About Fix It Up

Developed by workspace • Roblox Car Restoration Simulator Game

Fix It Up is a car restoration simulator developed by workspace on Roblox. Players purchase wrecked vehicles from the junkyard, bring them into the workshop, and meticulously repair each part — from the engine internals to the bodywork and wheels — before finishing with a fresh coat of paint. Once a vehicle hits 100% condition, it can be sold for maximum profit. Euro is the primary currency used to buy cars, parts, and upgrades, and codes provide a significant cash injection of free Euro — letting you take on better projects and upgrade your garage without grinding from scratch.

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues and how to fix them fast
🔡

Typos & Case-Sensitive Input

Fix It Up codes are case-sensitive — even a single wrong character will invalidate the code. Copy and paste directly from BLOXBURST to avoid input errors entirely. On mobile, autocorrect may silently alter a character after pasting, so always verify the text field before hitting Redeem.

⏰

Code Has Expired

Fix It Up codes can expire without any advance notice — especially high-value ones, which tend to go inactive faster than others. BLOXBURST monitors multiple sources and removes any code the moment it shows expired or conflicting status. If a code is no longer on the active list here, it has already been flagged and will not work.

🔄

Already Redeemed

Each Fix It Up code is single-use per Roblox account. If the game returns an already-redeemed message, the Euro was added to your account in a previous session. Check the active list before attempting codes to avoid repeated failed attempts.

🔧

Old Server After Update

Codes released alongside a new update can fail on servers still running the previous build. If a freshly announced code returns invalid — especially within the first hour or two of a patch — exit the game entirely and rejoin to land on a fresh server running the current version.

🛍️

Wrong Menu Location

Fix It Up codes can only be redeemed through the Hash (#) icon on the right side of the screen — this opens the Codes menu where you can enter your code and press Redeem. The Codes button may also appear labeled directly on the right side of your screen depending on the current game version.

📅

When New Codes Release

The workspace group typically drops new Fix It Up codes alongside major game updates, milestones, and special events. New codes tend to be announced in the Discord server first. Join the Fix It Up Discord and keep an eye on the announcements and general channels to catch codes the moment they drop.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for FIX IT UP!

Buy smart, repair thoroughly, and flip cars for maximum profit from your very first day in the garage
01
Redeem all active codes immediately — press the Codes button on the right side of the screen, enter each code, and hit Redeem to collect free Euros before buying your first car

Before spending a single Euro in the Junkyard, open the Codes button on the right side of the screen and redeem every active code. Fix It Up codes reward Euros — the game's currency — and active codes can give you tens of thousands of Euro combined, which is often enough to buy and fully repair multiple starter cars without grinding from zero. The developer .workspace releases codes at milestones and updates, and they expire without warning, so claiming them the moment you load in guarantees you do not miss free starting capital.

02
Complete the in-game tutorial fully before heading to the Junkyard — it walks you through every repair machine and tool so you do not waste money on cars you cannot fix yet

Fix It Up spawns you inside your garage with an in-depth tutorial that covers the wrench, engine disassembly, part cleaning machines, and the paint shop. The game's repair mechanics are technical and can be confusing without this foundation, especially if you are unfamiliar with automotive terminology. Finishing the tutorial before buying your first Junkyard car means you understand exactly what each broken part requires before you have spent Euros committing to a project you are not prepared to complete.

03
Always replace Spark Plugs and Injectors with new ones from the shop — reusing old ones reduces engine performance and directly lowers your resale value

When disassembling and repairing an engine in Fix It Up, every part must be cleaned and repaired using the appropriate machine — except Spark Plugs and Injectors. These two components must always be discarded and replaced with brand-new ones purchased from the shop. Installing old or worn Spark Plugs and Injectors causes the vehicle to underperform, which reduces how much buyers will pay for it. This is one of the most common mistakes beginners make that silently cuts into their profit margin on every flip.

04
Use metallic paint colors when finishing a car — metallic finishes consistently fetch higher resale prices than standard colors and cost the same to apply

The Paint Shop in Fix It Up is where you apply a fresh coat that also removes all remaining rust from the vehicle. The color you choose has a direct impact on how much buyers will offer. Metallic colors reliably command higher prices than flat or standard finishes, and since painting costs the same regardless of color choice, selecting a metallic finish every time is a free profit boost on every car you flip. Make it a habit before every sale rather than choosing colors based on preference alone.

05
Try the Auction for high-risk high-reward car deals — you can land rare vehicles at low prices, but always inspect carefully before bidding

The Auction system in Fix It Up features used cars that can turn out to be rare high-value vehicles or basic Junkyard trash depending on your luck and judgment. Winning a rare car at auction and restoring it can generate significantly more profit than flipping standard Junkyard purchases. However, bidding without assessing the vehicle's condition and potential sell price first is how beginners drain their Euro reserves on unprofitable projects. Build some experience flipping Junkyard cars first before regularly participating in auctions.

06
Join the .workspace Discord server to catch new codes and updates — codes are released at milestones with no fixed schedule and expire without any advance notice

The .workspace team releases new Fix It Up codes when the game hits Like and visit milestones, as well as alongside major updates. No expiry dates are ever announced in advance, meaning a code that is active today may be gone tomorrow without warning. Joining the official Discord server is the fastest way to see new codes the moment they go live. The Discord is also where the community shares repair tips, rare car spotting strategies, and news about upcoming content — valuable resources for anyone serious about maximizing their garage profits.
