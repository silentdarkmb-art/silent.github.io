How to Redeem Codes

  1. Launch Sailor Piece on Roblox.
  2. Click the Settings button on the top-left side of the screen, below your Health and XP bars.
  3. Scroll all the way down to the bottom of the Settings window to find the Codes section.
  4. Type or paste your code into the A Secret Code… text box — codes are case-sensitive.
  5. Press the blue Redeem button to instantly claim your free rewards.
⚠️ Note: Most codes require a minimum level to redeem — make sure you meet the level requirement before attempting!
✨ About the Game

About Sailor Piece

Developed by Shadowrise Devs • One Piece-inspired Roblox RPG

Sailor Piece is an anime-inspired Roblox RPG developed by Shadowrise Devs, released on November 17, 2025, and heavily inspired by the One Piece anime series. The game has accumulated over 364 million visits and features a current max level cap of 13,000, with players progressing through fast-paced bandit-beating combat across multiple islands and seas. The core gameplay loop revolves around defeating enemies and bosses to level up, hunting for rare Devil Fruits that grant unique combat abilities, collecting different Races with distinct passive bonuses, and upgrading equipment through the Enchant system. As of the Moon Update (March 31, 2026), the game introduced a new melee spec called Moon Slayer, a new boss Kokushibo on Boss Island, a new Power system, 2 new Ascensions, and confirmed Sea 2 as the next major content expansion. Codes in Sailor Piece consistently drop alongside CCU milestones, updates, and server restarts — making the Discord the essential first source for new codes.

⚔️ Gameplay

Players begin on the Starter Island with limited strength and work their way through thousands of levels by defeating bandits and progressively tougher bosses across multiple islands. The progression is driven by four core systems: Devil Fruits (50+ options from Natural, Elemental, and Beast types), Swords (dozens from basic Katana to mythical Yama), Races (21 options including SwordBlessed, Galevorn, Kitsune, Fishman, and Mink), and Haki (Busoshoku and Kenbunshoku). Dungeons, Runes, Clans, Traits, and Artifacts add additional depth for mid and late-game optimization. Devil Fruits spawn under trees every 60 minutes and despawn after 20 minutes, making active play essential for securing the rarest drops.

🎁 Code Drops

Shadowrise Devs releases Sailor Piece codes tied to CCU milestones (concurrent user peaks), major updates, server restarts, and special events like Eid Mubarak. Codes expire faster than most Roblox games — older codes can stop working the moment a new update goes live. All codes carry a level requirement before they can be redeemed, meaning new players must progress before claiming rewards. Codes are announced first through the official Sailor Piece Discord server and the Shadowrise Devs Roblox group, making both channels essential monitoring sources for catching new drops before they expire.

🎁 Rewards

What Do Codes Give You?

Free Beli, Gems, Clan Rerolls, Race Rerolls, Trait Rerolls, and Secret Chests
🎲

Rerolls (Clan, Race, Trait, Haki Color)

Rerolls are the most valuable rewards from Sailor Piece codes — each type targets a different progression system. Clan Rerolls change your passive combat bonuses tied to your bloodline. Race Rerolls give you a chance at rare options like SwordBlessed, Galevorn, or Cursed Vessel, which provide unique stat boosts unavailable any other way. Trait Rerolls change your active Trait for HP, damage, and stat bonuses. Haki Color Rerolls customize the color of your Haki cosmetically. Past codes have awarded 95–100 Clan Rerolls per redemption, making code drops the most efficient free source of rolls in the game.

Progression System
💰

Beli (Cash)

Beli is the primary in-game currency in Sailor Piece, used to purchase weapons, gear, and upgrades across the game's various islands. The launch code RELEASE awarded 25,000 Beli directly upon redemption — one of the largest single currency drops in the game's code history. Beli-awarding codes provide new players with an immediate head start on purchasing essential early-game equipment like Busoshoku Haki (available for 25,000 Beli on Starter Island) without requiring hours of bandit farming to accumulate the necessary funds.

Primary Currency
💎

Gems

Gems are the premium progression currency in Sailor Piece, used to reroll for Gem Fruits and unlock access to additional features and upgrades unavailable through standard Beli spending. The launch code RELEASE awarded 300 Gems alongside 25,000 Beli — establishing Gems as among the most sought-after code rewards in the game. Gem-awarding codes provide direct access to features that would otherwise require significant grind investment, making each Gem drop through codes a meaningful shortcut on the path to stronger builds and rare fruit variants.

Why Some Codes Don't Work

Common issues & how to fix them fast
⏰

Code Expired With the Latest Update

Sailor Piece codes expire faster than almost any other Roblox game — older codes can be deactivated the moment a new update goes live. With frequent CCU milestone codes, update codes, and apology codes all rotating rapidly, the gap between a code being announced and becoming permanently invalid can be as short as the next patch launch. BLOXBURST moves any code confirmed expired on any verified source to the expired section immediately, and treats all "Check" status codes as expired per our conflict resolution policy.

🎓

Level Requirement Not Met

Every Sailor Piece code requires a minimum player level before it can be redeemed — attempting to use a code below the required threshold returns an error that is easily mistaken for an expired or invalid code. Early codes required as little as Level 250, while more recent milestone codes require significantly higher levels. If a code fails despite being confirmed active, check whether you have met its level requirement before reporting it as broken. Focusing on Starter Island quests and the Academy Teacher early accelerates level gain enough to unlock most current codes within a few sessions.

🔡

Spelling Error — Codes Are Case-Sensitive

Sailor Piece codes are case-sensitive — entering a code with incorrect capitalization returns an invalid error even when every other character is correct. This is especially risky with the game's longer milestone codes like 305KCCUTYSMGUYS or SORRYFORRESTARTONLYONETONIGHTT, where a single miscapitalized character anywhere in the string causes the entire code to fail. Always paste directly from BLOXBURST using the Copy button on each code card rather than typing manually to eliminate both capitalization and spacing errors simultaneously.

🔁

Already Redeemed

Each Sailor Piece code is single-use per Roblox account. If the game indicates the code has already been claimed, the reward was already added to your inventory in a previous session. With Sailor Piece's large and frequently rotating code pool, it is easy to lose track of which codes have been redeemed across multiple play sessions. Checking your current Gem balance, Clan Reroll count, and inventory against your expected totals is the fastest way to confirm whether an "already redeemed" error is accurate before reporting the code as broken.

🔧

Server Running Old Build

Codes released alongside a Sailor Piece update can fail on servers still running the previous build. If a freshly released code does not register on first attempt — particularly one that dropped within the last few hours of a new update or Moon Update patch — exit Sailor Piece completely and rejoin to land on a current-build server. Switching servers rather than retrying on the same instance resolves this issue in most cases within the first few hours after each new update goes live.

⭐ Pro Tips

Beginner Tips for Sailor Piece

Level faster, find better fruits, and build a stronger character from day one
01
Join the Shadowrise Devs group immediately — it unlocks free rewards on Tutorial Island

Joining the Shadowrise Devs Roblox group before starting your first session unlocks exclusive bonus rewards on the Tutorial Island that group members receive and non-members miss entirely. Combined with redeeming all active codes (once you meet the level requirements), completing both steps in your first minute of play gives you a meaningful head start on Beli, Gems, and Rerolls before your first bandit fight. The group is also the primary secondary channel for code drops, so membership doubles as a code discovery tool for every subsequent session.

02
Start with Flame or Ice Fruit — both have Logia immunity for efficient early farming

As a new player, your first Devil Fruit choice significantly impacts early-game farming speed. Flame and Ice Fruits are both Rare tier and provide Logia immunity — a mechanic that makes elemental hits from bandits pass through you harmlessly until enemies have Haki. This immunity dramatically reduces the damage you take while farming early islands, letting you kill bandits faster without needing frequent healing. Upgrade to Light or Quake Fruit once you can spin Legendary tier, but Flame and Ice will carry you through the majority of the early game efficiently.

03
Buy Busoshoku Haki on Starter Island as your first major purchase

Busoshoku Haki (Buso Haki) is available for 25,000 Beli on the Starter Island and is the single most important early-game purchase in Sailor Piece. Haki allows your attacks to bypass the Logia immunity of enemy Devil Fruit users — without it, your hits pass through elemental-type enemies harmlessly, making entire island zones nearly impossible to farm. Prioritize reaching 25,000 Beli through early bandit farming and code redemption before spending on any other upgrade. Beli-awarding codes like RELEASE (25,000 Beli + 300 Gems) covered this cost entirely at launch.

04
Check under trees every 60 minutes for Devil Fruit spawns — they despawn after 20 minutes

Devil Fruits spawn under trees across the game's islands on a 60-minute cycle and despawn permanently after 20 minutes if unclaimed. Setting a reminder to check common fruit spawn locations every hour — particularly on islands where your target fruit tier spawns most frequently — is the most reliable free method of obtaining rare fruits without spending Gems on the Gacha NPC. The rarest fruits like Dragon, Fiend, and Kitsune (S+ tier as of March 2026) have extremely low spawn rates, so consistent monitoring over multiple sessions is required to land them through tree spawns.

05
Save Race Rerolls from codes until you understand which races are worth targeting

Race Rerolls from codes are among the most valuable resources in Sailor Piece — but spending them randomly before understanding the race tier list wastes rolls that are difficult to replace without additional codes. As of 2026, SwordBlessed and Galevorn are the best Mythic races, while Kitsune and Mink are strong Legendary options. Human is a solid free starting choice for early leveling. Stockpile your code-awarded Race Rerolls until you have identified your target race tier, then roll them in a single session to maximize your chances of landing the desired outcome.

06
Enable Discord notifications for the Sailor Piece server — codes drop faster than any other game

Sailor Piece codes expire faster than almost any other Roblox game — a new update can invalidate the previous batch of active codes within hours of going live. The official Sailor Piece Discord at discord.gg/sailorpiece is where every new code drops first, consistently hours before external tracking pages are updated. Enabling notifications on the #announcements and #update-logs channels is the only reliable way to catch CCU milestone codes — which appear without warning and can expire before most tracking sites even know they exist. Same-day redemption on every code is the only safe strategy in Sailor Piece.
