01

We search for potential codes from official sources — dev posts, patch notes, game updates, and social media accounts.

What We Don't Do

⚠️
Why Some Codes Still Fail

  • ⚡Codes can expire suddenly — developers can disable them anytime
  • 🌍Some codes have region or server restrictions
  • ⏰There may be a delay in updates after a patch
  • 🔁Try refreshing your server and attempt redemption again

🛡️ Why Code Verification Matters

Unverified code lists waste your time and damage trust. Sites that copy-paste codes without testing leave expired and fake codes mixed in with working ones — meaning players burn attempts on codes that never had a chance of working. Every code on BLOXBURST is manually tested in-game before publishing, and removed within 24 hours of expiry so you never waste a redemption attempt.
